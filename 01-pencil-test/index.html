Bolas.

The three experiments of From Imagetext to Worldtext, acted out by ball-and-cord figures. The dissertation text runs on the right; the figures act it out on the left, beat by beat.

This is an HTML page
The figures are computed in your browser from keyed poses. Pause, scrub, step single frames, switch overlays, click a line of text to jump to its beat.
This is a video
Press play and it runs straight through: three chapters, 877 frames at 24 fps, 36.5 seconds. The same frames are also rendered as a video file, bolas-videotext.mp4 (1920 × 1080).
