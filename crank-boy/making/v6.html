Research Context: Completing the 1910 Tetrad Through Play

In Soyoun Jang's Archive of Past Futures, Villemard's 1910 chromolithograph À l'École is coded under two of Marshall McLuhan's four laws of media:

  • Enhances: The rapid transfer of a book's contents into pupils' heads through overhead electrical wires.
  • Obsolesces: The slow, self-paced work of reading a printed codex.
  • Scope (Transforms practice): Everything else in the room remains a Third Republic schoolroom: wooden benches, folded arms, a stern schoolmaster (M. Lavisse), and physical hardcover books dropped whole into a hand-cranked hopper.

Because a postcard shows a single frozen instant rather than a morning lived inside the room, it leaves the other two quadrants of McLuhan's Tetrad—Retrieval and Reversal—unspoken. Version 6 was designed in collaboration with Jay David Bolter and Collin Freeman so that a player discovers the missing half of the Tetrad through play:

  • Reversal (Overdrive Cramming): If the player optimizes purely for speed (turning the crank at 120 RPM Overdrive with orthodox textbooks), the class passes Stage I of the Inspector's syllabus check in two turns—but the high voltage locks the front row's brass helmets into Galvanic Dogma and fuses incompatible claims into absurd technocratic slogans during Stage II.
  • Retrieval (The Whispered Counter-Book): When the player feeds two conflicting books on the same topic (an Orthodox Canon volume and a Witness or Poetic Counter-Text) and pauses the crank, the silence lets pupils compare what they heard across benches and write a chapter of Le Contre-Livre.

  2. Theory Phase (10:55–11:02 EDT).

    Before any HTML or JavaScript was written, the studio produced the 13-section Theory Report:

    • Visual Anomaly Identified: Henri, the boy in the orange jacket turning the crank, is the only student in the room without a brass headset. The electrical future runs on his manual cadence.
    • Spatial Topology (The 3-Row Voltage Gradient): Row 1 (Émile and Gustave, front bench) receives full voltage (`70V` steady, `120V` overdrive), boosting syllabus speed but risking dogma lock. Row 3 (Claire and René, by the left window) receives attenuated current (`25V` steady, `10V` slow), allowing dogma to fade while Claire records observations from outside the window.
    • The Optimization Trap & Two-Stage Inspection: To prevent high-speed cramming from trivially winning the game, the Inspector General's examination was split into Stage I (Syllabus Coverage) and Stage II (Unscripted Counterfactual Probe).

  6. Restyling and Simplifying for Immediate Play (11:30–11:40 EDT).

    To make Version 6 effortless to play while preserving every critic-verified rule:

    • Guided Next Step Banner: A dynamic banner at the top of the paper sheet tells the player what is waiting in the hopper, when a contradiction has landed on the benches, and provides a one-click primary action button (Turn Crank, Stop & Whisper, or Call Inspector).
    • One-Click Topic Pair Buttons: Instead of searching through six separate books to find matching topics, players can click Pair I: History (1515), Pair II: Human Motor, or Pair III: The Book to load both the Orthodox Canon line and its matching Counter-Text line straight to the front of the hopper.
    • Off-Register Multiplane Print Stage: The classroom was redrawn using the multiplane SVG print system from Version 5 (1.25px off-register colour fills, #rough turbulent ink displacement, diagonal ink hatching, paper grain, and IM Fell English typography).

Independent Play Critic Audit: Round 1 (FAIL) to Round 2 (PASS)

Below is the empirical record from the independent playtest subagent across both rounds.

Discriminating TestRound 1 ObservationRecursive RepairRound 2 Result
1. Topic-Pair Contradiction Delivery FAIL Queuing two 3-line books (`[A1, A2, A3, B1, B2, B3]`) into a 3-slot FIFO memory evicted `A1` before `B1` arrived (`0` chapters produced). Added automatic Topic-Interleaving Hopper Splice (`[A1, B1, A2, B2, A3, B3]`), one-click Topic Pair buttons, and 4-slot pair-preserving memory (`insertTokenSmart`). PASS Every paired book feed reliably creates a contradiction across the benches within two turns.
2. Turn-0 Action Farming FAIL An adversarial player could click Whisper or Window 20 times in Cycle 1 without advancing the clock, reaching `100%` Retrieval at Cycle 1. Added a 1-per-cycle tactical action budget (`tacticalActionsRemaining = 1`) that resets only when the crank advances a cycle, plus a 3-cycle cooldown on Distract Master. PASS Spamming 20 times in Cycle 1 executes once and blocks the remaining 19 clicks until the clock steps.
3. 3-Row Voltage Differentiation FAIL At Steady 60 RPM, Row 2 (`50V`) and Row 3 (`25V`) received identical tokens and produced identical memory arrays. At `<= 25V` (Slow & Steady), official dogma attenuates along the ceiling wire before reaching Row 3: Claire records window observations instead of dogma, and René reserves his notebook for witness evidence. PASS Row 1 (`70V`), Row 2 (`50V`), and Row 3 (`25V`) diverge into three distinct cognitive profiles.
4. Earpiece & Surveillance Enforcement FAIL Unclamping all 6 helmets still awarded `+100%` Syllabus while the room was unwired; 100% Suspicion never interrupted play. Scaled Syllabus gain by `wiredPupils / 6` (`0` wired = `+0%` Syllabus); added active Lavisse Intervention at `80%` Suspicion and a thermal cutout at `90%` Dynamo Heat. PASS Unclamping all 6 helmets yields `+0%` Syllabus and triggers Lavisse's clamp-down when suspicion reaches `80%`.

Soyoun Jang's coding of the postcard in the Archive of Past Futures (Enhances transfer of books' contents; Obsolesces reading; Scope = transforms practice), extended with Jay David Bolter and Collin Freeman to complete McLuhan's Tetrad (Retrieval of the whispered study circle; Reversal into locked dogma under high-speed transmission).
