The kit

Every element we have made for the Crank Boy and for Futuretown, in one place: the pieces cut from each card, the archive plates, each card's printing inks, the doodles, the cast of version 9, the operator prompts and the tools that make and publish them. Everything here is a plain file you can open or download.

Cards and cut pieces

Each card is the cast: pieces are cut from its own pixels (shorts/tools/cut.py) and the holes are filled from their surroundings. A piece marked slot is an actor another actor can be recast into. The eight inks are the card's own colours by k-means, darkest first; click one for its value.

Version 9: plates and cast

Five image-model plates of the school card line up with it to the pixel, each with less in it, so a layer is the difference between two plates. The cast was extracted from generated sprite sheets by connected alpha components. Both are Watson's; the model and prompts are not recorded.

Archive plates

Cut-free stretches of single shots from the cineosis-lab library, trimmed and re-encoded without sound. Each plate names its source clip, so it can be found again.
