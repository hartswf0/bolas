    SCION Case 01 · Eight Research & Design Artifacts

    This study applies Soyoun Jang's Historicising Speculative Design framework (and Jang & Bolter's 2026 McLuhan Tetrad method) to Record #19 of the Archive of Past Futures: À l'École (1899/1910).

    Artifact 01 · The Historical Artifact

    Provenance & Visual Forensics

    Deposited at the BnF in 1910 by Parisian chromolithographer Villemard (Cote QB-1 (1900)-FOL) with [Jean-Marc Côté] credited as presumed 1899 draughtsman. The scan with the English caption "At School" derives from Isaac Asimov's Futuredays (1986). Forensics of 13 regions reveal HISTOIRE DE FRANCE on the book spine, a 7th un-headsetted child turning the crank, and a 2nd-row pupil staring out the open window.

    Artifact 02 · What the Future Claimed

    Proposition Codebook & Competing Readings

    Separates replacement of the codex from mechanization of student reading labor. Tests three competing interpretations: H1 (Hyper-validation of the printed book as canonical fuel), H2 (Present-tense satire of post-Ferry Third Republic bourrage de crâne during the 1908–1910 guerre des manuels), and H3 (Acoustic remediation of Clément Ader's 1890 Parisian Théâtrophone).

    Artifact 03 · What the Future Preserved

    The Unchanged Ground

    When the Hopper-Grinder is conceptually removed, the entire 1910 Third Republic disciplinary apparatus survives intact: the instituteur as sole epistemic gatekeeper, state textbook monopoly, fixed wooden benches, compulsory age-cohort synchronization, and the class division between intellectual reception and manual crank labor.

    Artifact 04 · The Media Tetrad

    McLuhan's Four Laws & Figure–Ground Reversal

    Enhance: Point-to-multipoint pace and acoustic standardization. Obsolesce: Active student decoding and page-flipping verification. Retrieve: Pre-Gutenberg monastic lectio and medieval rote dictation. Reverse: Epistemic helplessness (bourrage de crâne) when effortless transmission eliminates comparative verification.

    T0 (1910) → T1 (2000) → T2 (1910–2026)

    Artifact 06 · Three Alternative Futures

    Structural Counterfutures A, B, and C

    Counterfuture A (The Book Survives): The unshredded printed codex becomes the random-access verification ledger against live broadcast drift. Counterfuture B (The School Disappears): The wire leaves the classroom via municipal Théâtrophone drops, dissolving compulsory benches.
