## Remix prompt

```
[skip theory] Build a new version of The Crank Boy as one self-contained HTML file.

Keep from version 1: the classroom drawn in the style of Villemard's "At School" (flat print colours slightly off-register from ink outlines, diagonal hatching, paper grain, IM Fell English type); one lesson ending at a bell; the teacher grading against his own copy; a ledger that separates the documented postcard from everything invented; the room on the top half of a phone screen and the controls on the bottom half with no scrolling.

Change exactly one relationship: [WRITE THE CHANGE HERE, e.g. "the least powerful person in the room now controls the rules"].

Before building, state in a few lines what the player now does, what new tension that creates, and what the player can discover without being told. Then build the smallest playable lesson that tests it.
```

## Version 2: The Teacher Cranks

```
[skip theory] Build version 2 of The Crank Boy, "The Teacher Cranks", as one self-contained HTML file in the same style as version 1 (Villemard's "At School": off-register print colours, ink outlines, hatching, paper grain, IM Fell English; room on top, controls below, no scrolling on a phone).

Reverse authority. The player now chooses which book goes into the hopper and when. The teacher turns the crank. He cranks steadily for books he approves of and slows, speeds up, or stops for books he does not. The class still receives only what the machine sends, and he still grades against his own copy at the bell.

Keep the river contradiction, the one-line bus, the rule that sent words cannot be un-taught, and the ledger. Before building, state what the player does, what the new tension is, and what can be discovered without being told.
```

## Version 3: Two Seats

```
[skip theory] Build version 3 of The Crank Boy, "Two Seats", as one self-contained HTML file in the same style as version 1 (Villemard's "At School": off-register print colours, ink outlines, hatching, paper grain, IM Fell English; room on top, controls below, no scrolling on a phone).

Two players on two phones, joined by a code or QR. Player one is the crank boy: cranks, can stop, open the hopper, read and swap. Player two wears a headset: sees only the words that reach their wire, garbled or clear, and cannot see the hopper. The two can exchange short messages only while the machine is silent, and silence raises the teacher's suspicion. At the bell, player two answers the teacher's question.

Keep the river contradiction, the teacher's grading against his own copy, and the ledger. Before building, state what each player does, what the new tension is, and what can be discovered without being told.
```

## Version 4: The Pump

```
[skip theory] Build version 4 of The Crank Boy, "The Pump", as one self-contained HTML file in the same visual style as version 1 (Villemard's "At School": off-register print colours, ink outlines, hatching, paper grain, IM Fell English; scene on top, controls below, no scrolling on a phone).

Move the method to the climate-futures case. A boy works a hand pump that feeds water to six houses on one shared pipe during a dry season. An engineer decides which houses stay connected and keeps a ledger of what each house is owed. The player pumps; pumping steadily delivers water, too slow lets the far houses run dry, too fast bursts joints. When the player stops, the households come to their doors. While stopped, the player can read the engineer's ledger, open or close a valve, or tell the households what the ledger says.

Keep a bounded season ending at a bell or a rainfall, an authority whose record can disagree with what the households observe, and a ledger separating documented history from invention. Before building, state what the player does, what the tension is, and what can be discovered without being told.
```
