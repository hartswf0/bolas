The shorts

Motor skates

Operator: Lotte Reiniger, articulated shadow theatre. A light comes up behind the card and the skaters turn to silhouettes, cut from the card's own drawing, on hinged legs. The couple skates, the lady glides off, and the man who fell tries to get up and falls again. A crowd filmed in 1927 watches from the horizon. Then the screen behind them becomes a real shadow play, and the skaters cross in front of its performers.

This page draws the short live without sound. The film with its soundtrack is in the shorts.

Ledger

Observed
The card, En l'an 2000, about 1910. Two plates from the cineosis-lab shot library: the crowd on the promenade in Autoraces op boulevard Scheveningen (1927), keyed to its dark shapes; and performers behind a lit screen from Industry on Parade, slowed to half speed.
Cut and moved
Ten pieces: the couple's bodies and each one's legs, the lady, the fallen man in two pieces and his hat, two lamps and the tree line. Each piece's edge is the card's own drawing, separated from the ground by colour.
Drawn and synthesised
The lit backdrop; the shadow figures, which are the cut pieces darkened, not new drawings; the hinges, the camera, grain and captions; the skate wheels, the fall and the waltz.
