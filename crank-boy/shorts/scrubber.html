The shorts

Un frotteur électrique

Operator: inverse kinematics, contact and reach. The brushes are told where to be on the floor, and the machine's legs are solved backwards from them: hip, knee, brush. The brushes never leave the floor. Where they have scrubbed, the parquet turns to a mirror, and what it reflects is a stage filmed in 1957.
