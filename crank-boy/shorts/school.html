The shorts

À l'école

Operator: Terry Gilliam, comic cutout metamorphosis. Start from what the card already implies and move the smallest piece that breaks it. The boy turns the crank, the lesson runs down the wires, and one pupil's head comes off and is replaced by the book. The book opens on a classroom that was really filmed. When the boy stops, everything stops.

Ledger

Observed
The card À l'école, En l'an 2000, attributed to Villemard, about 1910. The plate: a lecturer at a blackboard from Letter From an Airline Pilot (Part I), 3.5 s, from the cineosis-lab shot library, toned and unaltered otherwise.
Cut and moved
Three pieces of the card: the boy's upper body, the pupil's head and the teacher's book. The holes they leave are filled with the card's own colours, pulled in from the edges.
Drawn and synthesised
The signal in the wires, the open book, the camera, the grain, the captions and every sound (ratchet, hum, a voice without words, the knocks).
