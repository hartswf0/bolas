The barber

Operator: forward kinematics, hierarchical body logic. The man at the panel pulls one lever. Its angle is the only input: each arm's shoulder turns by a fixed share of it, and each wrist turns on top of its shoulder, parent to child. Meanwhile the mirror on the wall shows the job done by hand, by a barber filmed in 1959.
