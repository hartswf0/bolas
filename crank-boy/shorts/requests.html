The Town

Futuretown: the art department

The Town works, but every district looks the same: the card, cut and moved. These call sheets give each district its own aesthetic while it stays one town. Each sheet names the swarm that leads it and what the other swarms would do differently. It lists the archive plates to pull by clip id, the images to generate with their prompts and acceptance tests, and what to ask for. One test holds the town together: anything on screen must survive being re-printed in its card's own eight inks.

The test, already run

Each pair below is a real archive frame as found and then re-printed in a card's inks by shorts/tools/stencil.py: eight inks from the card by k-means, the print's dot screen, and a key plate one pixel off register. The 1906 ruin, the 1915 colonnade and the 1966 cleaning cart all read as part of their cards. This is the instrument for every new plate, background and generated image.
