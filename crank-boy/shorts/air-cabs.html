The shorts

Air cabs over Paris

Operator: multiplane parallax, spatial collage. The card is taken apart into planes at different distances: sky, far flyers, the big cab, the city and the nearest cab. As the camera drifts, each plane slides at its own rate, so the flat print gets depth. Through a gap in the far sky, a real plane comes on: a crop duster filmed in 1954, flying straight at the camera.

Ledger

Observed
The card, En l'an 2000, about 1910. The plate: a biplane flying low at the camera, from Airport America (1954), 5 s, from the cineosis-lab shot library, toned and seen through a soft-edged gap in the card's sky.
Cut and moved
Nine pieces: the city, the nearest cab, the big cab and its two wings, the yellow plane, the airship and two small flyers. The sky behind them is filled with its own colours.
Drawn and synthesised
The propeller's blur, each plane's depth and drift, the camera, grain and captions; the wind, wings and engines.
