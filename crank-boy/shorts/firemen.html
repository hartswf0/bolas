The shorts

Flying firemen

Operator: secondary dynamics, inertia and restraint. Nothing here moves on its own beat; everything follows something. Each drop of water keeps the direction the nozzle had when it left, so the jet whips as the nozzle swings. The flyers ride springs that lag the wind, and the embers drift on the heat. The fire and smoke are real, from the archive. At the end the jet runs into a real hose crew, filmed in 1947.

The card, En l'an 2000, about 1910. Three plates from the cineosis-lab shot library: flames from Watershed wildfire (1958) and a smoke blast from The Balloonatic (1923), each laid over the card's own fire and smoke so that only their light adds; and a line of men with hoses from Fire and Police Service (1947).

The two flyers on the left, as one piece, and the flyer above the fire. Their places are filled with the card's colours.
Drawn and synthesised
The water, each drop thrown from the nozzle; the embers; the springs the flyers hang on; the camera, grain and captions; the roar, the hiss, the wings and the pump.
