CRASH LAB / The Crank Boy

The Crank Boy

Seven cards, each worked three ways: a short film, a game, and a place in one world. Every film and game uses a different operator from the SCION animation swarms; every one lets in real archive film the way CINEOSIS does, from the cineosis-lab library; and the world over them is drawn in Secondhand Motion's doodles.

The Town

The seven cards as one world, joined by a drawn power line from the crank boy's machine. Nothing runs unless somebody turns the crank, and power reaches each card later the farther it is from him. Each card is a game, and the three handle-holders can be recast into each other's places: the same performance, a different hand on it.

Toward CINEOSIS

the archive comes into the card

CINEOSIS sets the crash-test family inside unaltered archive shots. These shorts take shots from the same cineosis-lab library the other way round, into a surface the card already has: a mirror, a screen, a polished floor, a sky, a fire. Each short is proposed as a candidate passage for one of the 45 signs. That is a reading, not a result: none has yet had the blind review the CINEOSIS handmade passages had.

Seven cards

a film, a game and a place in the Town for each

À l'écoleThe boy turns the crank and the lesson runs down the wires. One pupil's head comes off on its coil and the teacher's book takes its place. The book opens on a classroom that was really filmed. When the boy stops, everything stops. Film: Terry Gilliam, comic cutout metamorphosis · Game: Video Puppetry: perform the crank, do not key it · Proposed sign: 18 Mise en abyme Play the game · How it was made
Un frotteur électriqueThe brushes are placed on the floor first and the machine's legs are solved backwards from them. Where they scrub, the parquet turns to a mirror that reflects a stage filmed in 1957. Film: Inverse kinematics, contact and reach · Game: Sketchpad: drag a brush; the constraints keep the legs · Proposed sign: 36 Limpid and opaque Play the game · How it was made
The barberOne lever drives every joint of the mechanical arms, shoulder first and wrist on top. The mirror on the wall shows the job done by hand, by a barber filmed in 1959. Film: Forward kinematics, hierarchical body logic · Game: Character Animator: triggered states on a beat · Proposed sign: 35 Mirrors face to face Play the game · How it was made
Correspondence by phonograph and cinemaA voice without words moves the speaker's head, the receiver and the big horn, syllable by syllable. The screen answers with a dance filmed in 1914, and the music plays as loud as the dancers move. Last, the card is shown on a 1948 picture-house screen. Film: Sound to action · Game: JibJab: recast the answer, keep the performance · Proposed sign: 34b Sonsign Play the game · How it was made
Air cabs over ParisThe card is taken apart into nine planes at different distances, and as the camera drifts each slides at its own rate. A crop duster filmed in 1954 comes in low over the rooftops through the far sky. Film: Multiplane parallax, spatial collage · Game: Rive: a cab that runs on states · Proposed sign: 27 Movement of world Play the game · How it was made
Flying firemenEach drop of water keeps the aim the nozzle had when it left, so the jet whips; the flyers ride springs behind the wind. Real fire and smoke add their light to the card's, and the jet runs into a hose crew filmed in 1947. Film: Secondary dynamics, inertia and restraint · Game: State machines: flyers that refuse · Proposed sign: 13 Milieu Play the game · How it was made
Motor skatesA light comes up behind the card and the skaters turn to shadow figures on hinged legs. The man who fell tries to get up and falls again. A 1927 crowd watches from the horizon, and the screen behind them becomes a real shadow play. Film: Lotte Reiniger, articulated shadow theatre · Game: Drawing substitution: when a pose fails, swap the drawing · Proposed sign: 41 Bodies of attitude Play the game · How it was made
