CRASH LAB / The Crank Boy

The Crank Boy

One postcard printed in France in about 1910, a school in the year 2000, and what grew from it: seven versions of a game built by AI programs, an opening film, and Futuretown, where the series' seven cards become short films, games and one playable town. Every film and game uses a different operator from the SCION animation swarms, and every one lets in real archive film from the cineosis-lab library, the way CINEOSIS does.

The Town

The seven cards as one world, joined by a drawn power line from the crank boy's machine. Nothing runs unless somebody turns the crank, and power reaches each card later the farther it is from him. Each card is a game, and the three handle-holders can be recast into each other's places: the same performance, a different hand on it.

Toward CINEOSIS

the archive comes into the card

CINEOSIS sets the crash-test family inside unaltered archive shots. These films take shots from the same cineosis-lab library the other way round, into a surface the card already has: a mirror, a screen, a polished floor, a sky, a fire. Each short is proposed as a candidate passage for one of the 45 signs. That is a reading, not a result: none has yet had the blind review the CINEOSIS handmade passages had.

Films that go together

6 works of two or more films, in order

Some subjects have more than one film. The school card has three (a short, a trick-film proof and the game played), and the Bolas films run in chains: the family in three, CINEOSIS in five passes, the Found Odyssey as a search for its look and then seven episodes, WYGWYL from one ink cel to a poem. The film library shows each chain in order, with how each film follows the one before; each Bolas film page says the same.

À l'écoleThe boy turns the crank and the lesson runs down the wires. One pupil's head comes off on its coil and the teacher's book takes its place. The book opens on a classroom that was really filmed. When the boy stops, everything stops.
