CRASH LAB · WORLDS

The wall above is the reference board. Every tile below it is a still taken from the running page; touch one to go in.

41 worlds.
One orchestra.

Each world is a different answer to the same question: what does it look like when every jellyfish is one sound? Some are places, some are ways of seeing, and five are made on a wall.

NEWThe street

Paint and paper on concrete: a stencil, a three-colour poster, torn posters, a spray can, and a paste-up of real film prints. Heavy paint runs; anything pasted keeps the white edge of its paper and casts a shadow.

STENCILthe street: two cut sheets and a can, on a rolled cream groundTwo sheets only: black for whatever is lit, red for whatever is brightest.CUT POSTERthe street: three flat colours, red on top, pasted to a wallThree flat colours. Nothing is shaded; things are one colour or the next.DÉCOLLAGEthe street: the live picture, captured a beat at a time, pasted and tornNothing is drawn on the wall. Each sheet is a frame the camera took at the beat it was pasted, printed in two inks; it is live for one beat, then it sets. Tearing one off uncovers an older moment.SPRAY WALLthe street: the orchestra sprayed on concrete in its own coloursEverything lit is sprayed, with mist round it, and the heavy marks run.PASTE-UPthe street: prints taken from the live camera, pasted over each otherEach print is what the camera saw at the beat it was pasted, exposed onto a frame of the film. It is live for one beat, then it sets. The wall is one view at many moments.

17Builders

Any city can be seen through any lens, and each lens rebuilds a cut in its own way. Each link opens ALLWAYS · ARGON through that lens.

BY HANDCarried by hand, one frame a bar, and set into its floor.WARM PHOSPHORDrawn in light: every route from source to film is left glowing, so the city fills with the paths the cuts took.THE SETBroadcast: the frame is not carried. It jumps in three channel changes and arrives.SILHOUETTE THEATREHung on a rod, like a shadow puppet, and walked across.WARM CAVECarried as heat: the floor it reaches stays hot long after.LIGHT ALONEBurned in: the frame lights its floor and goes up as an ember.MAGIC CIRCLEWound in: the carrier spirals round its path on the way, as a needle would.SUN IN YOUR EYESBurned onto the eye: each delivery leaves an afterimage of the whole city.THE CARDPrinted: every delivered frame is laid beside its tower in rows, a contact sheet of the cut.THE MEASURED BAYMeasured: each frame travels with its in time, its out time and its length.CUT PAPERPasted: each frame is stuck to its tower at whatever angle it landed.SPACETIME WORMSTrailed: the frame leaves copies of itself along the way, so the route is a strip of film.STENCILStencilled: each frame is sprayed onto its tower through a cut sheet, a little crooked every time.CUT POSTERBillposted: every frame is pasted up beside its tower in rows, sheet over sheet.DÉCOLLAGETorn down: a frame is torn off its source, which stays bare, and pasted on the cut. REFThe brief

The reference boards the street worlds were made from. They are pictures; the worlds above are the running thing, and they do not match these yet.

Stills were captured in a slow test renderer with the sound off. Motion and sound were not judged by the machine that made this page.
