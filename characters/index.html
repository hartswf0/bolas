The Crash-Test Family

Bola, Shim and little Dot

Bola

yellow · red shoes · a bola on a cord for an antenna

The lead dummy. Bola takes the physical, opens the letter, misses the stair and walks into every archive clip in the atlas.

In: The Physical, Crash Lab, CINEOSIS · in the crash lab, CINEOSIS · hybrid pilot, CINEOSIS · hybrid atlas, CINEOSIS · handmade, CINEOSIS · Hand Butter, Handmade · first batch…

Shim

teal · blue shoes · six per cent taller than Bola

Bola's partner. Shim holds the door, takes the hug, carries the heavy end and stands beside Bola in the screening room.

In: Crash Lab, CINEOSIS · in the crash lab, CINEOSIS · hybrid pilot, CINEOSIS · handmade, CINEOSIS · Hand Butter, Handmade · signs 1–8, Handmade · signs 13–41

Dot

coral · black shoes · seventy-two per cent of Bola's size

Little Dot. She is lifted, carried and passed between the other two, and she runs under what they have to step over.

In: Crash Lab, CINEOSIS · in the crash lab, CINEOSIS · hybrid pilot

The extras

The crowd of grey dummies who fill the CINEOSIS worlds: queues, parades, audiences, the people Bola walks through.

The poet

a jointed paper cut-out from his own photograph · brass pins · paper hands

Cut into eleven pieces along his bones and moved by the rig like paper on a table. Replacement heads come from every photograph of him.

In: Poem 07, The paper poet · first proof

The replacement heads

six heads, mirrored to twelve turns

Front, three-quarter and profile heads, the head bowed in red light, the hand at the glasses and the camera held to the face, chosen frame by frame from the rig's turn and her words.
