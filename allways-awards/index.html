How to Judge an Archival Film:
Ratings, Comparisons & Category Awards

Every film across Claude Opus 5.5 (12 films), Claude Sonnet (11 films), OpenAI Sol (24 films & studies), and Gemini Argon (85 films) was evaluated on a falsifiable 100-point, 5-pillar rubric combining direct media telemetry (duration fidelity, single-shot cut cleanliness, loudness & acoustic continuity) with the independent blind critic records.

01 / Methodology

The 5-Pillar, 100-Point Jury Rubric

25 POINTS1. Brief & Structural Realization

Does the film fulfill its target duration and execute every required structural phase (e.g., three passes, memory resets, unannotated vs. annotated pass) without truncation?

20 POINTS2. Archival Foraging & Visual Purity

Uses real public-domain moving-image recordings rather than synthetic vector substitutes, with clean single-shot trims free of accidental internal camera cuts.

20 POINTS3. NEARMATCH Editorial Craft

"A match should make a difference easier to notice." Evaluates spatial registration, gesture/rhythm bridges, and preservation of the post-action pause.

15 POINTS4. Acoustic & Temporal Architecture

Synchronized optical sound, room tone, or spoken counterpoint (-19 LUFS) without jarring -91 dB digital dropouts, plus earned hold durations.

20 POINTS5. Blind Critic & Ethical Provenance

Independent blind critic verdict (PASS vs. inconclusive vs. fail), Round 1 → Round 2 verified repairs, and strict ethical source disclosure.
