The Found Odyssey · cast

The cast

Every character is the same keyed body in a different costume, drawn by one ink renderer: a new character is a costume preset, not a new body. The black-figure style is the same renderer with every fill black and every line incised.

Characters

Odysseus

The man of many turns. Every episode is his, and every costume below is drawn on his body.

In: Episode 1 · NOBODY, Episode 2 · THE GUEST-GIFT, Episode 3 · THE RAFT, Episode 4 · CIRCE, Episode 5 · THE BLOOD, Episode 6 · THE KNOT, Episode 7 · THE LOOP, Drawn tests · NOBODY, THE SIRENS, ARGOS…

Odysseus, black-figure

The vase-painting style used for title cards and flashbacks: every fill black, every line incised.

The crew

His companions, who open the bag of winds, eat the cattle and are turned into pigs.

In: Episode 2 · THE GUEST-GIFT, Episode 4 · CIRCE, Episode 6 · THE KNOT

Polyphemus

The Cyclops of Episodes 1 and 2, who asks the hero's name and is told "Nobody".

In: Episode 1 · NOBODY, Episode 2 · THE GUEST-GIFT

Zeus

Decides by stamping a rubber stamp on the screen where the gods watch Odysseus (Episode 3).

In: Episode 3 · THE RAFT

Hermes

Sent to Calypso's island (Episode 3); flickers like an unsettled projection as he brings the moly (Episode 4).

In: Episode 3 · THE RAFT, Episode 4 · CIRCE

Calypso

Keeps Odysseus on her island for seven years and builds the raft that takes him off (Episode 3).

In: Episode 3 · THE RAFT

Poseidon

The god who wrecks the raft (Episode 3).

In: Episode 3 · THE RAFT

Circe

Her cup and wand fail against the moly; on "You are Odysseus" the wand drops from her hand (Episodes 4 and 6).

In: Episode 4 · CIRCE, Episode 6 · THE KNOT

Athena

Compiles herself from grey box to ink to colour on the shore of Ithaca (Episode 7).

In: Episode 7 · THE LOOP

Tiresias

The blind seer: he drinks, gets his colour, and warns him off the herds of the Sun (Episode 5).

In: Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Anticleia

His mother. He tries three times to hold her, and each time his arms close through her (Episode 5).

In: Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Agamemnon

The king who never blinks; Odysseus draws his eyelids down for him, and they stay open (Episode 5).

In: Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Achilles

Drinks in a white field, and his colour leaves with him as he strides away (Episode 5).

In: Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Ajax

Stands apart the whole time, never drinks, and goes away in silence (Episode 5).

In: Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Sisyphus

After the credits of Episode 5 his stone gets away from him and rolls back down past him.

In: Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Odysseus, the old beggar

The disguise Athena puts on him, skin, hair, eyes and rags, one part per word (Episode 7).

In: Episode 7 · THE LOOP
