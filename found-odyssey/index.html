The Found Odyssey

Homer's lines are asked by the CALL track and answered by archive speech; a drawn cast acts in graded archive plates. Each episode runs one formal experiment.

Episodes

The Found Odyssey · 3:27Episode 1 · NOBODYBook 9 in three minutes: a prompt box asks for the hero's name, the archive asks for "our young friend's", and Odysseus answers "Nobody." The Found Odyssey · 8:29Episode 2 · THE GUEST-GIFTThe Cyclops again, as a flashback at the Phaeacian feast, told by two tellers who remember it differently. The experiment: the recollection-image. The Found Odyssey · 8:21Episode 3 · THE RAFTBook 5 as one story, each act seen from the point of view of whoever is watching it: the gods on their television, Calypso, Poseidon, the sea. The Found Odyssey · 6:29Episode 4 · CIRCEBook 10 re-cut around one line per scene, the place where power shifts: the Laestrygonians, Circe, and the order to sail to the house of Death. The Found Odyssey · 7:34Episode 5 · THE BLOODBook 11 in grey, where the only colour is the blood in the trench and whichever shade has drunk it. The Found Odyssey · 7:04Episode 6 · THE KNOTBook 12, built on hands that hold things: the mast, the cord, the oar. A held object is drawn inside the grip: palm, object, fingers, thumb. The Found Odyssey · 6:47Episode 7 · THE LOOPBook 13 as a loop: seven agents try to land the sleeping man on Ithaca and fail, until a grip holds, Athena compiles, and the old beggar is put together part by part.
