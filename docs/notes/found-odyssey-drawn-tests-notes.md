# The Found Odyssey, drawn: three test shots

This reel runs 1:56. It has a title card and three shots, each with its own card:
- **NOBODY**, Book IX: 24 s.
- **THE SIRENS**, Book XII: 33 s.
- **ARGOS**, Book XVII: 50 s.

## Sound (observed)

The sound in each shot is the Found Odyssey CALL track for the scene, taken from the cineosis-lab repo (`lab/odyssey/found-audio/call/`). In this track the studio line plays first, then the archive's spoken answer.
- I cut out the lines and placed them with pauses of my own. I did not change the words or the order.
- Under the answers, the captions show the source film and year for each answer.

| Shot | Scene | Lines | Archive answers from |
|---|---|---|---|
| NOBODY | OD-B09-S08 | 9–11 | *Humor and Health* (NIH forum, 1993) twice, then *Hi Diddle Diddle* |
| THE SIRENS | OD-B12-S03 | 7–9 | *Communications and Our Town* (1947), *Introduction to Haiti* (1942), *Man On The Land* (1951) |
| ARGOS | OD-B17-S03 | 0–2 and 8 | *Mother Mack Trains Her Seven Puppies* (1952), *Mother Mack's Puppies Find Happy Homes* (1953), *Fire! Patty Learns What to Do* (1951), *All in One* (1938) |

## Picture (observed)

The footage comes from clips in the cineosis-lab archive library (`lab/clips/`). These are not the clips the Found Odyssey picked for these lines, because those are hosted on r2.dev and this workspace could not reach them.

| Shot | Clip | How it plays |
|---|---|---|
| NOBODY | *Eyes and Their Care (An Introduction)*, 1941: an eye in extreme close-up | Retimed so the eye holds still while he talks and blinks on each answer |
| THE SIRENS | *Amateur Film Medicus09a*: surf | Two stretches, slowed, joined by a dissolve |
| ARGOS | *Mother Mack Trains Her Seven Puppies*, 1952: puppies by driftwood on a beach | Slowed while the narrator speaks, faster for "He rolls over and over" |

The ARGOS footage is from the same film the archive answers line 0 with.

## Drawn (generated)

- **Characters and props:** Odysseus, the two crewmen, the black-figure ship, the Sirens' vase panel, the stake, the staff and the ropes. All are original designs.
- **References:** the references you sent were used only for drawing principles:
  - ink line and flat colour, with long rubber-hose limbs, from the packaging art;
  - the black-figure vase panel;
  - drawn figures that touch real footage, cast shadows on it, and are shaded to match its light, from the live-action and animation films.
- **Animation:** bodies are keyed by hand in the rig (`crash/m_od1.js` to `m_od3.js`) and drawn by `ody/toon.py`. Lines are redrawn every second frame.
- **Mouths:** Odysseus's mouth in NOBODY and the Sirens' mouths follow the loudness of the audio.
- **Generated sound:** footsteps, the sea and surf beds, and a faint fire-truck wail under the Sirens' first answer.
