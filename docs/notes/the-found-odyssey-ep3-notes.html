THE FOUND ODYSSEY · Episode 3 · THE RAFT

All of Book 5 is one continuous story, from the gods' council to Odysseus asleep on Scheria. 8 min 21 s, -16 LUFS.

The experiment

Each act is shot from the point of view of whoever is watching it at that moment. Before each act, a CINEOSIS tile shows that act's sign.

Part Sign What it does
Previously (none) Frames from Episode 2 play under the Cyclops's curse.
I · Olympus So 1, Solid perception The gods watch Odysseus on a television set standing on a cloud. Zeus decides by stamping a rubber stamp on the screen. Hermes is sent.
II · the cave Ic 4, Icon Calypso's island is shown through her face, in close-ups held past the point of information. Archival eyes stand in for hers. She is drawn in violet.
The shore (none) Calypso and Odysseus on the beach at dusk. She gives him tools and he builds over four days.
III · the raft Lq 2, Liquid perception The picture flows like water. Seventeen days and nights pass as seventeen turns of light. The gold hand counts them ("17 DAYS.").
IV · the storm Ga 3, Gaseous perception Poseidon's storm is a field of moving dots (a BEFLIX gas), and the raft breaks inside it. Athena holds back every wind but one.
After the credits (none) Odysseus sleeps in a bed of leaves. Athena stamps it "SEALED".

Observed and generated

  • Every spoken line comes from the Found Odyssey CALL tracks for Book 5. The words and their order are unchanged; only the selection of lines and the pauses were chosen (ody/ep3/cut.py).
  • The answers come from The Found Odyssey's line-by-line matches. When an answer's own film is in the library, it plays in a framed inset.
  • The plates come from the cineosis-lab clip library: Bryce Canyon, The River, beach and sea footage.
  • Generated: all drawing and animation, the stamps, the flow warp, the dot storm, the ink notes, the sound effects and the music.
  • New to the cast, drawn in the same ink renderer: Zeus, Hermes, Calypso, Poseidon and Athena.
