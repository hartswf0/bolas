CINEOSIS HAND BUTTER: making-of, production record

A making-of film for the handmade CINEOSIS film, about 4 minutes long. The film's own cast performs it in a screening room: - Bola and Shim; - the Critic, a new third figure. He stands for the fresh blind reviewer and wears a blindfold, because he sees the pictures but not what was meant.

Observed and generated

Observed (on the screen): - archival plates, raw: the atlas sheet, 10.4 and 7.2; - frames of finished handmade passages: 10.4, 14.0, 14.1, 4.0, 30.0, 7.2, 21.1, 44.1, plus a montage of 18; - frames of the automatic atlas film, in scene 3; - the actual key code of 14.0 (crash/m_x14_0.js), in scene 3; - the sign card for 14, from the handmade film.

The passages carry their own generated figures, as recorded in cineosis-handmade-validation.md.

Generated: - The room: wall, curtains, floor, the screen mask, projection falloff and light spill. - The three figures. Every body pose is keyed by hand in crash/m_hb1.js through m_hb8.js. - The Critic's blindfold, drawn by the rig. - Shadows. The projector is the key light, so shadows run along the floor and up onto the picture. The shared integration standard applies: contact patch, cast shadow, tone, grain, softening.

Voices: - Synthesized locally with Piper, from open voice models: - Bola: en_US lessac; - Shim: en_US ryan; - the Critic: en_GB alan. - No recorded human voices are used.

Mouths: - Opened by the loudness of each synthesized line, sampled every 2 frames (hb/voice.py → crash/m_hb0.js). This is generated, not keyed by hand. - Expressions are keyed by hand per line.

Sound: - the screen's own track, low; - synthesized footsteps and room tone.
