# Epistemic ledger — Trouttown: Tempus Fugit

Three categories. Material never moves between them silently.

## VERIFIED LOCAL RECORD (with sources)
- Boone Creek was known as Kraut Creek. The name stuck when the sauerkraut cannery (Boone was North Carolina's leading kraut producer from the 1920s to the early 1980s) dumped waste into it. Source: Clio, "Kraut Creek (Boone Creek)", https://theclio.com/tour/79/17
- The creek was piped underground beneath what was ASU's baseball field. It was exposed again at Durham Park (2004). A 1965 photo caption says it "was piped underground". Source: Clio, as above.
- 28 May 1973: the creek flooded after 1.6 inches of rain. Source: Clio, as above.
- Daylighting near Rivers Street and Duncan/Peacock Halls is studied and planned, not built. Timeline: a feasibility study in 2019–20 (App State, New River Conservancy, Town of Boone, Jennings Environmental), a positive Federal Interest Determination from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers in March 2022, and a follow-up study. "The project awaits future funding decisions." Source: Appalachian State, "Boone Creek", https://appstate.edu/appalachians-future/building-physical-infrastructure/boone-creek
- Brook trout (*Salvelinus fontinalis*) are char, but they are not Arctic char. Rainbow trout (*Oncorhynchus mykiss*) and brown trout (*Salmo trutta*) are introduced. (Standard taxonomy.)
- The curb inlet in the plate carries East Jordan Iron Works casting text and the warning "DUMP NO WASTE! DRAINS TO WATERWAYS". This is read off the supplied plate. The plate's location, the casting's date and its watershed connection are **not verified**.

Only the last two lines of the credits use verified records. The story itself states no local fact.

## CHARACTER BELIEF OR RECOLLECTION
- The foreman trusts the 1968 plan ("INLET 7 — OUTLET TO 1968 LATERAL"). The plan is invented; within the story it is his belief, and the story shows it is wrong.
- The fitter's chalk arch on the bricks is his hypothesis, drawn before it is proved.

## INVENTED TROUTTOWN RULE
- The clock marked TEMPUS FUGIT runs only while the water presses, and it runs backward. It stops when the pressure falls.
- The inlet chamber, the bricked-up stone arch and the old channel behind it are invented geometry.
- The black-and-white view of "the place before the street" is a **reconstruction drawn for this film**, not an archival photograph. It is labelled on screen for its whole duration ("RECONSTRUCTION — drawn for this film, not a record") and again in the credits. Its background includes the engraved landscape from user plate B, which is itself a generated image.
- In the story the reconstruction works as the crew's *archival trace*: it shows where the old arch stood. The film does not claim that such a record of this inlet exists.
- Trouttown's drowned street, CURRENT COURT, the SILT ADVISORY notice, the crew's tin overshoes and their work habits are all invented.

## Kept out of the script
- Fishing regulations, links between underground streams, and Cherokee traditions are not used. Nothing in this episode needed them, and none was verified.
