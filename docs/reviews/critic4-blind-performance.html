Blind performance test (written before reading notes)

Method: 2 fps contact sheets for each passage (critic4/.jpg), then 1.5–12 fps crops around the figure (critic4/crops/.jpg). Times are in seconds.

x13_1. Color industrial panorama (pan), grey stone parapet across the bottom

  • Beats: 0–3 stands with back to camera, surveying. 3.5–5 hands on hips, then hand to chin. 6–9.5 shifts weight and gestures at chest. 10–13.5 walks right along the parapet; red shoes plant, shadow stays under the feet. 14–16.5 stops and points up at the smokestacks.
  • Attitude: from a contemplative surveyor to someone who has identified something.
  • Meaning: a small observer surveys an industrial town and points out its smokestacks.
  • Integration: the parapet gives clear support, and the shadow falls down and to the right under the feet. Saturated yellow over a faded Kodachrome plate is acceptable. No pops.
  • Grade: READS (the point at the end is small).

x13_2. B&W coal field / hopper cars

  • Beats: 0.5, 2, 4, 6, 11: bends to pick up a lump. 1.5, 3, 5, 7.5: tosses it over the shoulder, and a dark speck arcs in the sky at 3.5–4 and 6. 8–11 stands. 12–12.5 holds a hand out. 13.5–14.5 walks forward. 14.5–16 squats and studies a lump held in both hands.
  • Attitude: mechanical repetition, then a pause to contemplate.
  • Meaning: a tiny worker hand-clears an endless coal field, then stops and looks at one lump.
  • Integration: grayscale grade matches. The soft shadow falls to the lower left, which is plausible. Contact is good.
  • Grade: READS.

x14_0. 1940s pencil-workers picket line, two figures (light and dark)

  • Beats: 0–9.5 both march with the strikers holding blank three-line placards. 10.0–10.33 the signs tumble. 10.33→10.5 the signs vanish (pop), and a grey plank appears at the bottom edge. 10.5–14 both stand empty-handed, hands at chest, while the strike moves on.
  • Attitude: participation, then loss of nerve or loss of the sign.
  • Meaning: two outsiders join a strike with blank signs and drop out.
  • Integration: the scale is child-size against adults. The sign drop pops (10.33–10.5). The plank on the ground does not match the sign that fell. The figures walk in place, and the crowd does not interact with them.
  • Grade: PARTLY.

x14_1. Field brawl / crowd game, two figures wrestle

  • Beats: 0–1 standing face to face. 1–3 crouch in a sumo stance. 3.25 lunge. 3.5–9 locked grapple. 9.5–11 sit on the ground. 12 stand. 12.5–13 tangled again.
  • Meaning: two figures wrestle like the background crowd.
  • Integration: the overcast plate has no hard shadows, yet the figures cast very dark, hard, detached blob shadows offset to the right (FAIL, whole clip). The figures look semi-transparent mid-lunge (3.25). The scale sits in the near-middle ground and is fine.
  • Grade: PARTLY (the action reads, the integration fails).

x15_3. Close-up of a laughing man in a helmet

  • Beats: 0–1 stands. 1.2–1.6 startled O-mouth. 3.6–6 turns away, hands behind back, fidgeting. 6.4–7.6 thumbs-up toward the man. 8.0 hand on head. 9.6–10.8 covers its face.
  • Attitude: startled, then shy, then approving, then embarrassed.
  • Meaning: a little bystander reacts to a man's big laugh, warming to him and then wincing.
  • Integration: no support surface (the figure hangs over out-of-focus railings) and no shadow. Tone and grain match.
  • Grade: PARTLY. The beats read, but the thumbs-up and the facepalm contradict each other without a cause.

x15_4. Young man talking against damask wallpaper

  • Beats: 0–1.2 sits down on the chair-back doily (legs disappear). 3.6–4.4 head down, hand to face. 4.8–6 side-eye with worried brows. 6.4–8.4 hunched. 8.8–9.6 straightens. 10–12 both hands clamped over its ears.

  • Grade: READS.

x16_0. Color chorus line on stage
