## 31 Mk Mark
TEST: Can you point to at least three occurrences of the same image or motif that the characters or viewer come to read as one habitual series with a meaning to be deciphered?
COUNTERFEIT: A montage of three similar establishing shots of a city is repetition without inference: nobody treats the series as a clue, so it is style, not a mark.
