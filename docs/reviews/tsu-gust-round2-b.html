The gust test · critic round 2 · round 2 identity and physical tests

Round 2 identity and physical tests. A separate agent that did not make the work, started Oct 18 00:25 EDT. Brief and report verbatim. File paths in the brief are the working paths on the day.

The brief

You are an independent reviewer of a 7-second animation test of a handmade articulated cardboard puppet. You did not make it. Judge only what you see.

SOURCES (the real puppet): /home/claude/puppet/review2/source_07.jpg, source_09.jpg, source_10.jpg (front, backlit against a TV screen on a black box), source_05.jpg and source_01.jpg (side/rear, earlier, no scarf).
ANIMATION: /home/claude/puppet/review2/seq_1.png and seq_2.png = every second frame (12 fps), left to right then top to bottom, labeled. Full-size frames: /home/claude/puppet/review2/full_f*.jpg. Stills, not playback; say when a judgment needs playback.
Method, disclosed so you can check it: frames 0–21 are the unaltered photograph 09. After that, the puppet is photograph 09 cut into its cardboard segments and rotated about its joints. Background revealed behind moved parts is reconstructed. The head's right side face is generated (plain cardboard texture), because no photo shows it in this light. The scarf tail is the photographed tissue bent along a chain. White wind streaks and soft contact shadows are generated. Any colour difference between source files and frames should be checked by measuring pixels, not by eye: frame 0 is pixel-identical to the cropped source.

Run two tests. For each failure give frame numbers, the largest gap, and an acceptance condition.
IDENTITY: does it stay this photographed object? Check the drawn face (eye lines, smile), the clear push pins on both sides of the head, the long torso, the segmented limbs with dark connectors, the block feet, the tissue scarf with torn fringe, and that torn edges and seams stay on their surfaces. Flag additions the real puppet lacks, and anything that drifts.
PHYSICAL PRESENCE: support and contact (which foot bears weight; whether the planted foot slides or sinks; whether a lifted foot clears the box top and lands without sliding afterward); joint pivots; lighting consistency; revealed background (smears, ghosts, seams, halos); scarf behaviour; camera.
Then the single highest-leverage remaining repair, and the strengths to preserve.
Format: IDENTITY: PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL + findings. PHYSICAL: PASS/PARTIAL/FAIL + findings. REPAIR: one. PRESERVE: list. Under 400 words.

The report

IDENTITY: PARTIAL - Head side face (f038–f166, worst f049, f064, f088–f166): the generated right side reads as a separate flat board standing beside the head, not as the side of the box. In f049 and f064 there is a strip of background between it and the front face, about 8–12 px at 1280 width. The right push pin now comes out of this board, so pin and head look detached. In f088–f166 the board is skewed away from the head's top edge. Accept when the side face shares the front face's top and right edges with no background between them, and the pin's shaft enters the box's side. - Face drawing, the pin on the left side, the long torso, the dark connectors, the block feet and the scarf fringe all hold. Torso colour measured RGB (36,22,11) at f000 and (35,21,11) at f166, so no colour drift. - No other additions to the puppet. The wind streaks are scene effects; they cross the puppet's body (f049, f059, f064). Accept when streaks pass behind the puppet or stop at its outline.

PHYSICAL: PARTIAL - Planted foot (viewer-right) moves while it should be bearing weight. Its right edge goes from 748 px to 768 px (f059) and its bottom rises from 921 to 915. After the step its bottom rests at 918, 3 px up from the photograph. Accept when its bottom-edge position stays within 1 px from f000 to f166. - Stepping foot (viewer-left) slides 6 px at f049 before it lifts. It clears the box top by about 55 px at f059, lands 20 px to the left at f074, and does not slide afterwards. But it lands at 924 against 931 in the photograph, so it hangs 7 px above the box top with no contact shadow visible. Accept when the landed bottom is within 2 px of the box top and a contact shadow appears from the landing frame on. - Revealed background: there is a vertical ghost smear at x≈940–955, y≈430–495 (f088–f166), where the hand and pin tip were in the photograph. There is also a pale ice-coloured blob at x≈705–740, y≈195–225 (f088–f166), at the original right pin's position. Accept when neither spot differs from the background around it. - Halos: there is a light outline round the moved left leg (f049) and the lowered right forearm and hand (f106–f166). Accept when there is no rim brighter than the background around it. - Joint pivots look right in stills. The scarf bends plausibly. The camera is locked. Whether the timing and weight shift read correctly needs playback.

REPAIR: Rebuild the head's side face as part of the box: one solid with the front face, sharing its edges, with the pin set into it. This failure shows in more than half the frames and is the one place where the puppet stops being the photographed object.

PRESERVE: f000–f021 pixel-identical to the source; the cut-outs keep the photographed cardboard texture, torn edges and seams; the drawn face and pins on the front face; consistent backlit colour (measured); the hikers and sky gap stay intact behind the moved arm; the stepping foot does not slide after landing; the locked camera; the tissue scarf with its torn fringe.
