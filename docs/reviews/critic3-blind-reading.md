Method: per passage, a 2 fps timestamped contact sheet (critic3/sheets/), six full-res key frames (critic3/f/T_*.jpg), and denser 6 fps crops where the action was unclear (x5_4, x12_0, x10_3). Times are in seconds from passage start.

## x1_1 (15.25 s): dark ornate interior, circular ring/portal; the plate irises from the dark interior into a lit exhibition hall
- Beats: 0–1.5 empty plate. 1.5–2.5 she slides in from the left, cropped at the chest, and looks at the ring. 3–3.5 wide-eyed surprise as the hall appears through the ring. 4–5 she stands in front of it. 6–8 two-handed "ta-da" presenting gesture, then a chin-stroke/point. 9–11 points into the hall. 12–13 smug, pleased smile while pointing. 14–15 turns her back to camera, facing the hall, as if about to go in.
- Attitude: curiosity, then surprise, then host-like pride, then resolve.
- Meaning: she discovers a doorway into another world and decides to step through.
- Integration: never shown below the hips, so no ground contact. She is lit as warmly and brightly as the lit hall while standing in the near-black foreground, so she reads as a pasted sticker, not a body in that dark space. No shadow or rim light. She is in front of the ring, but the depth relation is unclear.
- Grade: PARTLY. The action is legible, but tone and placement fail.
