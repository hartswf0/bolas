# Club dancer rigs (first real characters built from the 08 sheet)

Three dancers were rigged from their own dance paintings: the dress, the shirt and the jacket. Each has two cards: a front dance pose and a 3/4 reach. Each card is its own master and is only posed on its own rig.

Built: `rigkit/chars/club_dress__club.py`, `club_shirt__club.py`, `club_jacket__club.py`, with `rigkit/parts` built for each.

Rendered at rest (studio/club_rigs.jpg). Checked by eye, not by a critic yet.

Known defects, seen in the render:
- A stray white blob by the dress dancer's right hand in the 3/4 pose (paper left in the cut).
- A stray white blob by the jacket dancer's hand in the 3/4 pose.
- The jacket dancer's 3/4 pose runs off the right edge of the frame.

What is still missing (body coverage): no neutral stand, no back, no profile. These dancers can only dance in front or 3/4.
