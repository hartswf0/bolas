# Drawings to commission, per actor

Each line is a body pose the scenes use that no clean drawing of this actor covers. A drawing must be the same character, the same costume and the same scale as the actor's existing drawings. Other costumes do not count: the black t-shirt poet has back views and a fall, but they are a different costume, so they can't stand in for the track-suit poet.

## Poet (track suit)
1. **Stand, back.** Full figure, back to camera, arms at the sides. Needed for the turn ring and for any back view.
2. **Ring, 3/4 back.** Full figure, 3/4 turned away from camera. Needed for the spin.
3. **Ring, back.** Same as the 3/4-back pose, turned fully away.
4. **Sit.** Full figure seated, 3/4 view, on a chair or ledge with no scenery baked in.
5. **Kneel or crouch.** Full figure, 3/4 view, one knee down.

## Vintage man (suit)
1. **Stand, front.** Only one clean front stand exists, and it's cut from a different card. Needed for entrances and standing.
2. **Walk, front.** Two frames with the legs apart, for a front-on walk.
3. **Reach or point, arm out.** Full figure, one arm out, 3/4 view.
4. **Sit.** Full figure seated, 3/4 view.
5. **Kneel or crouch.** Full figure, 3/4 view.
6. **Lean.** Full figure leaning on nothing (no baked-in prop).
7. **Lie or fall.** Full figure falling or on the floor.

## Vintage woman (gown)
1. **Stand, front.** The only front drawing is cut from a crop. Needed for standing and the spin landing.
2. **Walk, front.** Two frames with the legs apart, for a front-on walk.
3. **Sit.** Full figure seated, 3/4 view, in the gown.
4. **Kneel or crouch.** Full figure, 3/4 view.
5. **Lean.** Full figure leaning, no baked-in prop.
6. **Lie or fall.** Full figure falling or on the floor.
