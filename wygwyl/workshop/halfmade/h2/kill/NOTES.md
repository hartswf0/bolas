# H2 kill experiment: what I saw (description, not a grade)

Three held-out poses, each reproduced by one whole painting from another family. Sheet: `sheet.jpg` (target painting | pick, same pixel scale, feet on one line).

- **Front stand (13/06-refocus) -> 11/05-read-water-reathe-1, D=0.75.** Same man, same stance and boots, same painted hand. But the hands are wrong: the target has one hand at his temple, sunglasses off and held in the other hand; the pick holds a book to his chest with sunglasses on. No painting outside poet.13 has a hand at the head with the glasses off.
- **Crouch (04/04-4-empty-cuffs) -> 13/03-letter-fire-crouch, D=1.55, GAP.** It reads as a crouch, but it is a 3/4 crouch reaching down with a letter. The target is a frontal squat with both arms raised and empty cuffs, plus the poet.04 chest disc. The poet.04 paintings are also drawn bigger in the head and bulkier, so at matched body scale the pick looks like a smaller-headed man.
- **Seated profile with cup (13/part-2) -> 11/pool-edge-seated-three-quarter-journal, D=1.17, GAP.** It is a seated man, but on a stone block, holding a book, in 3/4 view. The target is a cropped profile with a cup and no visible seat. The only other seated paintings are the ledge sit seen from behind (08) and a t-shirt sit on a bed (02).

Nothing is warped, so there are no seams. Every failure is a wrong choice of painting or a missing painting, and each one goes into `../gaps.json` as a brief.
