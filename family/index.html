The measured bay

every vignette is a small drama and a crash test, read by four instruments

  • Weightthe centre of mass stays inside whatever holds the body up
  • Feeta planted heel moves less than 0.6 cm a frame
  • Handsa hand lands within 2 cm of where it was sent
  • Contacttwo bodies never pass through each other
