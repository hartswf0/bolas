The measured bay

The bay is a stage with instruments. Each vignette is a small drama and a crash test at once. The instruments report what a camera would miss:

  • Weight and support. The centre of mass is dropped to the floor and must sit inside everything holding the body up: heels, balls, knees, hands on furniture, another body.
  • Feet. A planted heel may not move more than 0.6 cm in a frame, the left foot stays on the left, and a planted foot may not face more than 65 degrees from the hips.
  • Hands. A hand sent to a body or a handle must arrive within 2 cm and may not sit inside a torso it was not sent to.
  • Bodies and props. Two torsos may not overlap; a prop changing hands is taken where it is and may not jump; a full cup may not be jerked or tipped.
