ALLWAYS 055 / TURN / V03

A 55-second adaptation of SCION 055 (Words — Polysemic Relay).
One film, eight passages, seven source clips. The organizing word appears only after the image sequence. No synthetic imagery. Aspect ratio preserved; low-resolution archival clips upscaled without generative restoration.

V03 changes from recovered V02:
- Removes opening explanatory title so the viewer can infer the word.
- Holds the radio through its illuminated state, ending before the next source action.
- Uses 11–17 seconds of the dance excerpt to emphasize the visible turn.
- Adds a newly composed instrumental score. It is neither source sound nor reconstructed historical sound.
- Silences the page passage (35.6–40.6) and the final look (46.6–48.88).

SELF-REVIEW / INCONCLUSIVE
Inspected sampled frames from all seven recovered clips and eight-frame motion strips from reel, radio, page and dance. Reviewed sampled final-output frames; this is not a claim of continuous normal-speed viewing or an independent viewer test.
Strongest proposed pivot: 17.2–28.8, turning a control changes the radio's visible state.
Largest gap: reel and clay repeat rotation; their semantic separation is weaker than the radio's switch to 'turn on'.
Observable repair condition: a viewer should infer at least two meanings without the end title. If the viewer reports only circles/spinning, replace one rotational shot with a visibly established 'take turns' action.
The return uses an overlapping interval from the same child excerpt; no later historical event or changed emotional state is claimed.

Prompt departures remain explicit:
- TURN adapts the prompt's proposed DRAW; this does not satisfy the literal drawing/curtain/drawer proof.
- Source audio has been omitted; required source-sound bridges remain untested.
- Independent word-identification and emotional-direction judgments remain pending.
- Source rights are unverified; the ledger records access/provenance, not permission clearance.

REBUILD
Unzip to an empty folder. With Python 3, numpy, scipy, Pillow, ffmpeg and ffprobe installed, run:
  python3 film55/make.py
The script expects the bundled editkit paths relative to its parent folder. It recreates the score, picture assembly, ledger and MP4. No online source downloads are required.
