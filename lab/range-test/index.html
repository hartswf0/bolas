    Ranges are measured from the skeleton's own joints: shoulder elevation is the angle between trunk and upper arm, elbow, hip and knee are flexion from straight, neck is head-over-trunk deviation, trunk is inclination from vertical. Norm marks follow common clinical reference values (shoulder 180°, elbow 150°, hip 120°, knee 135°). Video measurements are 2D, so rotation and depth are not measured.
