PLAY CRITIC, round 4: paeleo-00-v4.mp4

What I inspected: 1 fps and 2 fps contact sheets of the whole film with timestamps. 8 fps crops from 52–58 (climax), 58–63 (the neck test, 40 frames) and 53.5–58 (the tags). Full-frame stills at 60.0 and 62.5, and a 320 px still at 63.0. 6 fps sheets from 27–35 and 45.5–49.5, full 24 fps from 33.3–34.5, and 12 fps from 24–26.5 and across both title crossfades. A 640 px still at 69.0 and a 360 px still at 74.0. Per-0.25 s RMS for the whole track, plus a log spectrogram. In those last three ranges (33.3–34.5, 24–26.5, the crossfades) I looked at sampled frames only; I did not view every frame.

1. VERDICT: PASS. All parts of the previous critic's repair condition and the climax condition hold, and I found no pops in the ranges I sampled.

2. SUPPORTING MOMENTS
- 58.0–63.0, every 8 fps frame: the dog holds the same sill pose. The neck piece runs unbroken from shoulder to head, and the collar sits well below the shoulder top line. At the 4x zoom of 59.5 there is no gap.
- 62.5 and 63.0 (320 px): nose is about 183 px left of the right jamb's outer edge and the rump about 102 px right of it, so 36% is outside. Pass.
- 53.5–63.0: the "threshold" tag (y≈85–175) and the "dog · lying down" tag (y≈225–315) never overlap during the camera push.
- Climax: "come in." finishes typing at about 56.25. The head lifts at about 56.4 toward the left and the lit interior, and holds through about 57.5 (well over 6 frames). It lowers onto the paws at about 57.7, then stays down until the fade at about 62.8. Pass.
- 31.8–32.3: the person's sit goes through crouch frames and is continuous.
- 33.3–34.5: the dog gets up through intermediate frames. It turns by flipping edge-on at about 33.9–34.0, which reads as a cut-paper flip, not a pop.
- 14.0–14.7 and 17.2–17.9: clean crossfades of about 0.5 s, and the tags fade back in after the title.
- 69.0: the AEL / PAELEO / PEO nesting reads at 640 px.
- Sound: there is a gesture at each prompt, with peaks at 21.0–21.5 (call), 24.75–25.25 (reach), 56–57 ("come in"). It drops to about -40 dB during the sill hold (59.5–63.5), and music returns for the close. The quiet hold works.

3. LARGEST REMAINING GAP
The sill pose, which the film rests on for about 6 s, is a jumbled shape at phone size. Both forelegs are stacked over the neck and chest, and one upper leg crosses the collar, so the collar shows as two separate red bars (see 59.5 and 62.5). At 320 px the front end reads as a brown knot with an eye, not clearly "head resting on paws".

4. REPAIR CONDITION
In every 8 fps frame from 58.0 to 63.0:
- no foreleg segment overlaps the neck or collar;
- the collar is one continuous band;
- both front paws lie forward of the chest and under or ahead of the chin.
In a 320 px still at 60.0, the chin visibly rests on a paw, meaning the paw outline touches the lower jaw.

5. OTHER PROBLEMS, RANKED
1. Climax look, 56.4–57.5: the lifted head turns to a near-frontal three-quarter view, so it partly reads as looking at the camera rather than into the room. A clearer profile toward the interior would make "looks in" unambiguous.
2. Diagram at 69: the labels "intent / description / consequence" and the "THE PLACE / THE METHOD" kickers are about 4–5 px tall at phone width. The P→E→O arc also never closes back to P, so the recursion is not shown.
3. Porch ambience, 18–63: a broadband noise floor of about -30 dB reaching up to 16 kHz could read as hiss on phone speakers.
4. Title at 14.5–17.5: the small red line above "THE THRESHOLD" is unreadable at phone size.
5. 25.4–25.6: at the "turning its back" flip, the dog is a thin edge-on sliver for about 2 frames. That is acceptable, but the turn-away depends on the tag to read.

6. STRENGTHS TO PRESERVE
- The lineage from imagetext to environment text to worldtext (0–14) is clear, with smooth caption-to-tag transitions.
- Refusal has visible consequence: the dog backs away at 21 and turns its back and lies facing away at 25–27.
- The approach (40–49) is continuous and patient.
- The dog lies half inside and half outside at the jamb, which states the idea in one image.
- "come in." gets a look, then a settle. The film does not obey.
- The quiet audio hold at 60–63.
- The lockup at 74 reads at 360 px, and the credits are clean.
