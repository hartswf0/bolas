CRITIC REPORT, paeleo-01.mp4 (reel 3)

What I inspected: the mid-frame of all 54 lenses at 360 px wide, plus frames at +0.1, +0.8 and +1.5 s into each lens. I also looked at title and end-card frames (1, 3.5, 3.95, 4.05, 90.3, 90.5, 93 s), full-res crops of the dog in 03, 09, 23, 28, 40, 44, 53 and 54, and the plain dog from paeleo-00 at 47 s. For sound I measured RMS and spectral flux around all 55 cut times, including 90.4. I did not run any renderer. The reel never shows a plain dog in the same pose, so my "pixels differ" figures are a palette-match proxy: the share of dog pixels whose colour is not in the plain dog's tan/brown palette. Treat them as approximate.

1. Verdict: does not pass. The repair condition fails in three places: the dog-difference test fails on 03 and 53, the overlay-spacing rule is broken at least twice, and the 29.6 s cut has no onset.

2. Lenses that still fail
- 03 Ernst (7.2–8.8 s): the frottage hatching only shows at 1280. At 360 the dog reads as plain tan (proxy about 25%, borderline).
- 53 Atlanta (87.2–88.8): this is a sepia grade over the whole frame plus film sprockets. About 99% of the dog's pixels still match the plain palette, so it fails clearly.
- 54 Provenance (88.8–90.4): the dog itself is plain. The roughly 30–35% difference comes from labels and graph lines covering it, so it is borderline.
- 09 Igarashi (16.8–18.4): darker mesh texture, proxy about 14%. Visually it is just distinct.
- 05, 24, 40 and 44 now pass by eye: misty white, pink close-up, pale lit paper, and multicoloured parts.
- Overlay spacing (my own classification):
  - Part labels on 42 and 45, three apart.
  - Small dark text tags on 29 and 30, adjacent, and also on 19 and 21.
  - Top panels or cards on 47, 49 and 52.
- Neighbours that look too alike at phone size: 12 and 13 (same rainbow parts), and 29, 30 and 31 (one mask idea three times).

3. Broken picture or collisions
- The three earlier breaks are fixed:
  - Lens 14's dog is whole in all sampled frames.
  - Lens 38's dog is present, with a selection box.
  - Lens 49's person is present, as a grey maquette.
- "SCION nn" and the look text are about 3–4 px tall at 360 width, so they can't be read at phone size. The titles are about 8 px.
- Lens 47 starts with a striped dark dog that turns red within the lens, a small continuity flicker.
- I saw no caption colliding with the dog.

4. Largest remaining gap: sound at 29.6 s (the cut into lens 17). A loud event at about −15 dBFS runs from 29.2 to 29.65 s and drops away at the cut, so the cut has an offset instead of an onset. The other 53 cuts and the end card at 90.4 each have an onset of about +18 to +25 dB within 20 ms.
Repair condition:
- Within 30 ms after 29.6 s, RMS rises at least 12 dB above the preceding 100 ms.
- No sound longer than 0.3 s runs across any cut.
- Lenses 03 and 53 reach at least 25% non-plain-palette dog pixels at 360 px.
- No overlay type repeats within 3 positions (fix 42/45 and 29/30).

5. Strengths to preserve
- Cuts land exactly on the 1.6 s grid, with clean onsets.
- The dog stays recognisable and continuous in almost every lens.
- These lenses are strong: 02 Reiniger silhouette, 06 Whitney, 08 Sketchpad, 18 Cartoon Saloon, 24 Live2D, 31 matte, 33 IK, 36 BBW heatmap and 51 Mouris trails.
- The title and end card are clean.
