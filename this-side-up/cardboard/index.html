How it was made

  1. Grid the turntable clips and cut Boone's poses.
  2. Build the cardboard world kit and the paper effects kit.
  3. Re-pace the lean: real time, ramp, then 0.55x.
  4. Shoot the other scenes as short clips: raise, fire, click, gun, fall, lying, sit.
  5. Credits as shipping labels.

What this case teaches

  1. When the effects share the puppet's material, the film reads as one made world.
  2. Visible fishing line answers how the bullet moves.

How it differs. v1 and the Marble lean were photographic. v3 is all cardboard.
