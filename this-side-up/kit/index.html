The shooting kit

Can the set tell the person with the camera what to do, without a paragraph of instructions?

A bullet-time mock-up from one photograph, a calibration reel for the TV behind the set, and a pose guide in two versions: with words, then without.

How it was made

  1. Fake the orbit first from one photograph, and label it as a simulation.
  2. Make a calibration reel for the TV: 18 sections, each frame carrying its own 16-bit code.
  3. Make a pose guide with one card per photo: the target pose, the change in red, the moves in words.
  4. Remake it without words: the move plays, the moved part is outlined, a shutter flash says take it.

What this case teaches

  1. A frame code on the TV ties any photo of the set to the exact background frame.
  2. The second guide removes the text and keeps the information.

How it differs. The first case made the film from photos already taken. This one plans the photos.
