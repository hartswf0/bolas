How it was made

  1. Animatic from the TV-plate stills.
  2. Ray-march an ice cave in numpy.
  3. Give every drop its own clock.
  4. One drop lands on his head; he slips and spins.
  5. Sound from the same drop clocks.

What this case teaches

  1. A rule that runs in a radius is easy to see when hundreds of things obey it.

How it differs. v4 stopped the whole world. ICE stops all of it except a bubble around Boone.
