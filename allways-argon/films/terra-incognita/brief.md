# TERRA INCOGNITA (Material territories)

**Concept:** A passage through film’s damaged edges, where scratches, light leaks, and blank intervals become terrain and the recorded world keeps surfacing.

**Proposed First Proof:** Build a sixty-second passage from three recorded film surfaces with different visible defects. Establish a recognizable image before following a scratch or light boundary into the next fragment. Hold one surface long enough to reveal that the apparent landscape is a material carrier. Let a recognizable scene return, then end at a blank interval with its actual recorded noise or an identified original sound composition. Keep observed damage distinct from any generated extension.

**Boundary Note:** A generated transition is a separate authored layer. The material-study proof does not establish a historical relationship between otherwise unrelated fragments.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 071 — Standard Gauge — The Carrier as Evidence

**Creative Theorem:** Build a passage from three recorded film surfaces with different visible defects. Establish a recognizable image before following a scratch or light boundary into the next fragment. Hold one surface to reveal the material carrier, return to a scene, and end on a blank interval with optical noise.
