# PLANIMETRIC (Held frames)

**Concept:** Locked-off images turn rooms and public spaces into stages where routine, background activity, and a small deviation become the event.

**Proposed First Proof:** Stage or select one ninety-second fixed view of a room with two practical activities at different depths. Establish their ordinary relation before a small obstruction changes one person’s route. Hold the frame while the second activity continues, letting the viewer discover the conflict through timing and sound. End on the changed arrangement without a rescuing close-up. If staged, present it as fiction; if archival, preserve the actual action and camera behavior.

**Boundary Note:** Static, centered, and planimetric are separate constraints. The fixed-view proof takes precedence over supporting operators that permit camera moves.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 086 — Songs from the Second Floor — A Tableau That Accumulates Pressure

**Creative Theorem:** Select fixed, planimetric views of rooms and workspaces with practical activities at different depths. Hold the frame while foreground and background tasks interact and a small route change alters the arrangement, without cutting to a rescuing close-up.
