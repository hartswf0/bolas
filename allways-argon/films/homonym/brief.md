# HOMONYM (Words change worlds)

**Concept:** A word passes between voices and images, changing sense at each cut until speech seems to edit the world around it.

**Proposed First Proof:** Construct a sixty-second relay around one word with three visually legible senses. Establish its first use in an intact spoken context, then let an action carry the next meaning before another voice names it. Make the third sense alter the sequence’s tone or stakes. Return to the opening utterance with the new associations still active. Keep original speech intelligible, and test a version without explanatory captions before adding another semantic branch.

**Boundary Note:** A semantic connection needs a defensible interpretation beyond a similarity score. An assembled utterance must be presented as an authored construction, not attributed to a recorded speaker as something they actually said.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 055 — Words — Polysemic Relay

**Creative Theorem:** Construct a relay around one word with three visually legible senses. Establish its first use in an intact spoken context, let an action carry the next meaning before another voice names it, make the third sense alter the stakes, and return to the opening utterance.
