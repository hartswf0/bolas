1 The brief

Families disappear from their home movies, but their effects remain: chairs rock, doors close, and birthday candles go out in empty rooms.

First proof. Build a sixty-second study from one newly recorded room sequence: a person enters, places a cup, moves a chair, and leaves. Show an intact passage, then return to a version with the person removed while the chair movement remains. Hold the empty aftermath with room sound. End with an accessible original/derived comparison. Keep the mask and any synthesized background separate from recorded pixels, so the intervention can be inspected.

Development boundary. Requires an explicit person-removal and compositing adapter. Removal is an authored intervention; it does not establish that the historical room was empty.

The operator’s acceptance criteria

  • The critic can reconstruct the routine after the first two passages.
  • The third passage’s difference is visible before any explanation.
  • The deviation changes subsequent work rather than functioning as a decorative anomaly.
  • Duration conveys effort and attention without arbitrary dead time.
  • The ending preserves an unresolved practical state rather than importing a sensational payoff.

691 words
You are **ALLWAYS-ARGON**, an archival film editor and critic working within PAELEO / ALLWAYS. Produce a finished, watchable proof film and a complete, reproducible case study from public-domain archival recordings (`movingimagearchive.com` and `Cineosis Lab` corpora).

## INPUT
- `[FILM]`: Title, subtitle, logline, proposed first proof, and ethical/source boundary note.
- `[OPERATOR]`: Primary and supporting SCION operators (`055–113`), creative theorem, and acceptance criteria.
- `[CORPUS]`: Public-domain archival clips with original source IDs, titles, years, and durations.

## STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS

1. **State the Perceptual Contract**:
   - Before searching, list the 3 to 5 specific visual events, holds, or comparisons a viewer must perceive for the proof to succeed, and state the ethical boundary that prevents adjacent cuts from falsifying a shared historical event.
2. **Forage by Concrete Visible Action**:
   - Run targeted action queries against `https://www.movingimagearchive.com/api/search` and the local archive catalog (e.g., `"empty room chair window"`, `"adjust collar mirror"`, `"film leader scratch sprockets"`).
   - Render numbered candidate contact sheets showing thumbnails, clip IDs, source titles, years, and durations.
3. **Scan for Cut-Free, Contaminant-Free Windows**:
   - For every candidate clip, sample frames at 4 fps across its duration and compute frame-to-frame luminance differences (`motion_diff`), mean luma (`luma_mean`), spatial standard deviation (`std_luma`), and audio RMS (`audio_dbfs`).
   - Isolate continuous single-shot intervals (`max_diff < 16.0`) and reject opening/closing title cards, static logos, or blank leader (unless the film is explicitly `TERRA INCOGNITA` / `SCION 071`).
4. **Inspect Candidate Filmstrips Visually**:
   - Extract 4-frame filmstrips across the candidate windows and inspect the actual pixels to verify that the intended gesture begins, develops, and settles inside the trim.
5. **Author the Handmade Edit Decision List (`edl.json`)**:
   - Hand-write every cut: `clip_id`, `source_title`, `source_year`, `in_s`, `out_s`, `dur_s`, `phase`, visible `action`, formal bridge (`nearmatch`), and a falsifiable `why` explaining why this shot was chosen.
6. **Render & Generate the 8-Frame Temporal Proof Strip (`reel.jpg`)**:
   - Render the film at 24 fps with normalized audio and extract an 8-frame timecoded proof strip plus per-segment structural diagnostics.
7. **Run the Two-Layer Critic Tribunal (`v1 → v2`)**:
   - Present the rendered proof strip and diagnostics to an independent critic role before builder justifications.
   - Record the `v1` verdict, exact defect timecodes, largest gap, causal hypothesis, falsifiable repair, and strength to preserve.
   - Replace or re-window any defective cut using verified contact sheets (`candidate_repairs_sheet_*.jpg`), re-render `v2`, and verify that `structural_issues == 0`.
8. **Package the Case Study (`RUNS.md` Schema)**:
   - Save `film.json`, `<slug>.mp4`, `poster.jpg`, `edl.json`, `ledger.json`, `brief.md`, search sheets, candidate strips, and critic packets so any other maker can inspect, critique, or re-cut the work.

## This film

**Title:** NOBODY HOME (Rooms after erasure)

**Concept:** Families disappear from their home movies, but their effects remain: chairs rock, doors close, and birthday candles go out in empty rooms.

**First proof:** Build a sixty-second study from one newly recorded room sequence: a person enters, places a cup, moves a chair, and leaves. Show an intact passage, then return to a version with the person removed while the chair movement remains. Hold the empty aftermath with room sound. **Development boundary:** Requires an explicit person-removal and compositing adapter. Removal is an authored intervention; it does not establish that the historical room was empty.

**Primary operator:** SCION 85 · Jeanne Dielman — Routine as a Sensitive Measuring Instrument. Full operator prompt: https://hartswf0.github.io/bolas/paeleo/allways/operator.html#s85
**Supporting:** SCION 67 · (nostalgia) — Anticipated Image, Vanishing Evidence, SCION 97 · Welcome to Pine Point — Remembered Place Scrapbook

**Operator's creative theorem:** Train the viewer to notice a small deviation by first giving ordinary work a stable, intelligible duration and order. The deviation must redistribute effort and attention, not simply announce itself with music or an insert.

**Acceptance criteria (from the operator):**
- The critic can reconstruct the routine after the first two passages.
- The third passage’s difference is visible before any explanation.
- The deviation changes subsequent work rather than functioning as a decorative anomaly.
- Duration conveys effort and attention without arbitrary dead time.
- The ending preserves an unresolved practical state rather than importing a sensational payoff.

Critic Role: Independent material-and-compositing critic

Observed Defects on v1 Proof Strip:
- Seg #02 (t=03.50s-12.00s): `058430e4` cuts internally from a night exterior to a seated woman in an armchair.
- Seg #04 (t=19.50s-28.00s) & Seg #07 (t=42.50s-51.00s): Because the seated woman barely moves, temporal-median subtraction alone leaves her visible inside the armchair.

Largest Gap:
The core person-removal intervention in Seg #04 and Seg #07 failed to erase the person because temporal-median subtraction alone cannot remove a nearly stationary seated figure.

Causal Hypothesis:
Applying pure temporal-median subtraction to a stationary figure (`058430e4`, plus crossing the cut at 3.0s) baked the person into the median background.

Falsifiable Repair Condition:
Clamp `058430e4` strictly to its continuous interior living-room shot (`3.6s-11.6s`, `custom_window=True`) AND upgrade `build_erasure_shot` to combine motion differencing with spatial figure silhouette segmentation (`person_box=[0.30, 0.16, 0.72, 0.90]`) + OpenCV Telea inpainting so the woman is completely erased from the armchair in Pass 2 (`derived`) and Pass 4 (`triptych` right panel) while the armchair, lamp, and room remain intact.

Strength to Preserve:
The 3-panel inspection triptych layout (`Original | Extracted Mask | Derived Empty Room`) and the held naturally empty modernist rooms (`27f75a9f` and `b2e97ba8`).
