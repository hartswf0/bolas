# ANYBODY, ANYWHERE (Lives in parallel)

**Concept:** Strangers in different places perform related ordinary acts. Paired images reveal both their resemblance and the circumstances that keep their lives distinct.

**Proposed First Proof:** Build a ninety-second comparison from three pairs of situated recordings. Start with two people preparing a place to sit, allowing a moment of recognizable alignment. Let one person finish while the other waits or changes the task. Restore a wider contextual view on each side before the next pair. Conclude with two actions whose resemblance fails at the outcome. Preserve distinct sound spaces and show recording dates or unknown-date states where relevant.

**Boundary Note:** Parallel presentation does not establish simultaneous recording. The initial proof preserves different times and conditions. SCION 080 is reserved for a later shared-date edition with adequate timestamp evidence.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 056 — Symmetry — Imperfect Pairing

**Creative Theorem:** Build a comparison from three pairs of situated recordings. Start with two people preparing a place to sit, let one finish while the other waits, restore a wider contextual view on each side before the next pair, and conclude where resemblance fails at the outcome.
