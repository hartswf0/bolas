# ALMOST HUMAN (Unfinished gestures)

**Concept:** People hesitate, adjust their clothes, check a wrist, and look away. Their small unfinished gestures become a performance no one planned together.

**Proposed First Proof:** Make a seventy-five-second sequence from six situated gesture excerpts. Begin with one complete collar adjustment, including the pause afterward. Let another person begin a similar movement, then preserve their different outcome. Build a brief rhythm across three further gestures before allowing one participant to stop halfway. Return to the first setting after its action has ended. Use clothing, breath, and room sounds to carry the rhythm without assigning a shared emotional state.

**Boundary Note:** The comparison concerns visible actions. It must preserve source circumstances rather than infer identical motives or a universal account of humanity.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 057 — Moments — Microevent Attention

**Creative Theorem:** Make a sequence from six situated gesture excerpts: begin with one complete collar adjustment including the pause afterward; let another person begin a similar movement with a different outcome; build a rhythm across three gestures before one stops halfway; return to the first setting.
