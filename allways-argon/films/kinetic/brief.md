# KINETIC (Labor and interruption)

**Concept:** Work repeats across factories, workshops, and homes. Its mechanical rhythms are interrupted by maintenance, waiting, fatigue, and refusal.

**Proposed First Proof:** Make a ninety-second passage from three documented work processes. Establish a repeated movement in each, then briefly coordinate their rhythms through an original sound phrase. Break that coordination by preserving one complete maintenance or waiting interval at normal speed. Return to the first process after the interruption, revealing something the tighter framing initially excluded. End when one worker or mechanism stops. Keep contextual views available so the shared rhythm does not erase different conditions.

**Boundary Note:** Do not compress the Qatsi trilogy into one time-lapse style or infer fatigue and refusal from appearance alone. Include those conditions only when the source supports them.
