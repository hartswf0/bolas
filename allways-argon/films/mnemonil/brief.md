# MNEMONIL (A borrowed life)

**Concept:** A life story assembled from home movies begins to contradict itself: familiar rooms, gestures, and recollections belong to different people.

**Proposed First Proof:** Create a two-minute fictional composite from four clearly attributed source passages and original narration. Begin with an apparently coherent account of returning to a room. Introduce a second recording that resembles the room but contradicts a concrete detail. Let a later voice or image expose the difference rather than repairing it. Finish by showing how the sequence borrowed its continuity. Keep actual testimony separate from invented biography, and retain the identities of the underlying source records.

**Boundary Note:** The Library of Congress setting remains an earlier fictional proposition, not a claim of institutional involvement or an established source collection.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 074 — The Falls — A World Emerging From a Directory

**Creative Theorem:** Create a fictional composite from four clearly attributed source passages. Begin with an apparently coherent account of returning to a house and room; introduce a second recording that resembles the room but contradicts a concrete detail; expose the difference rather than repairing it.
