# COLLECTIVE INVENTORIES (A composite day)

**Concept:** A day is composed from many people’s ordinary work, with one gesture taking up where another leaves off.

**Proposed First Proof:** Make a two-minute composite morning from five situated participants or source records. Give each one intact practical action before connecting them through washing, carrying, preparing, or clearing away. Let a handoff between excerpts suggest continuation, then widen the frame to reveal its changed circumstances. Preserve one task that remains unfinished. End by returning to the earliest participant after their action, with a different pace and sound. Identify the assembled day as an editorial construction.

**Boundary Note:** The documentary primary governs source treatment. A shared action sequence does not make the recordings one documented day or their participants interchangeable.

**Primary SCION Operator:** SCION 065 — Five — Parallel Lives, Unequal Details

**Creative Theorem:** Make a composite morning from five situated source records. Give each one intact practical action before connecting them through washing, sweeping, preparing, carrying, and clearing away. Widen to reveal changed circumstances, preserve one unfinished task, and return to the first participant.
