The Found Odyssey

The Odyssey, one book per episode. The CALL track speaks Homer's lines; archive films answer with their own recorded speech; hand-keyed ink figures act in graded archive plates. Each episode runs one formal experiment (the recollection-image, point of view, the line, the colour rule, the grip, the loop) and opens each act on a CINEOSIS sign.

THE FOUND ODYSSEY · Episode 2 · THE GUEST-GIFT

The whole of Book 9 (the Cyclops), told as a flashback at the Phaeacian feast, by two tellers who disagree.

The experiment

Each episode tests one family of CINEOSIS signs. This one tests the recollection-image: the flashback, and what happens when two people remember the same events differently. Each act card is a CINEOSIS tile with the sign's number, symbol and name.

Part Sign What it does

THE FOUND ODYSSEY · Episode 4 · CIRCE

Book 10 runs as one story: the Laestrygonians, Circe, and the order to sail to the house of Death. It lasts 6 min 30 s at -17 LUFS.

The experiment: the line

This episode follows the direction "I don't need all of the lines, I need the line." Each scene was re-cut around one fulcrum line, the place where power shifts. The way each scene is made follows from that.

  • Framing. Everything else plays wide. The camera goes in tight only for the line, and holds after it so the answer has room.
  • Gesture. Each line gets one small held gesture, and the gesture carries the meaning instead of a caption.
  • Sound. The score goes silent about two seconds before the line. One low note sounds after it.
  • Writing. There are no ink notes in this episode and no labels in the frame. The act card names the sign, and the picture does the rest.

I · the sty Rd 25, Rich dream "they wept, remembering." The dream's colour stops swimming. The frame holds on one pig while a single tear gathers and runs. Then it cuts straight to the doors outside. Eurylochus keeps looking back at them, and Odysseus's hand goes to his sword hilt.
II · moly Rs 26, Restrained dream "black at the root, its flower white as milk." Hermes flickers like a projection that has not settled. The herb is the only solid thing in the scene. Odysseus's open hand comes up under it and closes.

Blending and sound

Blending. Drawn figures are matched to the film before they are composited: - their colour statistics are pulled toward the plate's; - they are softened to the plate's sharpness; - they carry the plate's grain; - their edges pick up light from the plate; - they get a contact shadow under the feet.

Then one film finish runs over the whole frame: gate weave, halation, lifted blacks and grain. The cold open stays in the black-figure pot style.

Sound. - Each act has its own score, built from the series' lyre motif (pluck, drone and frame drum, in a different mode per act). - The score is ducked under the voices. - Each place has its own room sound: open air, the hall, the dream. - The small sounds belong to the gestures: rope on a post, roots out of the earth, a hand closing, a wand hitting the floor twice, a sandal on sand.

The experiment: the colour rule

The land of the dead is cold black and white. Every plate is graded to grey. The only saturated colour in the film is the blood in the trench, plus whatever has drunk it.

  • Shades that have not drunk are drawn grey, half transparent and smeared, like something not quite there.
  • A shade that drinks gets its colour and its weight back while the blood is in it. The colour drains out again as it finishes speaking.
  • Odysseus, the only living man in the film, is drawn in colour and blended into the grey plates.

The structure carries on from Episode 4. Each scene is built around one line: - the camera goes tight only for that line; - the score goes silent before it; - one held gesture carries the line's meaning; - there are no ink notes and no labels in the frame.

The experiment: hands that hold things

You noticed that hands and held objects kept failing. Before this episode I rebuilt how a hand is drawn in the renderer (ody/toon.py):

  • A held object is now drawn inside the grip: the palm first, then the object, then four fingers across the shaft and a thumb over it. Nothing floats beside a fist.
  • Open hands, points, fists and flat palms are separate poses instead of one mitten.
  • The object is drawn by the near hand. The far hand either draws its own end of the cord or draws nothing.

Every scene in this episode is built on something held: an oar, a stick, wax, a cord, a spear, a cup, a spit's crank, a knot. The theme is the knot. Each line is carried by one pair of hands closing on something.

Episodes 4 and 5 were not re-rendered with the new hands.

THE FOUND ODYSSEY · Episode 7 · THE LOOP

Book 13 runs as one story, from the Phaeacian ship leaving Scheria to the old beggar walking up the high path on Ithaca. 6 min 48 s, -16.4 LUFS.

The experiment: pre-build bisimulation

Episode 5 governed colour (blood against grey). Episode 6 governed the grip (palm, object, fingers, thumb). Episode 7 runs the build of the world inside the film.

Ithaca is two coupled states in every shot: - A grey-box blockout. Every gift, the petrified ship, the olive, the cave and the rock wall are real 3D boxes in the same world coordinates as the rig, drawn through the plate's projection. - An interactive-fiction log in a column at the side of the frame: commands, the parser's replies, the checks between text and boxes, and the result of each check.

When the text and the boxes disagree, the frame is desynchronised and the plate will not ink or colour.

The machinery is in ody/ep7/loop.py, and it follows your spec: - ArchiveNode: a 3D box, an IF predicate, and a state (GREYBOX, INKED, COLOURED, or STONE for the ship). - HandRig.is_holding: the Episode 6 grip latch. A hand counts as holding a node only if its wrist is inside the node's box and it is drawn with the grip layering. - step_bisimulation: grip first (the node goes to colour and is locked); otherwise a desynchronised node reverts to grey box at the reset.

Every number printed in the log comes from that code reading the rig's joints and the boxes. Examples are a wrist inside a box, a hand's distance to a shoulder, a count of tripods, and how far the stone moved. The scenes are evaluated once over all frames, so any frame renders the same on its own.

Part Sign The line What happens
Cold open · the ship (none) "At sunset Odysseus boards the convoy ship and lies on a prepared bed in the stern." The last full colour before the loop. He lies down on the fleece and his hand opens and stays open. The empty ship then turns to stone far out, and the colour drains from the plate starting at the ship.
I · the loop As 6, Any-space-whatever "The crew carries the sleeping man and his treasure onto the shore." (played seven times) Seven agents (01_BARD, 02_BLOCKER, 03_GRIP, 04_BLEED, 05_PHYSICS, 06_DOUBT, 07_ATHENA) are seven instances of one base rig, each with its own skin. Each spawns as a bare wire rig, types its command, tries it and fails. For example, 03_GRIP's wrists are inside the tripod's box, but the base rig has no grip layers, so the hold is rejected. Each failure stamps a report, tears the frame and rewinds the plate to frame 0, and the line plays again. On the seventh pass, 07_ATHENA's hand stops short of the sleeping man's hand.
II · the wake (As 6) "Tripods, cauldrons, the woven robes, the gold." He wakes and is drawn in colour; the beach is still grey box and still resets. His right hand closes on a tripod, then his left takes the robe, then his right takes a low olive branch. Each grip latches, survives every later reset, and bleeds colour into the plate around it.
III · Athena Lo 36, Limpid and opaque "I am Athena, daughter of Zeus, and we two are alike, crooked-minded both," The seven agents walk in from all sides and become one figure whose skin cycles through all seven. His Cretan lie builds volumes (an island, a king's son, a Phoenician ship) that have no Ithaca ground under them, so the frame desynchronises and colour is withdrawn, from him too. Her rebuke knocks the lie volumes down. When she says her name she compiles from grey box to ink to colour, and her hand rests on his shoulder.

The lab shows two beats. Each is shown first as a six-way grid, then in each style at full size. All six styles use the same hand-keyed bodies, the same sound and the same animation layer; only the way the drawing meets the footage changes.

  • Eye: NOBODY, "Nobody is my name", the slump and the stake.
  • Beach: ARGOS, delight, then "He rolls over and over and yells", then shh.
# Style What it does
1 Clean ink Flat ink cartoon over the film, shaded toward the film's light, with a cast shadow.
2 Matched to the film The drawing takes the film's saturation, tint and exposure (so it flickers with it), is softened to the film's sharpness, carries the film's grain and dirt, and picks up film light at its edges (light wrap).
3 Scratched on the film Lines are white, as if scratched into the emulsion, and move every frame. Fills are translucent dye that wobbles from frame to frame.
4 Cut-out from the film Paper cut-outs with a white paper edge and a hard shadow, moving on threes. Each fill is coloured over a texture taken from the footage itself.
5 Black-figure A black silhouette with lines cut in the clay colour; the film's grain shows through it.
6 The film inked too The footage is painted down and its edges traced in moving ink, and the drawing is matched to it.

found-odyssey-drawn-v2.mp4

New in every shot

Animation layer. It is computed from the hand-keyed motion and changes how the motion is drawn, not the keys: - squash and stretch, and a squash after each landing; - a slow breath when he stands still; - a spring on the head, so the cap tip and beard follow through; - speed lines behind fast hands; - dust and impact marks where his feet land; - sparkles on joy.

Camera. Push-ins are cropped from the footage, and the drawing is redrawn sharp at every zoom. They are timed to the jokes: tight on Odysseus for each line he speaks, wide again for each archive answer so the footage can respond.
