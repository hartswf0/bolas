THE FOUND ODYSSEY · Episode 1 · NOBODY

Running time 3:27, 1280×720, 24 fps.

Running order

Part What happens
Cold open A prompt box types "Tell me of the man —". The archive asks for "our young friend's" name. Odysseus walks onto the box and says "Nobody is my name." He stamps on the box, the prompt text erases itself into NOBODY, and he bows to camera.
Title Meander border. The archive footage plays inside a vase cartouche. ODYSSEY stamps in letter by letter, and its O is an eye that blinks. Then the tagline, the "EPISODE 1 · NOBODY" plate, a black-figure ship crossing the frame, and Odysseus popping up behind the letters.
I · THE EYE NOBODY (B09). He hops on his own name and the eye ripples where he lands.
II · THE WINDS AEOLUS (B10). He sleeps on the bag of winds, the winds burst out, he is blown away and comes back.
III · THE SONG SIRENS (B12). He is lashed to the mast, with hearts drifting toward the Sirens' panel.
IV · THE DOG ARGOS (B17). Delight, a jolt, and a goodbye wave.
Credits The cast, the archive sources, the prompt quoted back as credits, and the studio card. Odysseus walks the border, sits to watch the roll, then waves.
Post-credits The eye. "Nobody I know, huh?" He shrugs.

Each act opens with a card: a film-strip whip out of the last frame of the previous act, a pluck, and the act title.

Fourth-wall devices

  • The prompt is on screen. The episode opens on the request being typed, and Odysseus treads on it.
  • Ink notes. A third voice scribbles on the captions in gold:
  • "young?" under "our young friend";
  • "again." and "?!" in NOBODY;
  • "zzz", "economy, one way", "remember this one" and "ouch" in AEOLUS;
  • "WEE-OOO" and "aww" in SIRENS;
  • "told you", circled against the source, in ARGOS.
  • He looks at us. He bows, winks, waves and shrugs to camera.
  • He uses the furniture. He sits on the credits border to watch them.
  • The credits quote the brief. The direction you gave ("make it funny", "a short micro episode", "break the fourth wall", "steep the brand identity") appears as credits under THE PROMPT.

Observed and generated

Observed: - Every spoken line is from the Found Odyssey CALL tracks. The words and their order are unchanged; only the pauses are cut. - The source titles shown on screen are the films each answer comes from.

Not the Found Odyssey's own picks: - The footage under the drawings comes from the cineosis-lab library (lab/clips/). The clips the Found Odyssey chose are hosted on r2.dev, which could not be reached from here.

Generated: - all drawing and animation; - the title, cards and credits design; - the ink notes; - the music, effects and footsteps.

Studio card: "CONSEQUENCE WORKS · FANTROL" is a placeholder and can be swapped in one line of ody/episode.py.

Character designs: all original. The reference images were used for principles only, not for any character.

Review: none yet. In the credits, Odysseus walks in front of the last source lines for about a second before he sits.
