CRASH LAB · CELLULOID CITIES

The archive is a city. The jellies are its archivists.

Fourteen cities built of film hold the whole Cineosis archive: a city for each poem, a street for each scene, a tower for each film, a floor for each shot. While a song plays, the jellies go into the towers looking for their material. On every street one of them comes back carrying a frame, the shot the poem’s cut chose, and splices it into a reel in the avenue. Touch any floor to read its address.
