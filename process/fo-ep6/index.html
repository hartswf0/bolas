Episode 6 · THE KNOT

Can a hand hold something without the object floating beside it?

Episode 6, THE KNOT: Book 12, 7:05. Every scene is built on something held: an oar, wax, a cord, a spear, a cup, a knot.

How it was made

  1. Rebuild the hand in the renderer: palm first, then the object, then four fingers across the shaft, then the thumb.
  2. Separate poses for open hand, point, fist and flat palm, instead of one mitten.
  3. The near hand draws the object; the far hand draws its own end or nothing.

What this case teaches

  1. Draw order is the fix: the layering palm, object, fingers, thumb is what makes a grip read.
  2. Choosing a theme that stresses the weak point (every line carried by hands) tests the fix on every scene.
  3. Older episodes were not re-rendered. The notes say so, so the change is traceable.

How it differs. Episode 5 governed colour. Episode 6 governs the grip.
