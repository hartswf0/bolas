How should a drawn figure meet found footage?

Three drawn test shots (NOBODY, THE SIRENS, ARGOS), a style lab showing six ways of meeting the footage, and a second pass in a seventh style.

How it was made

  1. Take the Found Odyssey CALL tracks as the soundtrack. Words and order unchanged; only the cuts and pauses are chosen.
  2. Key the bodies in the rig and draw them with the ink renderer.
  3. Hold the bodies, sound and animation fixed and change only the style: clean ink, matched, scratched, cut-out, black-figure, inked footage.

What this case teaches

  1. Changing one variable at a time is how a style is chosen: six styles, same motion.
  2. An animation layer computed from the motion (squash, breath, follow-through, dust) adds life without changing a single key.
  3. The footage reacts too: the eye ripples, sand dust takes the beach's colour. Both layers have to touch.

How it differs. CINEOSIS put a fixed character into footage. Here the drawing style itself is the experiment.
