WYGWYL cels and the paper poet

Can a paper puppet built from photographs of one person scale to a whole series?

The paper poet: eleven paper pieces, a bank of replacement heads, plates made from his photographs, and Poem 07 as the first full film. Cel-style tests run beside it.

31 sheets · Oct 19 17:53 to Oct 20 13:00

How it was made

  1. Cut the poet into eleven paper pieces with a white paper edge.
  2. Build a head bank: six heads mirrored to twelve turns, swapped by the head's yaw.
  3. Make plates from his photographs, with the holes filled and interface marks removed.
  4. Drive the rig from a cue list, so a new poem is a new cue file, not new keys.

What this case teaches

  1. Scale comes from banks and cues: more heads, more plates, more cue files, the same rig.
  2. Paper puppets fail in specific places, and each was fixed by a rule: an arm over the face, a floating head, a giant puppet on a close-up.
  3. The proof films come first, so the series format is tested before the series is made.

How it differs. Every earlier case keyed a drawn or rigged body. Here the body is cut from photographs of a real person.

What broke and what changed

  • The arm crossed the face; the hand landed on top of the head.Two-bone IK toward the wrist; a hand near the head anchors to the ear or chin and draws behind it.
  • The head floated above the neck.Neck pin placed straight below the chin; head size from the area above the chin.
  • A giant puppet on close-up plates.Scale capped and the figure anchored to the eye line.
  • Holes left where the person was removed from a plate.Push-pull fill; carousel interface marks detected and inpainted.
