Episode 5 · THE BLOOD

Can one colour rule tell the story?

Episode 5, THE BLOOD: Book 11, 7:35. The land of the dead in grey; the only colour is the blood and whoever has drunk it.

21 sheets · Oct 18 23:53 to Oct 19 02:38

How it was made

  1. Grade every plate to grey.
  2. Draw shades grey, half transparent and smeared until they drink.
  3. Give a shade its colour and weight back while the blood is in it; drain it as it finishes speaking.

What this case teaches

  1. A colour rule is a story rule: who has colour is who can speak.
  2. The rule is strict, so any break in it is visible and has to mean something.
  3. Gestures carry the line again: a hand stopping short, arms closing through a shade three times.

How it differs. Episode 4 governed framing and sound. Episode 5 governs colour.
