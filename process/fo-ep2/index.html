What does a flashback look like when two people remember it differently?

Episode 2, THE GUEST-GIFT: all of Book 9 told at the feast by two tellers who disagree. Five acts, each a CINEOSIS sign of the recollection-image.

How it was made

  1. Give each teller a style: his version a black-figure pot, Polyphemus's version the film itself, in colour.
  2. In the split-screen act, anything that crosses the seam changes style as it crosses.
  3. Two hands argue in the margins in gold and blue ink, crossing out each other's notes.

What this case teaches

  1. A point of view can be a visual style, and a conflict between two views can be a seam between two styles.
  2. Each act card names its sign, so the episode is also a lesson in five CINEOSIS signs.
  3. Callbacks (the hop on the name, "see Ep. 1") start the series memory.

How it differs. Episode 1 had one narrator. Episode 2 has two who contradict each other.
