How it was made

  1. Build the bay: grid floor, scale marks and fixed camera positions, so every scene is filmed against the same measures.
  2. Write each vignette as a small drama: a letter opened, a shoulder touched, a door pushed, a stair missed.
  3. Run each world through the checker and record the instrument readings beside the film.

What this case teaches

  1. Acting notes such as "the weight is wrong" become numbers: centre of mass over the feet, foot slide, hand reach, contact frames.
  2. Three bodies of different sizes doing the same scene expose problems one body hides.
  3. The checker built here is used by every later case that has a rig.

How it differs. The Physical audited one body. The crash lab measures how bodies meet each other and the room.
