How it was made

  1. Assign one perception sign per act: Solid, Icon, Liquid, Gaseous.
  2. Gods watch on a television set; Calypso is seen through her face; the raft flows like water; the storm is a field of dots.
  3. Add five gods to the cast, drawn in the same ink renderer.

What this case teaches

  1. The four perception signs become four ways of filming: a set, a face, a flow, a gas.
  2. A long continuous book tests whether the format holds for eight minutes.
  3. New characters are added by the same renderer, so the cast stays one family.

How it differs. Episode 2 split one event between two people. Episode 3 hands the camera from watcher to watcher.
