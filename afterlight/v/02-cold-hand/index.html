The note that produced it

“Uninspired and unwatchable.”
Make it a story that can be rendered again and again across all the cases.

What was made

One scene held constant: a score of six timed choices of a hand, rendered per case and per medium. The first case is the fangzhu, a bronze mirror set out under the moon to gather dew, in silhouette theatre.

Silhouette cut-outs on tinted tissue · one red material for the teller · counterfactual re-renders (take the moon away, clear the clouds)
