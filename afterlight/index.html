Afterlight

Light does not arrive in an empty world. What it becomes depends on what receives it, and what people do next.

Twenty-one versions · five eras

What light leaves behind

Afterlight began as an instrument for reasoning about light and heat and ended, for now, as ten sample studies of found sound and archive film. Each version on the strip was made in answer to a note on the one before it. The card shows that note, so the strip reads as an argument as well as a list.

Eras: Instrument → Essay → Story → Trailer → Mosaic. A teal dot marks a live instrument rather than a film. Each era name opens its set, which plays straight through and can be sent as one link or as the video files.
