The fourth pest

PAE-05 · SCION 17 · South Park: repeatable graphic grammar · cast after a garden scarecrow show, a tortoise, a public-health comic and the 1958 Four Pests poster

A scarecrow is for birds. Drive out the bird and nothing is left to eat what the scarecrows cannot scare.

Cast

  • The Astronaut, the Paperclip, Darth Hayder, the Bear, the Beekeeper, the Painter and the Sleeper (with his crow): the scarecrows.
  • The Medic and the Bug, carrying the Four Pests sign.
  • The Sparrow, the fourth pest. The Tortoise, the film's clock.

Made of

Flat construction-paper pieces with a fixed, seeded scissor wobble and a cast shadow, on one flat garden stage (wall, hedge, mulch, path). Each figure is drawn from closed shapes in its own frame; nothing is a photograph.

Moved by

Whole drawings swapped and held on twos: eyes lit or dark, brows up or down, mouths in a few shapes, a sparrow in sit, duck, peck and fly drawings. Only positions are interpolated. Each locust is assigned to one sparrow, which is at that locust on the frame it vanishes.

History

China's Four Pests campaign (1958) named rats, flies, mosquitoes and sparrows. The mass killing of sparrows was followed by plagues of locusts and other crop insects, and in 1960 sparrows were taken off the list and replaced by bedbugs.

Characters are new drawings after the user's photographs; no photograph, logo or poster is reproduced. No independent critic has watched this yet.
