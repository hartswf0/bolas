Independent critic (fresh agent; frames every 0.5 s plus dense windows, full resolution, frame-to-frame difference in a dog crop; verdict from the picture before any builder notes).

Per choice: backing away reads (crouch, head low, moves away; the wall tiles turn to trumpeters and alarms under the call); turned back reads (end-on box at 3.6–4.2, lies facing away); turning to look reads (rises ~7.5, facing flips 8.25–8.5 with the score's turn; end-on frame like a giraffe); coming head low weak (head near level; an unmotivated change from brown to bright orange cartoon fill at ~17–20); lying across the sill reads; looking in weak (reads as "looks up", not into the door). Reads as a dog throughout.

Boil: mild (mean frame difference 6–7/255 in a dog crop). Legible, not noise; too calm for Mouris.

Bottom-up: partly. Every surface is a quadtree of real archive tiles with meaningful subjects (animals for the dog, bulbs for the door, household objects for the wall, which switch with the words). But the dog's shape is a parametric silhouette with a drawn outline and eye; the tiles are a fill tinted until the images mostly disappear; the end card's "no figure is drawn" is not quite true; few rhythmic slots, holds or changes of cut speed. Different from PAE-00 in material, not in staging.

Craft: end-on views read as a table or a giraffe; unmotivated colour change; flat pink hand; dog tiles unreadable at playback size; tiny title line. Sound follows events.
