Independent critic (fresh agent; frames every 0.5 s plus dense windows, full resolution; verdict from the picture and sound before any builder notes).

Per choice: backing away reads (crouches facing the door, drifts away; hindquarters smear to a lump 1.6–2.3); turned back reads (front-on 3.62–3.70, clearly a dog; away by 3.79; lies facing away from 4.5, the fog thinning it to a faint lump at 7.0–7.3); turning to look weak, late (rises at 7.54 as a headless lump, muzzle up and away 7.9–8.2, head to the door only ~8.5: the "rise that still faces away"); coming head low reads; lying across the sill reads, modestly (sphinx-low by 24.0, head and forepaws in the door light, rear and tail out; part-in by overlap); looking in weak (the head turns to face the camera, a cat-like mask; the focus pull from the dog to the bowl inside carries the "looking in"); staying reads.
