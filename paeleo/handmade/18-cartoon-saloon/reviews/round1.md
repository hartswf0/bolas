# SCION 18 · critic round 1 · PASS (narrow)

Independent critic (fresh agent; all 912 frames, contact sheets and crops; verdict from the picture before any builder notes).

All six choices read on time: backing away (crouch, tail tucked, backs away facing the door, feet stepping); turned back (three-quarter rear 3.625, full rear 3.667, lies curled, rump and tail to the door, head away, 4.6–7.4); turning to look (head round to the door at 7.50; body rises through the turnaround drawings by 8.5); coming head low (below the back line 16–23, eyes half-lidded, feet planted while held); lying across the sill (really lying, forequarters inside the door post, tail outside); looking in and staying (back of the head to the viewer, into the room, 26.7–28.3). Mostly a dog in every frame: the rear view at 3.667–3.708 is a featureless bean; at 8.00–8.21 the tail is drawn on the head side.

Bottom-up: authored turnaround drawings swapped by substitution; a restrained rig on legs, tail and neck only; tapered brush outlines, grained painted fills, a patterned interior; no maquette, no photographic pixels. Fundamentally different from PAE-00. Against the lineage: a tube torso on noodle legs, not a strongly designed shape; spidery splayed crouch legs.

Defects: (a) tail on the wrong side 8.00–8.21; (b) the door light 6.2–7.2 is a uniform warm haze veiling the porch like fog, not light from the doorway, and the cut-away room is already lit; (c) the rear-view blob; (d) the low hand reads only as a lowered arm in the wide shot. Sound follows events; soft voice ~12 dB under the calls, ~10 dB above room tone. No state labels; end card true. Rights clean; nothing copies the studio's characters.

Repair conditions (recommended, not blocking):
1. 8.00–8.21: the tail attaches at the rump, away from the head, in every frame.
2. 6.2–7.4: the light visibly comes from the door opening (a brighter wedge or pool on the sill and boards), not a uniform haze.
3. 3.65–3.72: the rear view shows two hind legs or a head/neck silhouette.
4. From 6.8: the low hand readable at wide-shot scale (open palm toward the dog at about knee height).
5. Optional: the retreat crouch legs not splayed wider than the torso is long.
