BUILT FROM THE GENOME

1 · the four House of Dust axes are wired into the ledger: each deed has a room, a light (the fire clock raises it from deed IX), a material trace, and whoever else is in the room. Witnesses learn the deed if the room is lit; a sack entering a lit room finds the traces left there. A tied sack cannot act for two deeds. 2 · T ZERO in the player: pause and see the twelve ways the current deed could go, with the agents' probability and the deed's believability; tap one to rewrite it. 3 · SIX MEMOS: a MAP-Elites archive over exactitude (weakest strand) x multiplicity (effective number of doers) x visibility (deeds known to a third sack). Only films whose four strands hold enter. 4 · exact count: deeds I-IV enumerated with the full ledger, deeds V-XII counted by dynamic programming over (who is alive, who is tied until when). A property test on 20,000 random films agrees with the full ledger on every film. Believability tiers depend on continuous feelings, so they are estimated with a 95% interval.
