The Policies

Each slider is the weight kept by scenarios that break the rule. 0 forbids them. 1 ignores the rule.

Arson is not in the list of twelve deeds, so the arsonist gets a prior. Sliders are relative weights.

Source and clauses

  • The film's five rules are the ones counted on Twelve to the Twelfth; with them set to 0 and the rest to 1 the count here is 2,663,172,255,744, the same figure.
  • A scenario assigns each of the twelve deeds one perpetrator and one other victim among the four tenants: 12 choices per deed, 12^12 scenarios.
  • Payout = P(list planted) + P(list genuine) x (1 - P(voiding condition)). P(voiding condition) is the weighted share of scenarios where the clause holds.
  • Widow's life voids when Ogiva knifes or strangles the widow and the attack is fatal. Tour cover voids when the widow incites Belindo to suicide; the wider reading adds slander, blackmail and drugging of Belindo by the widow.
  • Fire cover voids when Inigo is the arsonist. Wig cover voids when Ogiva is the arsonist. The arsonist is independent of the deeds in this model.
  • If the list is planted, the model treats it as proving nothing, so no clause can be shown and all four policies pay.
  • The black bracket on a bar is the span of that payout when each rule in turn is set to 0 and to 1 with the others held.
  • Deed order is used only where a rule needs it (gun threat before tying). The counter enumerates three independent groups of deeds exactly; no sampling is used for the payouts.
  • Red cells and red arrows are deed assignments that trigger a voiding clause.
