The Whole House

The project has grown to three films, five live tools and two classroom films on two sites. This page is for presenting it: one sentence that holds it together, a running order, every piece and where it lives, what is still missing, and the look the pieces should share.

PAE-04 · presenter's page · Nov 2026

One question, five answers

A burned copybook lists twelve deeds and not who did them. That leaves 8,916,100,448,256 stories. Every piece of the project answers the same question, which of them, in a different way. Say this sentence first and each tool has a place.

  1. 1 · countHow many are thereTwelve to the Twelfth: the number, the ruler, the exact counts.
  2. 2 · sieveWhich can be thrown outThe rules. Calvino used the computer to discard stories.
  3. 3 · decideWhich would the tenants chooseThe film bank: memories, beliefs, plans, the most likely story.
  4. 4 · watchWhich makes the best filmThe watcher, the author, the Six Memos archive, By What Light.
  5. 5 · priceWhich does the insurer pay forThe policies. This is why Skiller asked for the count, and it was the missing piece.

What is missing

added

The story's reason for counting is money. Four policies each fail on one condition. The Lab 7 script has a scene for them, but no live tool priced them and Twelve to the Twelfth does not mention insurance. The policies page now does this by exact count.

added

The film's five rules, the ledger's consistency rule and By What Light's room and light rules all appear as "the rules" and give three different counts. Name them every time.

The ending inside the film bank

open

The bank's films run twelve deeds and then the fire. Skiller, Waldemar and the question of who lit the fire appear only in the first film and as row 13 of the deed bank. A last card on each bank film that names the arsonist prior and states the four payouts for that film would close the loop between the film bank and the policies.

The look

The pages use four palettes and three type sets. Two of them are deliberate and worth keeping as two registers. The other two are drift.

Watch

Film bank, deed bank. Big Shoulders Display, JetBrains Mono. Warm paper, brick red. Keep.

Read

Lab 7 film pages, Twelve to the Twelfth, the policies. IM Fell English, Inter, IBM Plex Mono. Cream, yellow mark. Keep.

Case page

Same type as Watch on a cool grey. Drift: move its background and accent to the Watch values. This page already uses them.

Payout engine, first draft

Blue-grey ledger. Retired: the policies page is now in the Read register.

Six things every page shares

  • Tenant colours. Twelve to the Twelfth already defines them. Use them wherever a tenant appears, in both registers.
    R RoesslerB BelindoI InigoO Ogiva
  • Roman numerals for deeds. I to XII in index order, XIII for the fire. The case page and the film bank do this; the policies page uses three-letter codes and should gain the numerals.
  • One red. Cut, void and fire are the same idea. Use #a3321f for all three; it is #8a3a2c, #a3321f and #b8432c today.
  • The number. 8,916,100,448,256 is always set in mono with its commas, never rounded in a heading.
  • The top bar. The same six links in the same order on every page.
  • Sacks, not people. No faces and nothing shown for the four deeds behind the shut door. Diagrams draw tenants as coloured circles with a letter.
