Every Film in the Burned House

A process account of combinatorial cinema made with compiled agent swarms, after Calvino

Watson Hartsoe · Georgia Institute of Technology · draft 0.1, 10 November 2026
Drafted with Claude from the project repository, its published pages and one working session. Not yet reviewed by the author.

Italo Calvino's "The Burning of the Abominable House" gives a programmer twelve deeds, four dead tenants and no record of who did what to whom: 1212 = 8,916,100,448,256 possible stories. We report how that space was made watchable. A library of 54 agent-swarm prompts, SCION, and a compiler that turns a source into one standalone operating instruction, were used to animate every deed between every pair of tenants, attach consequences and memories to each deed, count the consistent stories exactly, let simulated tenants and a simulated audience choose among them, and price the four insurance policies that motivate the story. The work ran in eleven days. Its method was tested first on a smaller problem, one flour sack animated six ways, where eighteen rounds of independent criticism showed that the builders' own checks passed work the critics failed. We give the dated process, the evidence for each step, the failures, and what a publication still requires.

1 · The problem

Calvino wrote the story in 1973. A house has burned with its four tenants: the widow Roessler, her adopted daughter Ogiva, the landlord Inigo and the wrestler Belindo. A copybook survives with only its index, twelve deeds in alphabetical order. An insurance man, Skiller, asks a programmer to reconstruct what happened, because four policies each fail if a particular tenant did a particular thing. Four tenants make twelve ordered pairs, and each deed takes one pair, so the index is compatible with 1212 histories. The programmer prunes with rules and ends by suspecting his client.

The story suits a study of generative method for three reasons. The space is exactly countable, so any generator can be checked against a count. The units are small and uniform, one deed by one pair, so the same unit can be animated, simulated and priced. And the story already contains its own critique: the data comes from the party who profits from one reading of it.

2 · Method

The swarm library

SCION is a set of 54 prompts in five families: artists and directors, research pioneers, studios and software, algorithms and techniques, archives and provenance. Each prompt turns one lineage, such as Lotte Reiniger's shadow theatre or as-rigid-as-possible deformation, into a working studio with a proof to make and a critic to answer to. All 54 share eight numbered sections and fifteen points of doctrine. Five of the fifteen matter most for this account: build the hard proof before scaling; keep canonical, versioned state; the builder cannot approve its own important work; repair by root cause; and label evidence honestly. repo

The compiler

The SCION system instruction compiler, version 2.0, generalises the library. It takes any source, a prompt, a workflow, a story, and emits one standalone instruction in nine sections. Its five compile steps are the frame for the process below. author

Compiler stepWhat it asksWhat it became in this project
1 · Recover the workThe domain's own unit of change and its invariantsOne deed by one ordered pair. Invariants: a doer and a different victim; nobody dead acts; readable acting with nothing shown for the four sexual deeds.
2 · Invert the substrateAgents work on the smallest representation that can be built, inspected and repairedA film is a twelve-digit base-12 number. Every frame is a pure function of that number and the time, drawn in the browser from code.
3 · Define the barThe hardest case that could disprove the method, with a verifierAny deed must be able to follow any other and read as one film. Counts must match brute force on small cases.
4 · Fit the operating modelWho owns intent, acceptance and interventionThe author owns intent and the merge to the public site. Fresh agents act as critics. The host enforces the merge gate.
5 · Extract the wedgeThe smallest reusable primitive, proved in one complete caseThe deed module: one file per deed, playable between any two tenants. Proved on one sack before four.

6 · Findings

The builders' checks were kinder than the picture

In PAE-03 each film measured itself, and each film's self-measure passed rows that a fresh critic failed: a head-low check passed a rearing sack, a catch check passed a penny that was carried. Five failures recurred across six unrelated materials: the cause arrived too late, the penny was tossed instead of dropped, the brace retreated, the sack went top-heavy, and the checks tested something other than what the critic saw. Because the materials differed and the failures did not, the failures belong to the task and to self-checking, and the repair was to rewrite each check to test what the critic looked at. page

One fixed story is a test bench for prompts

Holding the story and its ten choices still while changing the operation separates what a prompt contributes from what the story contributes. PAE-04 reuses the design at scale: twelve operations, one cast, one house.

Count first, then generate

An exact count is a verifier that does not share the generator's blind spots. The five-rule count was reproduced in this session by a second counter with a different decomposition, and By What Light checks its dynamic-programming count against brute force on the first three deeds. A million sampled stories could not have shown either.

The limiting queue moved to presentation

The compiler asks where a system saturates when candidate volume rises a hundredfold. Here it was neither animation nor counting. By 10 November the project held three films, five live tools and three classroom films on two sites, in four palettes, with three different rule sets all called "the rules." The author's report that it was hard to present is the saturation signal the compiler predicts, and the case-file rebuild of that afternoon is the repair.

A gate held when the author asked for it to open

Asked to push the new pages to the live site, the drafting agent attempted to merge its own pull request and the host refused it as a merge without review. The agent did not try another route. This is the compiler's authority rule observed once in practice: the builder does not accept its own consequential work, and the control sits with the host, not in the prompt. session

7 · Failures and corrections

  • The first payout engine used a rule set of its own, the letter W for the widow, and a fourth colour palette. It was rebuilt on the film's five rules, the letter R and the project's read register. session
  • The pull request and the author's case-file rebuild edited the same page on the same afternoon. The conflict was resolved by keeping the author's version. session
  • The narrator's printed total is reported two ways: 874,296,672,256 on the numbers page, citing Wikipedia, and 8,874,296,672,256 in an English text read online. Neither is 1212. The printed translation has not been checked. unknown
  • Believability scores come from the film bank's own rule tables. No reader has rated a film. page
  • The two new pages were checked by running their code, and were not viewed in a browser before this draft. session

8 · What a publication still needs

From the author
  • The earlier sessions. The commits of 30 October to 6 November name one agent session. Its transcript holds the critic verdicts, the retries and the prompts actually run. This draft could not read it, so sections 3 and 6 lean on commit messages and page text.
  • The compiled instruction for this story. The compiler's output for the Abominable House, as it was run, belongs in an appendix.
  • Dates and making of the three Lab 7 films, which live in a second repository this draft did not open.
  • Cost. Hours, sessions and compute for each stage. The compiler treats a tenfold to hundredfold compaction as a hypothesis until measured against a baseline that includes review and rework.
New work
  • Readers. A small viewing study: do people rate the most likely film as more believable than a random one, and can they tell what a deed was without the chapter card?
  • An ablation. The same deed animated by one operation for all twelve, against twelve operations, judged by the same critic.
  • The ending. A closing card on each bank film that names the arsonist prior and the four payouts for that film.
  • Rights. The narration paraphrases Calvino in the Parks translation. A publication should confirm what may be quoted and how the story is credited.
  • A statement on AI use in the form the venue requires, naming which parts agents built, which they criticised and which the author decided.

Candidate homes, to be checked against their current calls: the Electronic Literature Organization conference, the International Conference on Computational Creativity, ACM Creativity and Cognition, and Leonardo. The first two fit the Oulipo and generator material; the third fits the process account; the fourth fits a shorter artist's statement with the films.

3 domain_primitives
  Event: date, actor (author, builder, critic, host), action, artifact
  revision, evidence pointer. Claim: sentence, label (repo, page, author,
  session, unknown), event ids. Invariant: no claim without an event; no
  event without a pointer.
4 quality_contract
  Hard gates: every number recomputed or labelled page; every quotation under
  fifteen words with its source; every date from a commit or transcript.
  Falsifier: a reader with the repositories cannot find the evidence for a
  claim within two minutes.
5 execution_and_state
  Build the event table first as one JSON file. Write prose only from the
  table. One agent per source (commits, transcripts, pages), no shared edits.
6 verification_and_repair
  A fresh agent receives the paper and the repositories, without the event
  table, and tries to break ten claims chosen at random. Record PASS, FAIL
  or INCONCLUSIVE for each. Repair the table, then the prose. Two rounds.
7 failure_and_recovery
  If a transcript is unreadable, mark its events unknown and continue. If a
  recount disagrees with a page, stop that section and report both figures.
8 delivery_and_stop
  Deliver the event table, the revised paper and the verifier's record in a
  pull request. Stop on the author's acceptance or after two repair rounds.
9 start_now
  List the commits touching paeleo/flour-tower and paeleo/abominable-house,
  oldest first, and open the session each one names.
