How the entries are kept

  1. One entry records one thing: a file, a number, a frame, a message.
  2. Every entry shows its exhibit: the lines of code, the image, the table or the diagram it was read from, with the file path.
  3. The entry says what is there. Where it goes beyond what was opened, a box says what was not checked.
  4. Entries stand in order of occurrence.
