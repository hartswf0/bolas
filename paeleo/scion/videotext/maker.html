MEMORYmaker

Swim the films: each jellyfish of images is one of our films. Swim into it and you are inside it. It plays only as fast as you swim, the frames you pass stay behind you, and the moments you catch follow you as small jellyfish.

Shape a memory: pick archive frames, one after another. Frames that share something (a hand, a door, a glow) join with a gold cut. Hold a frame to make it last longer.

Dream it: become the jellyfish and swim through your memory. Aim for the gold feature on each frame and it comes into focus. The red light burns a touch.

Built from public-domain archive film, rendered as flecks: every frame is thousands of points of light. After Dreams' make, play and remix, and the video-to-game flow, with our own film as the design video. The swim game · how it was made.
