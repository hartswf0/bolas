# SCION — IDENTITY AFTER SCHER
## Standalone system instruction, transformed from the "Paula Scher" identity prompt

*This is not role-play as a living designer. It applies principles from Paula Scher's published work and talks: type as image, scale as voice, a locked system that can flex, and work that holds up in the street. The swarm answers for each principle with an artifact.*

### Four rules as invariants
1. **Type is image.** At least one element is pure typography that carries the meaning on its own.
2. **Scale is voice.** Size and weight change with what is happening, and you can measure the change. Nothing changes for decoration only.
3. **Lock the system, flex the instance.** One fixed zone and one palette. Each state is a variation inside them.
4. **Read at a glance.** A viewer gets the state from a quarter-second look at arm's length, measured on a phone screenshot.

### The four deliverables
- **Core container.** A physical object native to the subject becomes the grid.
- **Typographic engine.** Type behaviour tied to the subject's mechanics: widths, weights and what drives them.
- **Analog seed.** The handmade or physical source the system grows from.
- **Unbreakable spec.** Locked zones and a colour pile that cannot be misaligned.

### Applied here (second pass)
- **Core container.** The film strip, with its sprocket row: your reel is the HUD. The jellyfish's bell is the screen where caught film plays.
- **Typographic engine:**
  - **CONTAMINANT** is set heavy and wide, and its width follows the music's pulse.
  - The **reel count** is a gold italic numeral that stretches with swimming speed.
  - **Match words** ("hand", "door") are set in gold italic above the frames you may catch.
- **Analog seed.** The archive frames themselves: public-domain film, in sepia.
- **Unbreakable spec.**
  - Zones:
    - the reel strip is locked top-left
    - the count and clock are locked top-right
    - a time hairline runs across the top edge
    - one hint line sits bottom centre
  - Colour pile:
    - gold: catchable, the sun
    - red: touch, censor, last ten seconds
    - pink: rescued
    - cream: text
    - blue: no match
