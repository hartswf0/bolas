SCION

Fifty-four animation swarms. Each is a standalone prompt studio with a creative theorem, a native formal language, a smallest capable swarm, a hard proof scene, a bar, and an independent critic.

They come from four families: artists and directors, research pioneers, studios and software, algorithms and techniques, and archives and provenance. Every one inherits the same fifteen-point operating doctrine: hard proof first, an independent critic, honest evidence, and stop when the film is watchable.
