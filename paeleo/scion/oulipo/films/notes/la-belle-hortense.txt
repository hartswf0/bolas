La belle Hortense, a sextine exercise in hommage to Roubaud's novel; nothing is taken from the novel or its cover. Foraged: FSA/OWI photographs by Jack Delano and Carol M. Highsmith's bench (Library of Congress, public domain), an 1861 envelope (LoC), Daniel Jolivet's Bourré station plates (Commons, CC BY 2.0), a CC0 key, a public-domain bell photograph, and Commons recordings of a station bell, steps, door, lock, keys and paper (PD, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA). Handmade: the cut-out rigs, stencil dye, pan exposures, receipt, hook, pencilled key outline and the Hortense Slab typeface. Declared: the fresh reader was a separate Claude session shown stills, not a human viewer with sound.
 All foraged works are modified: sounds are cut and re-timed; photographs are cut out, stencil-dyed and pan-exposed. Titles and source links for each are in the credits below. Review status: the rights reviewer failed it only because the film's end card does not say the works were modified or give titles and links; this note and the credits now do. The critic failed it: in the defining beat the coat is a dark oxblood rather than the cover's scarlet, and the runner reads as a stiff puppet walking, not sprinting.
