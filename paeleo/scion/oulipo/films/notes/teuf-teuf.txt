An homage after Jean Jabely's Teuf-Teuf (1956): no material from that film is used and the captions are original, not Queneau's text. Foraged from Wikimedia Commons (licences read from extmetadata): the wall is "Cueva de las manos - detalle" (Suzko, CC BY-SA 3.0), flattened to a cream wash, its real hands printed as pale stencils; the wheels are a GrabCut cutout of the Ljubljana Marshes wheel (Petar Milošević, CC BY-SA 4.0). Sounds: PDSounds knocks and air (public domain), Pwaivers steps (CC BY-SA 3.0), Gravity Sound gravel (CC BY 4.0). Handmade: puppets, stone, logs, cart, scratched animals, ghosts, title; scrape, rumble and grind synthesized. Staged compression, not chronology.
 Review status: both the independent critic and the rights reviewer passed it. The critic noted that the stone's dashed surface reads as procedural rather than brushed. The rights reviewer noted that the on-screen date 1956 is sourced but could not be re-checked.
