Foraged: Wilhelm Busch's Max und Moritz plates (1865, public domain, Wikimedia Commons) give the miller, porter and boys, cut out as jointed puppets. The paper is James Puckett's paper scan (CC BY 2.0) and the table is a CC0 wood scan. Paper ticks, thud, switch click (public domain) and the station bell (Work With Sounds, CC BY 4.0) come from Commons; room tone comes from Wreckless (1935) via movingimagearchive.com. Handmade: pen lettering, balloons, the key, the porter's forearm, the door, fold marks, the light box, the fold geometry and every stop-motion exposure. The lamp hum is synthesised. No key jingle was found.
