One factory break played twice on one clock. Foraged: every figure and the room are Library of Congress FSA/OWI photographs (Russell Lee's time-clock wall and tool-box pile, Marjory Collins' young woman with an apple, Arthur Siegel's Renzo and foreman, Jack Delano's doorway and the foreman's back), cut out and articulated on pins. Sound is Commons recordings (bells, steps, ticks, paper, wood) plus Valley Town (1940) room tone. Handmade: paper clock hands, scissor-cut bites, the CAPOREPARTO card, the slotted-strip slate. Declared: the bite is pistachio husks, the punch is a cupboard and a knock, two different men play the foreman, and Renzo has no arm.
