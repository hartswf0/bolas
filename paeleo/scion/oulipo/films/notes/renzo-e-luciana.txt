One factory lunch break played twice on one clock, staged with cut-outs. Foraged: every figure and the room are Library of Congress FSA/OWI photographs: Russell Lee's time-clock wall and tool-box pile, Marjory Collins' young woman with an apple, Arthur Siegel's seated worker (Renzo) and, from another Siegel negative, a hard-hatted worker cast as the foreman; a third Siegel photograph gives his back, wearing the front's hat and legs. Jack Delano's doorway has its technician retouched out. Sounds are Wikimedia Commons recordings. Handmade: paper clock hands, scissor-cut bites, jaw substitutions, the CAPOREPARTO card, the schedule-strip slate. Declared: the bite is pistachio husks, the punch a cupboard and knock.
 Review status: the rights reviewer passed it. After two repair rounds the independent critic still failed it on three points. The late bell in version B is explained by the slate rather than staged. The couple at the meeting read as cut-outs from two negatives. The foreman's front and back photographs show two different men.
