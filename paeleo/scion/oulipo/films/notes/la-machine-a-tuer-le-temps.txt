Eight short drawn clips (clock, hand with parcel, second hand, service door, glance, dropped receipt, person exiting, empty threshold) are rendered once, hashed and frozen as a pool. Two 20-second cuts are built only from explicit recipes (asset, in/out frame, output frames, direction). Cut A plays in plain order and reads as an ordinary handoff. Cut B opens on a long glance, puts the door handle before the parcel, plays the glance in reverse and holds on the receipt, so the parcel seems illicit. Screening 1 plays both cuts side by side over one shared bed. Screening 2 gives each cut its own synthesized mix. A closing report lists the recipes and the inventory check. There are no voices, only a murmur.
