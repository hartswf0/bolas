Eight short drawn clips (clock, hand with parcel, second hand, service door, glance, dropped receipt, person exiting, empty threshold) are rendered once, hashed and frozen as a pool. Two 20-second cuts are built only from explicit recipes (asset, in/out frame, output frames, direction). Cut A, in plain order, reads as an ordinary handoff. Cut B opens on a long glance, puts the door handle first, reverses the glance and holds the receipt, so the parcel seems illicit. Screening 1 plays both side by side over one shared bed, and screening 2 gives each its own mix. All sound is synthesized. There are no voices, only a murmur.
