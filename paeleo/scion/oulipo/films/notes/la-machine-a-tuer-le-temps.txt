A drawn red spiral notebook, animated frame by frame, holds eight photographs Jack Delano took at Chicago's Union Station in 1943, foraged from the Library of Congress (FSA/OWI, no known restrictions) and frozen as one hashed pool. Cut A plays them in pool order; the prints are lifted, re-slotted and re-numbered in red pencil, and cut B plays the same prints reordered and retimed. Room air (Wreckless, 1935) and a whistle (Valley Town, 1940) are public-domain Prelinger audio via movingimagearchive.com. Captions, story B, the station crowd, tick and foley are invented or synthesised; no station recording could be fetched, and no fresh viewer has screened it.
