Two optical discs and two text discs in strict alternation (A · 1 · B · 2), then a still pass. Optical A carries the bench key and pushes toward the eye; optical B carries the closed service door and sinks away, both at a fixed 0.5 rev/s. Text disc 2 turns at 0.09 rev/s, the chosen reading speed. Sound: a click and low pulse cue each switch, a tick marks every half turn, and two bells from the station seed. Puns, discs and sound were all written and drawn in code.
