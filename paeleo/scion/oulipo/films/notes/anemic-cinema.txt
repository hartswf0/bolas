Shot as a monochrome 1920s print, following the two reference frames. The text discs are lit grey plates with bold white capitals; the optical discs are warm grey grooved rings on black. They alternate strictly (A · 1 · B · 2), then a still pass shows three frames of the reading disc on a strip. Optical A pushes toward the eye and B sinks away, both at a fixed 0.5 rev/s. Text disc 2 turns at 0.09 rev/s, the chosen reading speed. Each pun is a chiasmus about the key, bell and door. Sound: a click and low pulse cue each switch, ticks mark half turns, two bells from the station seed. All drawn and synthesized in code.
