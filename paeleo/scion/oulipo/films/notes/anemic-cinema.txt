Handmade in code: the engraved ring discs, the title, and two original French chiasmus puns ("la porte tourne sur le gond", "la cloche du quai sonne la clef"), set as spirals cut from the same groove stock. One plate turns throughout; letters are redrawn frame by frame into rings, then into keys. Foraged from Wikimedia Commons: a skeleton key photograph by Noy-nac (CC BY-SA 4.0) and "Llave bronce" by Jorge Barrios (public domain), both cut out with GrabCut; one bell strike, "Striking a bell 15cm large" by stephan via pdsounds.org (public domain), only trimmed and levelled. The bell is a declared contemporary intervention; the 1926 film was silent. Otherwise silent.
