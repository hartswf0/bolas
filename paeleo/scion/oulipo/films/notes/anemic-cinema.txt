Handmade in code: the engraved ring discs, the title and two original French chiasmus puns in Liberation Sans Bold (SIL OFL 1.1). One plate turns through all four discs and the switches; title and still pass are static. Letters become rings, then keys, frame by frame. Key imagery adapted (GrabCut cutout, recoloured, arranged on rings) from "Skeleton key (Yale & Towne)" by Noy-nac, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skeleton_key_(Yale_%26_Towne).png; the adapted key imagery is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Also cut out: "Llave bronce", Jorge Barrios (Commons, public domain). Bell: "Striking a bell 15cm large", stephan via pdsounds.org (Commons, public domain), only trimmed and levelled, a declared contemporary intervention. Otherwise silent.
