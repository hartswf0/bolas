Handmade in code: the engraved ring discs, the title and two original French chiasmus puns in Liberation Sans Bold (SIL OFL 1.1). One plate turns through all four discs and the switches; title and still pass are static. Letters become rings, then keys, frame by frame. Every key is adapted (GrabCut cutout, recoloured, mirrored, arranged on rings) from "Skeleton key (Yale & Towne)" by Noy-nac, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Skeleton_key_(Yale_%26_Towne).png; the adapted key imagery is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Bell: "Striking a bell 15cm large", stephan via pdsounds.org (Commons, public domain), trimmed, faded and levelled, a declared contemporary intervention. Otherwise silent.
 Review status: both the independent critic and the rights reviewer passed it. The critic noted that the depth of the two optical discs rests partly on static cues, and that the keys read as cream silhouettes rather than showing the photograph's patina.
