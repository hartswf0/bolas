Handmade: one brush-ink plate of a long-snouted creature on a park bench at night holding a sealed envelope, built from a recorded 41-stroke ledger and brushed on in the prologue; a five-drawing eye/breath library used only before the plate is frozen; seven balloons and all page lettering written stroke by stroke in a single-stroke pen hand. The art raster is sha256-identical in all eight panels and every film hold. Foraged from Wikimedia Commons: a public-domain ink-pen recording (stephan, pdsounds.org), cut into one grain per stroke, and a CC BY-SA 4.0 night cricket chorus (DrTrumpet). Nothing synthesized; no speech; no typeset font.
