One plate, drawn once in heavy black and white: the visitor on the bench against a black wall, holding a sealed envelope. It is repeated eight times; the script checks bytes, crop and frame match. Only the balloon layer changes, and it fades in on its own plane as the camera reaches each panel. In strip 1 she talks herself into waiting for the bell, and its third panel is wordless, backed only by room tone. In strip 2 an offscreen questioner turns the same pose into an alibi: "Look. The envelope is still sealed." Both strips are then held side by side. Voices are captions over synthesized murmurs.
