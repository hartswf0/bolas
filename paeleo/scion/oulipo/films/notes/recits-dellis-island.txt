Two 25-second movements around a fictional closed halt, Saint-Ory. The first shows only what the site can show: a pan along the empty platform, a painted notice reading "last service 30 IX 1979" (when it was painted is marked unknown), the empty bench and an undated station diagram, with wind and no voices. The second gives two staged, fictional witnesses, each labelled with name, segment timecode and "staged testimony". They agree on the seed: envelope, key on the bench, door shut. They disagree on whether the bell rang before or after the key was put down. The bench is shown today and does not prove either account. A ledger ends the film with both orders marked unresolved. Voices are captions over a murmur.
