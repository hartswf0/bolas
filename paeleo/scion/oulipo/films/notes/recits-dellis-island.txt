Two 25-second movements at a fictional closed halt. The first shows only what the site shows: the empty platform and a painted "last service 30 IX 1979" notice (painting date marked unknown). It also shows the bench and an undated diagram, with wind and no voices. In the second, two staged, fictional witnesses are labelled with name, segment timecode and "staged testimony". They agree on the seed (envelope, key on the bench, door shut). They disagree on whether the bell rang before or after the key was put down. A closing ledger shows both orders as unresolved. Voices are captions over a murmur.
