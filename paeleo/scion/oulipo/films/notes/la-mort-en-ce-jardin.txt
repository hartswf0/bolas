Three handmade grey card puppets, with split pins, pencilled faces, charcoal mud and sweat glints, cross a flood channel on a card plank that holds one person. The jungle is foraged: Commons photographs of buttress roots, lianas, ferns, leaves and Mekong flood water, cut with GrabCut into five parallax planes. Card fibre comes from a CC0 Commons cardboard photograph (Humusak). Ripples come from Shock Troops of Disaster (1938, movingimagearchive.com). The wedding shoe is a public-domain LACMA photograph. The plank, splash, ledger handwriting and cards are handmade. Jungle, stream, splash and lap recordings are foraged from Commons. Steps, creaks, tap, rustle, liana and pencil sounds are synthesized.
