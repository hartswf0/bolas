Three people must cross a flooded underpass with one dry bag and a plank that holds one person. An immaculate wedding shoe, with the brass key inside, drifts into reach. In Version A it lands at 9.5 s, Lio lifts it, and Mara takes the bag across before him. Version B runs the identical drift 5 s earlier, so Ezra lifts it and the order becomes Lio, Mara, Ezra. A panel tracks plank occupancy, the bag, the shoe and the key on every frame, and the script asserts that the plank never holds two. The only impossible thing is declared: the shoe stays dry. Voices are captioned murmurs.
