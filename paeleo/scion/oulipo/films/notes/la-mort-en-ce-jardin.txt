Three handmade grey card puppets cross a flood channel on a plank that holds one person. They have split pins, pencilled faces, charcoal mud and wet hair strands,. The jungle is foraged: Commons photographs of buttress roots, lianas, ferns, leaves, forest floor and Mekong flood water, cut out with GrabCut into five parallax planes. Water ripples come from Shock Troops of Disaster (1938, movingimagearchive.com). The wedding shoe is a public-domain LACMA photograph. Picking it up costs a hand, so the bag changes hands. Plank, ledger handwriting and cards are handmade. The stream sound is foraged (CC0). Insects, creaks, tap, splash and pencil are synthesized stand-ins.
