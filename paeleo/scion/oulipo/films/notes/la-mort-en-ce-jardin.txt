Three handmade grey card puppets, with split pins, pencilled faces, charcoal mud and sweat glints, cross a flood channel on a plank that holds one person. The jungle is foraged: Commons photographs of buttress roots, lianas, ferns, leaves and Mekong flood water, cut with GrabCut into five parallax planes. Ripples come from Shock Troops of Disaster (1938, movingimagearchive.com). The wedding shoe is a public-domain LACMA photograph. Plank, splash, ledger handwriting and cards are handmade. The stream sound is foraged (CC0). Insects, wood and mud steps, plank creaks, the shoe tap, the bag rustle, water lap, splash, liana and pencil are synthesized stand-ins.
