One 30-second picture of a paper cité, played twice with two soundtracks. Fiction: model and names invented. Handmade: the cut-paper model (blocks, curved zinc roofs, dormers, hedge, road, lamp), the articulated walker and the window-16 silhouette, cut in code from foraged paper scans (Library of Congress blank sheet; Commons kraft and vintage paper); also room tone, wind, hums, drone, squelches. Foraged from Wikimedia Commons: door, latch, tap, kettle, footstep, car, a 78, relay click, typewriter, shutter, hum, wind, buzzer. Foraged speech: one indistinct radio voice in B (Library of Congress, 1917). Missing: Lingua Libre words (rate-limited); viewer readings not run.
