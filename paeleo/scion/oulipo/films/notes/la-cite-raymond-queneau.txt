One 30-second picture of a paper cité, played twice with two soundtracks. Fiction: the model, names and log entries are invented. Handmade: the cut-paper model (blocks, curved zinc roofs, dormers, hedge, road, lamp), the articulated walker and the window-16 silhouette, cut in code from foraged paper scans (Library of Congress Federal Theatre Project sheet; Commons kraft and vintage paper). Also handmade: room tone, wind, bag rustle, music box, hums, drone, radio squelch. Foraged from Wikimedia Commons: door, latch, tap, kettle, footstep, car, an orchestral 78, relay click, typewriter, shutter, mains hum, wind, buzzer. Missing: real French speech (Commons downloads rate-limited): no voice is heard, so the passes carry no captions.
