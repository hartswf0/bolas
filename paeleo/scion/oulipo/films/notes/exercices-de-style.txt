One station incident, six facts, told five ways on one handmade book page. Foraged: Agence Rol's 1920 photograph of a walking man (Wikimedia Commons, public domain), cut out and jointed as the visitor; the shadow puppet is rotoscoped from his matte. Edna C. Rex's painted key (National Gallery of Art, CC0). Via movingimagearchive.com, Prelinger and archive.org public domain: a 1927 Berlin platform, a 1947 bell strike and belfry frame, 1935 room tone. Commons: a gravel footstep (Gravity Sound, CC BY 4.0) and a thud (public domain). Handmade: page, type, bench, door, guichet, notice, stamp, envelope, tear, arrows, matrix. Declared synth: typing clicks, key tick, tear, pen. Missing: a bowler hat.
