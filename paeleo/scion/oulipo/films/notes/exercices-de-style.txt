One station incident, six facts, told five ways on one handmade book page. Foraged: Agence Rol's 1920 photograph of a walking man (Wikimedia Commons, public domain), cut out and jointed as the visitor; the shadow puppet is rotoscoped from his matte. Edna C. Rex's painted key (National Gallery of Art, CC0). Via movingimagearchive.com (Prelinger, public domain): a 1927 Berlin platform, a 1947 bell and belfry frame, 1935 room tone. Commons: a footstep (CC BY 4.0) and a thud (public domain). Real people and places play invented roles. Handmade: page, type, bench, door, guichet, notice, envelope, tear, matrix. Declared synth: clicks, tick, tear, pen.
 The visitor's bowler is grafted from a second Agence Rol print (1917, public domain), now credited.
