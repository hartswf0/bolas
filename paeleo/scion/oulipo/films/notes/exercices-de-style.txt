A typographic film. A frozen six-beat ledger of the station incident comes first, then four retellings, each moving by its own rule. The reverse log types newest-first against a backwards clock, then draws cause-to-effect arrows that still point forward. The sensory inventory gives only what a clerk behind frosted glass could sense, and it declares that the key was only a glint. The notice shows its empty boxes before typing facts into them. The key narrates in darkness until the envelope tears. A closing matrix checks every telling against the six beats. There are no voices, and the sound is synthesized.
