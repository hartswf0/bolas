After Bang Bang (Jean Jabely and Raymond Queneau, c. 1958 dossier); no part of it is reproduced; the slips are original. Foraged: the glider and inventor puppets are cut from a Popular Science Monthly engraving (1894-95, public domain); the helicopter from Penaud's flying models plate (1875, public domain); the magpie from Bowen's lithograph after Audubon (octavo printings 1840-1871, public domain by age). Sound: Oona Raisanen's public-domain magpie recording and US Fish and Wildlife Service field air. Handmade: sheets, panels, paper wing, pins, slips, the joined pen hand, impact sounds. Declared: the inventor, the paper wing and all three flights are fiction; the helicopter and glider are real machines.
 Review status: both the independent critic and the rights reviewer passed it. The critic noted that the bird's hand still reads somewhat like one repeated script. The rights reviewer noted that the magpie lithograph's public-domain status is reasoned from its printing dates, not read from its own record.
