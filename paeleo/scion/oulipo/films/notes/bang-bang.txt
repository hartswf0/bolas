A 30-second pencil animatic in ten boards. Each board is labelled with its number, time range, shot and FX cue, and a timing strip runs along the bottom. A pigeon on the station gutter watches a fictional inventor try paper wings, then a rubber-band rotor that lifts him a centimetre before snapping, then a glide. The glide fails across five boards: run-up, launch, nose up at 35° (where the failure first reads), stall, fall. The pigeon's calm lines are captions over a coo-like murmur. It treats each attempt as sensible bird behaviour and says only what it could see. All FX are synthesized.
