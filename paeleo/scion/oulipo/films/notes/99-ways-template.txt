A photo-comic page performed. Foraged: Arthur Rothstein's 1937 FSA photographs of the Randolph, Vermont station agent and a Vermont tavern clock, Esther Bubley's 1943 OWI station front and visitor, a 1922 Red Cross key, and a blank 1934 Federal Theatre Project sheet as paper, all from the Library of Congress. Sound comes from the Moving Image Archive (1914 room tone) and Wikimedia Commons (clock, door, chair, steps, thud, a CC BY train recording). Handmade: the GrabCut cutouts and their hinged rig, borders, balloons, pen lettering, memo, SERVICE card, hook and tag, and the ledger card. Declared: a synthesized counter bell and paper sound; voice is balloons only.
