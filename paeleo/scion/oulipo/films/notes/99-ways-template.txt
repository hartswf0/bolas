The neutral baseline, set in the station office and drawn in plain black-and-white line with mixed-case lettering. In six fixed, objective panels, the clerk leaves the desk with a note about the service key, hears "What time is it?" from the platform, answers "Ten past four!", opens the key cupboard and forgets why. The brass key stays on its hook. After a reading and a full-page hold come a two-panel compressed retelling and a three-panel version from the porter's bench, where E1 is out of view. An event ledger (E1 to E5) closes the film. A clock tick, footsteps and murmurs stand in for sound and voice.
