A photo-comic page, then performed. Foraged from the Library of Congress: Arthur Rothstein's 1937 FSA photographs of the Randolph, Vermont station agent and a Vermont tavern clock, Esther Bubley's 1943 OWI station front and visitor, a 1922 Red Cross key, and a blank 1934 Federal Theatre Project sheet as paper. Sound: Moving Image Archive room tone (1914), Wikimedia Commons public-domain foley, and one CC BY train recording by Glaneur de sons, modified. Handmade: pieces cut by hand from the photographs, the hinged rig, plate repairs, borders, balloons, lettering, dialogue, memo, cards, hook, tag, ledger. Declared: synthesized counter bell and paper sound; no voices.
