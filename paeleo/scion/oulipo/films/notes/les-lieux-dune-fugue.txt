A 57-second cut-paper film. Foraged: public-domain Library of Congress photographs by Carol M. Highsmith (a Columbus courthouse doorway, a Savannah riveted door, a Sacramento park bench), a 1934 Federal Theatre Project blank sheet scanned at the Library of Congress for the paper stock, a CC BY-SA Commons photograph of rue du Soleil for the junction, and a CC BY-SA recording of the Murbach abbey bells. Handmade: the scissor-cut paper stage and its lamp, the hinged door leaves and their fall, the route strip, the record card, and the fictional text. Room tone, creaks and thuds are synthesized. From 0:44 the sound is declared digital silence.
