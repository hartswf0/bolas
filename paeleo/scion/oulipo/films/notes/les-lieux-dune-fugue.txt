A 57-second cut-paper film; the story is fiction, and the first slate says so ("récit fictif"). Foraged: public-domain Library of Congress photographs by Carol M. Highsmith (a Columbus courthouse doorway, a Savannah riveted door, a Sacramento park bench), a 1934 Federal Theatre Project blank sheet as paper stock, Polymagou's CC BY-SA 4.0 Commons photograph of rue du Soleil, Paris, and Amada44's CC BY-SA 3.0 recording of the Murbach bells. Handmade: the scissor-cut paper stage, its two lamps, the hinged door animation, the route strip, the record card and the text. Room tone, creaks and thuds are synthesized; from 0:44 the sound is digital silence. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0.
