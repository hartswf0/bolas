A girl wants the tram because the métro is on strike. A jump cut moves her about two kilometres while she laughs. Her word "Doukipudonktan", cut as fourteen paper teeth, falls out whole and becomes the two-row queue that blocks her, then the stairs onto the tram's top deck. Foraged: four Library of Congress Photochrom plates of Paris, c.1890-1900 (public domain) (the tram is cut from one), Commons paper scans (PD/CC0) filling every piece, and a CC BY-SA wind recording. Handmade: the jointed puppet, letter tiles, signs, plaques and captions; the strike sign is staged on period photographs. Bells, steps, clicks and the tunnel rumble are synthesized.
