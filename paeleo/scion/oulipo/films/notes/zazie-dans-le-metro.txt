A 22-second cut-paper chase. A girl wants the tram because the métro is on strike. A jump cut moves her two kilometres while she laughs. Her word "Doukipudonktan", cut as paper teeth, falls out and becomes the queue that blocks her, then the stairs onto the tram's top deck. Foraged: four Library of Congress Photochrom plates of Paris (public domain), the tram cut from one of them, Commons paper scans filling every piece, a CC BY-SA wind recording and a Valley Town (1940) factory roar. Handmade: the jointed puppet, letter tiles, signs, plaques and captions. Bells, steps and clicks are synthesized; no street recording could be downloaded.
