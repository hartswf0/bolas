A 20-second chase for "the 4·10 to the sea". A girl in a red coat and beret, holding an envelope, points at the station sign and runs. It starts as a straight chase with a clean continuity cut. A jump cut then carries her two kilometres in one stride and lands her at the back of a queue. A match cut turns the people in the queue into the bars of a locked gate at the same positions. The envelope then becomes a ticket, and the gate opens. Then it becomes a paper dart. The letter catches the train and she doesn't, and the key that fell out stays on the bench by the shut service door. A negative control replays the jump without coat, beret or envelope. Sound is synthesized.
