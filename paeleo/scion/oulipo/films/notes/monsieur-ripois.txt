Foraged: John Vachon's 1942 photograph (Library of Congress 2017831564, public domain), cut into card pieces and articulated, with its window blinds as the shadow play's far plane, plus her gloved hand and coat sleeve from the sister frame 2017831565. Sounds are eight Wikimedia Commons recordings (public domain or CC0, one CC BY-SA): clock, old door, door handle, hinge, cupboard door (CC BY-SA 3.0, Secretlondon), knocks, paper, chair creak. Handmade: the engraved silver box, the pawn receipt, and the jointed shadow puppets, chair, lamp and door. Declared on screen: the 1942 sitters play fictional roles; box, receipt and silhouettes are props. No voice (captions only), no music; nothing from Clement's film.
