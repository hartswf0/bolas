A prologue leaves the brass key on the station bench, kept as a separate record. A reverse tour then follows sand-cast yellow brass backwards: finished key, opened sand mould, molten brass, then copper and zinc ingots. In the mould the casting sinks back into its hollow, and the pour climbs back into the crucible. Shot direction is labelled apart from process order. Four original French alexandrines carry glosses and a marked 6+6 break. They are sung as a wordless chant of twelve syllable pulses with a pause at the caesura. Cut 1 falls in a caesura, cut 2 against one ("lai|ton"), cut 3 on a line break.
