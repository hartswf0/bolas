An original six-line poem about an unknown "mirlocca" is released in three stages. Sound comes first: a murmured, wordless reading, plus the rustle, the bell and a small metal click it names. Two fictional readers guess (a bird with a bell; a station ticket). Then the unchanged text appears, and they guess again (a station cat; a letter or a lock). Then an image shows the bench, envelope and brass key. Ondine reconsiders: the mirlocca is the key. Bastien keeps an alternate. A ledger keeps cue and response order. Voices are captions with murmurs; no speech was synthesized.
