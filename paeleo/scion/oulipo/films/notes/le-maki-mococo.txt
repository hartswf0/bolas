A cut-paper mouth in profile reads one fixed poem: as sound alone, while its words type, and after an image arrives. Foraged from Wikimedia Commons: the voice, 14 single-word Lingua Libre recordings (16 words) by Grendelkhan (CC0) and Vealhurl ("binturong", "popcorn", CC BY-SA 4.0), trimmed and re-ordered with two declared /z/ splices; the soundtrack is CC BY-SA 4.0. The binturong is F. Specht's public-domain engraving from Brehm (1895), cut out and re-hung by its tail by us; in the original it stands. Handmade: the layered paper mouth, a printed card gong, cord, type, pencil. Room tone and paper sounds are synthesised; nothing rings. Readers are fictional.
