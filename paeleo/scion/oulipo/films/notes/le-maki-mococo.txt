A cut-paper mouth in profile reads one fixed poem: as sound alone, while its words type themselves, and after an image arrives. Foraged: the voice is a collage of single-word Lingua Libre recordings from Wikimedia Commons (Grendelkhan, CC0; Vealhurl's "binturong", CC BY-SA 4.0). The binturong is the public-domain engraving by F. Specht from Brehm's Life of Animals (1895), cut out with hand-seeded GrabCut. Handmade: the articulated paper mouth and its pose library, the type, a red pencil stroke, and a bronze-card gong on its cord. Room tone and paper sounds are synthesised. Readers A and B are fictional. Line three is mouthed silently: its recordings could not be downloaded.
