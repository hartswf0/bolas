Foraged: an Agence Rol 1916 photograph of the new Madeleine-Bastille bus and two Agence Rol 1919 crowd photographs (BnF Gallica via Wikimedia Commons, public domain). The bus, wheels, passengers and waiting crowd are GrabCut cutouts from them; in the photographic condition their real texture refills the same carved outlines. Sound: a Commons bus-engine recording (CC BY 4.0), street noise (public domain), a bicycle bell (CC0). Nothing came from the Moving Image Archive cache. Handmade: carved facade, gouged pavement, platform, destination board, roof-light, articulated silhouette puppets on twos, cards. Declared: synthetic thud and clicks, the anachronistic engine, and a 1933 plate dropped for an unverifiable licence.
