Foraged: an Agence Rol 1916 photograph of the new Madeleine-Bastille bus and two Agence Rol 1919 crowd photographs (Gallica via Wikimedia Commons, public domain). The bus, its wheels, passengers and waiting crowd are cut out of them with GrabCut. Sound is a Commons bus-engine recording (CC BY 4.0), street noise (public domain) and a bicycle bell (CC0). Nothing came from the Moving Image Archive cache. Handmade: the carved facade, gouged pavement, platform, destination board, articulated silhouette puppets on twos, the overhead plan and the cards. Declared: a synthetic thud and clicks, the anachronistic engine, and the Bourse 1933 plate dropped for an unverifiable licence.
