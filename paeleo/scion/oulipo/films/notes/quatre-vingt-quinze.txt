Foraged: the paper is a blank sheet scanned by the Library of Congress (Federal Theatre Project, public domain). The brass key is Blenheimears' CC0 Commons photograph, density-cut. Cells A and H carry two-level stencils of Jack Delano's 1943 Union Station waiting room (FSA/OWI, public domain). Sounds come from Wikimedia Commons: the Fridingen station bell and concrete steps (CC BY-SA), key clinks (CC BY), and public-domain knocks and creaks. Handmade: the jointed cut-paper puppets with replacement heads and hands, the props, the pen lettering, the ruled page, the painted pools, the marks, the paper tear and the room tone. The Moving Image Archive bell was rejected because narration covers it.
