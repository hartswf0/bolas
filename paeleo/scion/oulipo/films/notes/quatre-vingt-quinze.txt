Foraged: the paper is a Library of Congress scan of a blank Federal Theatre Project sheet (public domain). The brass key is Blenheimears' CC0 Commons photograph, cut from a downscaled copy. Cells A and H carry two-level stencils of Jack Delano's 1943 Union Station waiting room (FSA/OWI, public domain). Commons sounds: the Fridingen station bell, whose ring is extended from its own grains, and concrete steps (CC BY-SA; the soundtrack is therefore CC BY-SA), key clinks (CC BY), public-domain knocks and creaks. Handmade: cut-paper puppets with replacement heads and hands, props, pen lettering, ruled page, painted pools, marks, hall reverb, tear, scrape, slaps, clicks, room tone. Archive bell rejected.
