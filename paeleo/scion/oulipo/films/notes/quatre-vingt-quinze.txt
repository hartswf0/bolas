A 3×3 grid of nine pen-and-ink cells, with red as the spot colour. The camera reads it twice. Across (A to I) it is a lost-key story: she misses her key, finds one that is not hers in an unstamped envelope, leaves it and goes. Down (A D G, B E H, C F I) the same cells follow the envelope, the bell and the key back to whoever is behind the service door. The centre cell, "So someone was watching", answers the note in one route and the bell in the other. Both routes end on cell I, where feet show under the shut door. Murmurs stand in for voices.
