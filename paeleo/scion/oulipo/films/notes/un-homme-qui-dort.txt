One 35-second picture is shown twice with the same five second-person captions. In pass I each line comes 1.2 s before the action it names; in pass II, 1.2 s after. Foraged: five FSA/OWI negatives from the Library of Congress (Esther Bubley 1943, the same man in the room shots; Russell Lee 1936; John Vachon 1940; Carl Mydans 1935) and three Wikimedia Commons recordings (crank telephone bell, wooden stairs, quiet room). Handmade: the GrabCut cutouts, warps and multiplane motion cut from those negatives, the print curves, the captions and the I/II cards. Declared: the climber is a different man; some wall behind moving figures is rebuilt. No voice recording exists.
