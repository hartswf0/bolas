One 35-second picture is screened twice. A man wakes, lets a telephone ring three times, sits in a held shot by the window, and goes down a staircase twice. Both flights are the same rendered clip, reused frame for frame. The camera stays high, across the room or outside the door, and never takes his view. The same seven second-person lines run in both passes. In track 1 each line comes 1.2 s before the action it names, in track 2 1.3 s after. A strip at the top marks action ticks against voice ticks. The held shot stays voice-free for at least five seconds and has only room tone. Voices are captions over a wordless synthesized murmur.
