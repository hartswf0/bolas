One 35-second picture is screened twice. A man wakes, lets a telephone ring, sits in a held shot, and goes down a staircase twice. Both flights are the same rendered clip, reused frame for frame. The camera stays across the room or outside the door and never takes his view. The same seven second-person lines run in both passes. In track 1 each line comes 1.2 s before its action, in track 2 1.3 s after. A strip at the top marks action against voice. The held shot stays voice-free for over five seconds with only room tone. Voices are captions over a wordless synthesized murmur.
