A book of ninety-nine pages, leafed through on a dark table. One frozen incident at a small station: a memo, a stranger asking the time, ten past four by the clock, thanks, the cupboard, a key left on its hook. Each page obeys one constraint and then performs its own key beat on twos before the leaf is rolled over and the next is uncovered. Foraged: FSA/OWI and Library of Congress photographs and engravings, Agence Rol press photographs, Daumier and Busch prints, Commons textures and a cave-rock photograph (CC BY, CC BY-SA, credited); sound from a public-domain paper recording and a 1914 print's room tone. Handmade: cutouts, puppets, stencils, discs, pen lettering, table, turns. The book PDF adds a title page and contents.
