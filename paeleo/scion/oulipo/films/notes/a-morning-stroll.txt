Foraged: a July 1959 Angelo Rizzuto stoop photograph; two Carol M. Highsmith photographs, a Philadelphia row house (photographed 2019, standing in for 2009) and a colour rooster (2019); an 1870s stereograph rooster; Esther Bubley's 1943 OWI silhouette (Library of Congress). From Wikimedia Commons: Plennert's sliding-door photograph (CC BY-SA 4.0, so this film is CC BY-SA 4.0) and a CC0 knock. Street, room and footstep sound comes from four Prelinger films (1936-1950s) via movingimagearchive.com. Handmade: the cut-outs, the joints and frame-by-frame animation, the shadows, holdouts, the chain, the sensor strip, the paint chips, the creak and the silhouette cards. Declared: no voice, no clucks, and no plate dated 2009.
