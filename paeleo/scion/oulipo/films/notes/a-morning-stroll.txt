Highsmith photographs (a Philadelphia row house photographed 2019, standing in for 2009, and a colour rooster), an 1870s stereograph rooster and Esther Bubley's 1943 silhouette. From Wikimedia Commons: Plennert's sliding-door photograph (CC BY-SA 4.0, so this film is CC BY-SA 4.0), a CC0 knock and a public-domain door knock. Handmade: the cut-outs, joints and frame-by-frame animation, shadows, holdouts, door chain, sensor strip, paint chips, creak, chime and silhouette cards. Declared: no voice, no clucks, no plate dated 2009.
