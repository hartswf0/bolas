Foraged from the Library of Congress: Angelo Rizzuto's 1959 stoop photograph, two Carol M. Highsmith photographs (a Philadelphia row house photographed 2019, standing in for 2009, and a colour rooster), an 1870s stereograph rooster and Esther Bubley's 1943 silhouette. From Wikimedia Commons: Plennert's sliding-door photograph (CC BY-SA 4.0, so this film is CC BY-SA 4.0), a CC0 knock and a public-domain door knock. Street, room, latch and footstep sound: four Prelinger films via movingimagearchive.com. Handmade: the cut-outs, joints and frame-by-frame animation, shadows, holdouts, door chain, sensor strip, paint chips, creak, chime and silhouette cards. Declared: no voice, no clucks, no plate dated 2009.
 Review status: both the independent critic and the rights reviewer failed it, each narrowly. The critic said that in 1959 the bird vanishes behind the balusters into the dark hall rather than behind the door jamb, and that in 2059 the black-and-white 1870s rooster reads as a pasted print in a hazy grey world. The rights reviewer noted that the Rizzuto plate is recorded as Library of Congress 'No known restrictions' rather than an explicit public-domain statement; other Rizzuto files in the same collection are public domain on Commons.
