# boucle-là: hinge alternatives (U2, dialogue writer)

Every candidate keeps the same pairs: A B C [H] C B A, with the same art, balloons and speakers. Only the hinge line changes, and with it the note face where noted. The candidates are grouped by the **speech-act change** they produce in the return, which plan invariant I5 requires.

Each group has one lettered alternate balloon. These are RGBA PNGs at page scale in `films/sources/boucle-la/dialogue-writer/lettering/`, drawn in the same hand, pen and seed rule as the chosen balloons. There is a contact sheet at `films/swarm/boucle-la/dialogue-writer-build/lettering-sheet.jpg`.

Act codes:
- A goes from accuse to counter-accuse.
- B goes from deny to jab.
- C goes from press to deflect.

## G1: addressee reveal. The accuser turns out to be the addressee. Leverage passes to Gustave. **(CHOSEN)**

| line | note face | return reading |
|---|---|---|
| **H1 (chosen)** « À MA CHÈRE ODETTE... » TIENS. C'EST POUR TOI. | « À MA CHÈRE / ODETTE » | **C′** is a flustered feint ("so who's it from?!"). **B′** is sarcasm ("me, write *you* notes?"). **A′** is a lame counter-charge ("you planted it?"). |
| H1b · LIS DONC L'ADRESSE, MA CHÈRE ODETTE. | the same | The same act change. It is weaker: he tells her to read rather than reading it aloud, so the address stays on the note face and is not quoted in the balloon. If the face is illegible at phone size, the reveal is lost. |

- Lettered: `balloon-H.png` (chosen) and `balloon-H-G1b.png` (alternate).
- Why H1 wins: the balloon quotes the note face exactly, so the reveal is carried twice, once by the lettering and once by the turned card on the bell. It also hands the object back ("TIENS"), which gives Gustave a gesture to animate.

## G2: author reveal. The accuser turns out to be the author. Leverage passes to Gustave, and A′ becomes an evasion of her own guilt.

| line | note face | return reading |
|---|---|---|
| H2 · « MON CHER ALFRED... » C'EST BIEN TON ÉCRITURE, NON ? | « MON CHER / ALFRED » (alternate face, `note-face-G2-alfred-page.png`) | **C′**: "then whose is it?!" becomes a denial of her own hand, a deflection by a guilty writer. **B′**: "you think I've nothing better to do?" becomes a jab at *her* idle afternoons. **A′**: "you left it here?" becomes an accusation of snooping and planting. |

- Lettered: `balloon-H-G2.png`.
- Cost: it needs a second note face and a third name. The story moves from a love note *received* to an affair *conducted*. That is a stronger turn, but it is heavier, and the balloon runs to three lines.

## G3: benign reveal. The quarrel becomes a gift. Leverage passes to Gustave, but tenderly. The acts turn to embarrassment and teasing rather than to attack and defence.

| line | note face | return reading |
|---|---|---|
| H3 · C'EST DE MOI, GROSSE BÊTE. BONNE FÊTE, ODETTE. | « À MA CHÈRE / ODETTE » (unchanged) | **C′**: "then who's it from?!" becomes mortified backpedalling. **B′**: "you think I've nothing better to do?" becomes affectionate irony, since he plainly did. **A′**: "it's you who left it?" becomes delighted recognition, a question now answered "yes". |

- Lettered: `balloon-H-G3.png`.
- This is a different named branch. Note that it contradicts the first-pass B: he denied writing it, so in G3 B′ re-reads as a joke, not as a lie. It is legal and funny, but the leverage is soft. The B3 reader test may read the turn as "they make up" rather than as a leverage shift.

## G4: inert control. No act changes, and leverage stays with Odette.

| line | note face | return reading |
|---|---|---|
| H4 · IL FAIT FROID ICI. | back-out (the note never turns) | The structure is legal, but **every pair keeps its first act**: accuse, deny and press repeat as accuse, deny and press. |

- Lettered: `balloon-H-G4.png`.
- Kept as the plan's "legal but inert" counterexample. It shows that the mirror is necessary but not sufficient. It is also the fixture's "different hinge with the same pair sequence passes as a separately named candidate" case, and the reader test should fail it.

## Rejected outright

- "C'EST LE FACTEUR." It reveals a fact about delivery, not about the addressee, so no pair changes act. This is the plan's bland case 1.
- Any hinge spoken by Odette. Gustave must speak H so that the leverage flip is *his* act. Odette speaking H would make her reveal against herself, and the B′ jab would have no prior.
