LE MOT, after Lécroart's Boucle-là. One page read A B C H C B A. Foraged: the quarrelling couple, cut out of Honoré Daumier's lithograph Mœurs conjugales no. 3 (1839, Rijksmuseum, CC0 via Wikimedia Commons), and two Commons sounds: a hand-bell struck once at the hinge (CC0) and a paper rustle (public domain). Handmade: the cut-out pieces, the pinned rig and every in-between drawing; all lettering, balloons, borders, the note and margin furniture, by pen stroke in code. Silence otherwise. No voice is heard: the archive voice grains were English, so the balloons speak. No room tone, no music, nothing synthesized.
