Foraged: Wilhelm Busch's Max und Moritz plates (1865, public domain, Wikimedia Commons) give the miller, porter and boys, cut out as jointed puppets. Paper is James Puckett's scan (CC BY 2.0, graded); the table is a CC0 wood scan. Paper, thud and switch sounds are public domain from Commons; the bell is Work With Sounds / Konrad Gutkowski (CC BY 4.0, one strike cut and re-levelled); room tone is Wreckless (1935, Jam Handy, Prelinger, public domain). Handmade: pen lettering, balloons, key, forearm, door and fold marks. The table, fold and light box are rendered in code from those scans, exposure by exposure, not photographed. The lamp hum is synthesised.
