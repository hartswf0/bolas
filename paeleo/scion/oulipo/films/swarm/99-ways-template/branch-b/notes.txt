Foraged from the Library of Congress: Arthur Rothstein's 1937 FSA photographs of the Randolph, Vermont station agent and a clock, Esther Bubley's 1943 OWI station front and visitor, a 1922 Red Cross key, and a blank 1934 Federal Theatre Project sheet as paper. Sound: Moving Image Archive room tone (1914), Wikimedia Commons foley and a train recording by Glaneur de sons (CC BY 3.0, excerpted, low-passed). Handmade: cutouts cut by hand (GrabCut) from those photographs, the hinged rig, plate repairs, borders, balloons, pen lettering and dialogue, memo, SERVICE, TICKET OFFICE and RANDOLPH cards, hook, tag, ledger card. Declared: synthesized counter bell and paper sound; no voices.
