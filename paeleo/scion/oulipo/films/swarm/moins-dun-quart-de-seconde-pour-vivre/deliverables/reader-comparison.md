## Editor's side-by-side notes (not blind-reader answers)

**Medium and tones**
- Two tones: ink #141414 and paper #FAFAFA, with no colour.
- Mid-tone share on the page is 1.8%, all of it on edges. The reference is about 12% and kf-1 about 5%.

**Framing against kf-1**
- Tighter: head and torso take about 0.66 of panel height, against kf-1's ~0.6.
- The bench is cropped by both edges.

**Lamp and balloons**
- The lamp globe is clear in all eight panels. kf-1 hid it in six.
- No balloon tail crosses the ear, and no balloon covers the eye or the envelope flap.
- B2 sits upper right, so the lamp it cites is visible.
- B3 leaves the sealed flap visible.

**Against the reference**
- The contour is heavier and more even than the reference's photocopied pen line.
- The lettering is a looser single-stroke capital hand.
- The family resemblance holds: black night ground, white masses, a wordless third panel, a short last line.

## Reader questions (plan.json `reader_questions`)
1. What is the figure doing and holding, and does the drawing change at any point across the panels? How do you know?
2. In the top strip, what does the figure decide, and how does each balloon move it toward that decision?
3. What happens in the panel with no words, and what would be lost if a balloon were put there or the panel removed?
4. In the bottom strip, who is the figure talking to, and what is the figure trying to establish?
5. Which line in the bottom strip changes how you read the figure's eye, and what does the eye mean before and after it?
6. What do 'the lamp' and 'still sealed' refer to in the picture, and what role do they play in what is being said?
7. Comparing the two strips at the same size: what is identical, what is different, and what does each strip's last panel make you understand about the same expression?
