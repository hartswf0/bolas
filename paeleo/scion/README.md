# SCION · the swarm operator

Fifty-four animation swarms. Each is a standalone prompt that turns one artist, researcher, studio, algorithm or archive into a working studio with a proof to make and a critic to answer to. PAELEO films are made with them, one swarm per box. Live: https://hartswf0.github.io/bolas/paeleo/scion/

- 54 swarms, in five families: Artists & Directors 7 · Research Pioneers 8 · Studios & Software 13 · Algorithms & Techniques 20 · Archives & Provenance 6
- 8 numbered sections in every prompt, in the same order; 15 points of doctrine, word for word the same in all 54; 5 inputs (work, direction, delivery, resources, rights)
- 1,127 to 1,250 words each, 62,531 in all; 23 name a research starting point
- Each name is a lineage, not an endorsement; the prompts say so themselves.

## Anatomy

Header: operator lineage, interpretation, identity, the five inputs. Then:

1. Creative theorem to prove
2. Native formal language
3. Assemble the smallest capable swarm
4. Hard proof scene; produce before scaling
5. The bar and acceptance tribunal
6. Evolving inquiry, not blind retries
7. Discovery and recursive expansion
8. Deliverable contract

Then the inherited doctrine, and START NOW.

## The doctrine

1. **Creative thesis.** Establish the viewer's central experience and one defining passage; name what would make the result bland.
2. **Inspectable bar.** Inspect relevant source clips, artworks, implementations or experiments when accessible.
3. **Hard proof first.** Build the most revealing short, end-to-end passage with image, movement, spatial staging, sound and a real export or working interactive playback.
4. **Accountable swarm.** Recruit only working agents necessary for coupled problems.
5. **Acting.** Link attention → intention → gesture → resistance → changed state.
6. **Consequential world.** Model anchors, scale, visibility, physical constraints and contact.
7. **Audio and staging.** Make attention, sound events, holds, contrast and edit timing integral to the shot, not a polish stage.
8. **Canonical state.** Version assets, masks, source identifiers, rigs, seed, camera, timing, sound, tests and dependencies.
9. **Independent criticism.** The builder cannot approve its own important work.
10. **Root-cause repair.** Reproduce the defect, intervene in the smallest justified state, re-render and test the neighboring shots and whole assembly for regressions.
11. **Recursive inquiry.** When repairs stall, list observed failure, causal hypothesis, assumption, alternate model and a discriminating experiment.
12. **Creative mutation.** Keep promising accidents as bounded forks, compare against the bar and either integrate or archive with a rationale; preserve roughness when it is expressive.
13. **Whole-work assembly.** Keep an always-playable cut that reveals cumulative rhythm; local approvals never guarantee the film works.
14. **Honest evidence.** Label claims as measured, structurally verified, perceptually judged or unknown.
15. **Resources and stopping.** Stop on independent acceptance, human stop, authorized budget exhaustion or a justified no-progress boundary.

## How PAE-00 ran it

| Doctrine | What happened |
|---|---|
| Creative thesis | A dog the person can understand but not command. Bland would have been a dog that obeys. |
| Inspectable bar | No reference film was available, so every critic judged against the bar in the prompt and said so: three times "inconclusive" or "does not pass" before a pass. |
| Hard proof first | The proof scene in the prompt (watch, approach, retreat, lie down, refuse; earn the approach) became the film's middle, built end to end with sound before anything else was added. |
| Accountable swarm | The six roles were merged into one working session (dramaturg, state graph, clip assembly, interaction, model-based testing), because the coupling was tight. The critic was always a separate agent. |
| Acting | Attention, intention, gesture, resistance, changed state: ears, tail, crouch, the turned back, the chin on the paws. The tags say the same thing, but the critics checked it with the tags masked. |
| Consequential world | The sill, the door, the floor and the distance between the two bodies are in the machine; nothing crosses the threshold that the machine does not allow. |
| Audio and staging | Keys for the prompts, a wordless voice, the door, paws that come nearer, and a quiet hold at the end. |
| Canonical state | One deterministic timeline: the film renders identically from tools/paeleo, and every cut is committed. |
| Independent criticism | Four rounds, four fresh critics. Each saw the film and the bar, never the makers' notes, and each left a falsifiable condition for the next. |
| Root-cause repair | An absorbing state and a speed jump were found by the model-based tests, a neck that stood up like a plank by a critic; each was fixed in the rig or the machine, not painted over. |
| Recursive inquiry | When the "come in" beat failed twice, the question changed from "is it visible" to "does it read as a choice": the look in through the door, then the chin down for good. |
| Creative mutation | The jamb drawn over the dog's back, first a fix, became the image of the idea: half in, half out. |
| Whole-work assembly | Every critic watched the whole 82 s, not the repaired shot. |
| Honest evidence | Critics measured the sound; none could hear it, and the page says so. No human viewing is claimed. |
| Resources and stopping | Stopped at the first independent pass; what the last critic left is listed as the next repairs, not done quietly. |

## The 54 through the dog

Each lens (`lenses.js`) draws the same porch, dog and loop of prompts the way that swarm would; stills in `paeleo/media/scion/`, the reel is PAE-01.

### SCION 01 · Terry Gilliam — Comic Cutout Metamorphosis

*Artists & Directors · 1974 BBC cutout demonstration; Monty Python collage interludes*

- **Through the dog (engraved cut-outs, a hard substitution).** The dog is an engraving cut from a book. When it is called, its head is swapped for the thing it wants least, a door, and the gag moves the smallest piece.
- **Theorem.** Convert found illustrations into surprising physical comedy. Start from what an image *already* implies, then violate that expectation by moving the smallest necessary fragment. Prefer visual logic over smoothness.
- **Proof scene.** An engraved dignitary notices a fish; his head is replaced by the fish; the fish becomes a door; a crowd walks out of his collar in an 8–12 second scene. Build it first as key poses with sound, not as a polished asset catalog.

### SCION 02 · Lotte Reiniger — Articulated Shadow Theater

*Artists & Directors · 1920s silhouette puppets, hinged limbs, glass-table exposure*

- **Through the dog (shadow theatre, articulated silhouettes).** Every piece becomes a black silhouette on a lit screen, so the dog's choice has to read from outline alone: the ears, the tail, the turned back.
- **Theorem.** Make emotional action legible through contour, positive/negative space and precisely limited articulation. Preserve the beauty of silhouettes: never rescue weak action with internal textures.
- **Proof scene.** A small figure climbs a gate, hesitates, offers a hand to another figure, then chooses whether to let go. Prove the action in silhouette at 12 fps and normal speed.

### SCION 03 · Max Ernst — Surreal Collage Grammar

*Artists & Directors · 1934 Une semaine de bonté; nineteenth-century book-illustration recombination*

- **Through the dog (collage grammar, frottage parts).** Each part of the dog is filled from a different found surface (bark, feathers, print, weave), so it reads as a dog made of other things.
- **Theorem.** Construct impossible worlds whose visual surfaces feel uncannily continuous. Combine sources by associational friction, not randomized absurdity. Make one hybrid image capable of causing the next.
- **Proof scene.** A lion-headed magistrate enters a flooded drawing room; the window sprouts wings; the character steps into a new engraving as if crossing rooms. Make a three-beat animated collage with one visual motif repeating in changed function.

### SCION 04 · Frank Mouris — Autobiographical Image Torrent

*Artists & Directors · Frank Film (1973); fast-moving image collage and spoken autobiographical narration*

- **Through the dog (an autobiographical image torrent).** The dog is built from a flood of tiny images, a new one every frame, and still reads as one dog choosing.
- **Theorem.** Treat the archive as a temporal vocabulary capable of describing a life by collisions of words, images, and rhythm. Density is useful only when recurrence and changes of speed make it legible.
- **Proof scene.** A 15-second memory told by a voice that pivots between three meanings of the same word, using 25–50 rapid images and two deliberate holds.

### SCION 05 · Yuri Norstein — Atmospheric Multiplane Cinema

*Artists & Directors · Hedgehog in the Fog, Tale of Tales; layered glass multiplane camera practice*

- **Through the dog (atmospheric multiplane).** Glass plates in depth: haze between the house and the dog, the yard moving slower than the porch, so the dog sits inside air.
- **Theorem.** Make depth, obscurity, and reveal carry emotional information. The world should resist immediate visibility: focus, parallax, and occlusion become dramatic events.
- **Proof scene.** A figure follows a light through three foreground occluders into fog, momentarily loses its position, and finds a nearer hand instead of the expected distant object.

### SCION 06 · John Whitney — Harmonic Motion Orchestra

*Artists & Directors · Computer-generated abstract motion, Permutations and harmonic choreography*

- **Through the dog (harmonic motion).** Every joint of the dog leaves a trace, and the traces are read as harmonics: the walk is a set of phased circles, the wag a faster one.
- **Theorem.** Compose motion as interacting frequencies, phases, symmetries, and accumulations. Numerical structure must produce perceptible musical development, not ornamental complexity.
- **Proof scene.** Animate twenty engraved ornaments into a dancing procession where a 3:2 phase relation creates approach, interference and reunion across 12 seconds.

### SCION 07 · Peter Foldès — Drawn Metamorphosis

*Artists & Directors · Hunger (1974), early computer-assisted interpolation between hand-drawn forms*

- **Through the dog (drawn metamorphosis).** The dog is a line drawing that boils and leaves its last positions behind, so every change of state is a drawing becoming the next drawing.
- **Theorem.** Use progressive transformation of drawn forms as narrative consequence. Every in-between should sustain shape identity long enough for the change to be felt.
- **Proof scene.** A seated diner grows into the furniture he is consuming; the linework gradually becomes an architectural enclosure in an 8-second action.

### SCION 08 · Ivan Sutherland — Constraint Sketchpad Swarm

*Research Pioneers · Sketchpad (1963); graphical constraints and direct manipulation*

- **Through the dog (constraints, points and lines (Sketchpad)).** The dog becomes a constraint drawing on a phosphor screen: its feet are held to the floor and its lengths are kept, while the light pen moves the hand.
- **Theorem.** Build an animation interface in which the animator changes relationships rather than manually repairing every dependent point. The user should move a hand and the rest of the constrained body should respond coherently.
- **Proof scene.** Allow a user to drag an archived figure’s hand onto a door handle while both feet remain planted, body lengths remain consistent, and the elbow chooses a believable configuration.

### SCION 09 · Takeo Igarashi — As-Rigid-as-Possible Puppetry

*Research Pioneers · As-rigid-as-possible shape manipulation, 2005*

- **Through the dog (as-rigid-as-possible puppetry).** The dog becomes a mesh of triangles that bend but try not to stretch; red triangles are the ones being stretched by the pose.
- **Theorem.** Make image deformation behave like bending a piece of paper or articulated cutout, preserving local shape without suppressing expressive exaggeration.
- **Proof scene.** Deform a single historic character’s sleeve, jaw and torso through three deliberately extreme poses while preserving buttons, eyes and linework.

### SCION 10 · Schaefer, McPhail & Warren — MLS Deformation

*Research Pioneers · Image Deformation Using Moving Least Squares (2006)*

- **Through the dog (moving least squares deformation).** A few handles, and the dog warps smoothly between them; the grid behind shows the warp the handles make.
- **Theorem.** Let an artist drag carefully chosen landmarks and contour segments to reshape historical images intuitively, comparing affine, similarity, and rigid variants based on visual effect.
- **Proof scene.** Animate an engraved face opening its mouth and a long bird neck curving around a frame using identical control handles but multiple MLS variants.

### SCION 11 · Connelly Barnes — Video Puppetry Tabletop

*Research Pioneers · Video Puppetry: A Performative Interface for Cutout Animation (2008); coauthors Jacobs, Sanders, Goldman, Rusinkiewicz, Finkelstein and Agrawala*

- **Through the dog (video puppetry on a tabletop).** The paper dog is held on rods from above, the way a tabletop performer tracks and moves real cut-outs under a camera.
- **Theorem.** Turn the tabletop into a stage: let a person move paper figures while software captures a reusable performance without recording the puppeteer’s hands as part of the fictional world.
- **Proof scene.** A filmed tabletop puppet walks through an arch, opens a door, knocks over a vase, and crosses into a digital background. Capture in real time and replay deterministically.

### SCION 12 · Alec Jacobson — Localized Deformation Weights

*Research Pioneers · Bounded biharmonic weights for real-time deformation (2011), coauthors Baran, Popović and Sorkine*

- **Through the dog (localized deformation weights).** Each bone owns the paper near it, and the colour shows how much: the weights that let one hand move without dragging the whole dog.
- **Theorem.** Create responsive 2D puppet rigs with meaningful automatic weight falloff, especially in concave and complicated shapes, without making the artist paint every influence by hand.
- **Proof scene.** Auto-rig an octopus and a coat-wearing human from cutout masks; bend tentacles and elbows while unrelated regions remain stable.

### SCION 13 · Nora Willett — Secondary Motion Swarm

*Research Pioneers · Secondary Motion for Performed 2D Animation (2017), with Li, Popović, Berthouzoz and Finkelstein*

- **Through the dog (secondary motion).** The ears and tail lag behind the body and overshoot; the springs that make them follow are drawn, so you see the follow-through the dog's choices cause.
- **Theorem.** Give performing paper figures secondary dynamics that communicate material and intention. Hair, coat tails, reeds, scarves and loose limbs should reveal acceleration, resistance and stopping.
- **Proof scene.** A fox sprints, stops abruptly, looks back, and lets its scarf settle while the world remains still. Compare no secondary motion, generic oscillation and tuned material-specific follow-through.

### SCION 14 · Nora Willett — Pose2Pose Casting

*Research Pioneers · Pose2Pose: Pose Selection and Transfer for 2D Character Animation (2020), with Shin, Jin, Li and Finkelstein*

- **Through the dog (pose to pose, cast from a library).** Along the bottom, the dog's library of drawn poses; the one it is in now lights up. The machine chooses the state, the library supplies the drawing.
- **Theorem.** Build a performance-transfer engine that uses a small set of artist-approved drawings to animate a character across new recorded actions. Prefer coherent drawing sequences over continuous warped artifacts.
- **Proof scene.** Take an 8-second reach, recoil, turn and walk performance. Transfer to a small collection of Victorian engravings with only twelve target drawings, then reveal the actual selected path.

### SCION 15 · Omid Poursaeed — Learned Layered Puppet

*Research Pioneers · Neural Puppet: Generative Layered Cartoon Characters (2019), with Kim, Shechtman, Saito and Belongie*

- **Through the dog (a learned layered puppet).** The dog pulled apart into the layers a model would have to learn from a few drawings: each part on its own plate, stacked in depth.
- **Theorem.** Infer consistent character structure from a few disconnected illustrations while preserving layers, occlusion and out-of-plane variations. Treat learned motion as a hypothesis, not a substitute for explicit tests.
- **Proof scene.** From six frames of a drawn character, reconstruct a reusable layered actor that can turn, crouch and reach; provide side-by-side model output and hand-authored corrections.

### SCION 16 · JibJab — Recastable Performance Studio

*Studios & Software · Personalized Starring You films; tracked live-action plates and face compositing*

- **Proof scene.** A dancer bows, turns to profile, exchanges a hat, and reacts to a surprise. Replace the head with three stylistically different authorized characters using one body plate.

### SCION 17 · South Park — Repeatable Graphic Grammar
