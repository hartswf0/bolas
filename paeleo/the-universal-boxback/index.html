The Universal Boxback

Can a house be composed the way a mind plans one, from the finished form back to the first step, and can one refused beam re-phase the whole house as music?

the film · 1280×720 · 24 fps · song by Treblo, unaltered

Twenty corrugated boxes start as the constellation of a finished house. They dissolve into an oscillator bank: a ring, a rational lock, a detuned drift, a re-lock. Then the house is made, one box per beat. The architect gives directions, and the builder replies that BEAM 7 CANNOT GO THERE. The next direction re-plans every row. A 3:2 accumulation builds to the densest moment and returns exactly. The release slows to stillness, and the house rains back row by row, landing on the last downbeat.

the hard proof · 3 : 2

The swarm's proof: a procession where a 3:2 phase relation makes approach, interference and reunion. The red box laps three times while the yellow box laps twice, and you can count them. Measured by the critic: 3.04 and 1.96 laps, with motion stopping on the beat.

The case

The question

From the producer's cards "The House That Language Built". In Aristotle (Metaphysics VII.7), thought starts from the end form and works back to a step that can be done, and making starts there. In Plato (Statesman 259e), the architect directs until the work is done, and the test is the builder who reports that a beam cannot go where the plan puts it.

The trick

Motion is composed as frequencies, phases and accumulations (Whitney), so the plan is literally a score. A refusal is a phase event: one local fix propagates through every row.

The recipe

A parametric oscillator bank locked to the song's 130.03 BPM grid, with rational locks against deliberate detuned drift. Kraft boxes on black, with red and yellow markers so the 3:2 can be counted. Typed architect and builder cards. Synthesised bell tones at the reunions, mixed under the unaltered song.

Something to try

In the proof, follow only the red box for twelve seconds, then only the yellow one. Three against two.

What broke

Six rounds, each with a fresh independent critic. None could hear the film; sound is measured only.

  2. 2: the middle stalled for 39 beats, a card was clipped, the ring went off the frame, and the beam collided.
  3. 3: the making section was uncontrolled, with boxes colliding and clipping at the edges.
  4. 4: content touched the edges, landing flashes covered labels, and the release outshone the peak.

  6. 6: pass / pass. The peak window is 14.50 against 11.03 for the next best, the release decays monotonically, the landing falls 0.02 s from b368, and nothing lit is near an edge.
