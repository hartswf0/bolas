Corrugated Sky

Can a house keep a family's day when the family is only shadows on its wall, and can a child's choice to let go read from outline alone?

the film · 1280×720 · 24 fps, poses on twos · song by Treblo, unaltered

The cardboard house wakes and sets breakfast for four. Its family are white cut-outs on a black wall. At night they step out, and each place on the wall goes dark. At the gate a girl climbs, hesitates, takes the dog's paw and lets go. The house burns and sinks, the figures go back to the wall, and soft rain falls on the corrugated roof.

the hard proof · the gate

The swarm's proof scene, built before anything else: a small figure climbs a gate, offers a hand to another figure, then chooses whether to let go. Here her hand opens (frame 272), her arm withdraws (frames 278–282), and only then does the paw fold (frame 284). The order makes it her choice.

The case

The question

After Ray Bradbury's "There Will Come Soft Rains", taken as theme only and never quoted: a house that keeps its routines for people who are gone. Can shadow theatre carry that without a single face?

The trick

Reiniger's rule is that nothing is rescued by internal texture. Every relationship has to read in pure contour, with the colour removed. So the family is negative space: white figures on a black wall. Leaving is a place going dark, and returning is it lighting again.

The recipe

Pinned-joint silhouette rigs with joint-angle limits, poses on twos, and a backlit gradient running dawn → noon → bruise → fire → rain-grey. Every event sits on the song's 141.05 BPM grid. The house's own cards are written for the film (“12:00 · LUNCH IN THE GARDEN”, “I WILL KEEP THE LIGHT ON.”). The SFX are synthesised and mixed under the unaltered song.

Something to try

Watch the gate scene with the sound off and the colour off (any greyscale filter). If the let-go still reads as hers, the silhouette is doing the work.

What broke

Three rounds, each judged by a fresh independent critic. None of them could hear the film; sound claims are measurements only.

  1. Round 1: reference stronger / reference stronger. The let-go read as the dog letting go, because the paw folded first. The return was a dog, gate and flames blob. The fire read as weeds once the colour was removed. The night scene had eight family figures on screen. The beat grid ran about 60 ms late.
  2. Round 2: reference stronger / reference stronger. The let-go order was fixed and measured. The thumb's light-gap cut it off the palm entirely, so the limb detached. The dog was a two-legged plate that merged at night, at the return and in the rain.
  3. Round 3: pass / pass. Hand and thumb form one connected shape. The dog is its own four-legged shape at least 17.5 px clear at night, at the return and in the rain. The collapse happens on screen before the return.

Still open

These were named by the last critic and not yet repaired:

  • The father leaves the wall but never appears outside.
  • The dog touches the gate post once colour is removed.
  • The red end title nearly vanishes without colour.
  • The house's details are small on a phone.
