Operationchanges a state of affairs. A single person can perform one. This is the primary research concept: operative ekphrasis is the phenomenon, ekphrastic operations the umbrella.
Orchestrationarranges participants, agents, tools and sequences. It is one way of coordinating many operations: an important technique inside the program, not its name.

Imagetext · environment text · worldtext

The program's lineage, and the first thing its first film shows.

1 · representation

Imagetext

text beside a picture · the page

Text and image remediate each other on a flat plane: the page, the screen, the early interface. The Bolter and Mitchell paradigm.

2 · situatedness

Environment text

text pinned to a place · AEL

The imagetext is lifted off the screen and anchored to coordinates, markers and space. The AEL era: DART, Argon, locative media.

3 · execution

Worldtext

text the place runs on · PAELEO

The environment text gains rules and agency. The text does not just sit in a space; it generates and governs it, as a move in a language game.

Why not videotext: a video implies a fixed timeline meant to be watched. A world is inhabited, queried and simulated. Videotext is a closed loop, a broadcast; environment text is an open system, a space; worldtext is a playable language game.

PAE-00Oct 24 · 1 min 22 s · one swarm: SCION 48, State Machines

The threshold

Can a description make a character that chooses, so that the person who prompts it can understand it but not command it?

The trick

Nobody animates the dog. The prompts change the world (posture, place, door, voice, hand); a five-state machine reads that world through what it perceives, trusts and remembers, and the paper puppet acts out whatever it chose.

Recipe
SCION 48 · State Machinesmachine.jsstage.jsChromium canvasnumpy soundindependent critic
  1. Write the dog first as a machine: five states, guards in priority order, a minimum window in each state, and three things it carries between frames: trust, pressure from repeated calls, and how many times it has refused.
  2. Test the machine before drawing anything: 400 random operators, every state reachable, no state it cannot be coaxed out of, never faster than its legs, and commands alone never earn an approach.
  3. Cut the cast from paper in code: limbs as tapered pieces with a fibre texture fixed to each piece, brass brads at the joints, hand-cut edges that stay the same from frame to frame, a cast shadow.
  4. Let springs carry the machine's choice into the pose (ears, tail, crouch, lie, head, turn), so every change takes time; tie the walk to the distance actually travelled.
  5. Score the film as prompts: each one is typed on screen (P), the paper tags pinned to the door, person and dog rewrite themselves (E), and the dog acts (O).
  6. Open with the lineage: a caption on a page is snipped out, carried into the place and pinned there, then becomes the rule the dog runs on.
  7. Synthesize the sound from the render's own cues: keys, a wordless voice with the contour of the words, paws that grow louder as the dog comes near, the door, crickets, a pad under the titles.
  8. Hand each cut to a critic who has not seen the notes; repair the causes it finds; hand the new cut to a fresh critic.

Collisions
    
      
  • POML already names Microsoft's Prompt Orchestration Markup Language. A reference or substrate, not the identity of this contribution.
    • 
      
  • PEO is the Person–Environment–Occupation model in occupational therapy. A collision, and also a precedent for a situated, relational understanding of action.
    • 
      
  • The Georgia Tech name is Augmented Environments Lab, singular words exactly as they are. PAELEO never renames the lab.

    
Formal and public
    
      
  • For the dissertation: Ekphrasis as Operation, the theoretical subtitle.
    • 
      
  • For the studio: Ekphrastic Operations. Shorter, more active, broad enough for film, games, archives, XR and agentic performance.
    • 
      
  • Promptable names an affordance of the environment. A place, a character, a film or an archive can become promptable and keep its own constraints and agency.

The argument in one breath: operative ekphrasis is the phenomenon investigated. PEO is the theoretical and experimental model. PAELEO is the language and working environment in which descriptions are authored, executed, contested and evaluated. AEL is the institutional lineage from which it develops: a laboratory that develops a language for operating within environments.
