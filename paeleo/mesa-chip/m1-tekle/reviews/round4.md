# SCION M1 · critic round 4 · PASS (narrow)

Fresh independent critic (a 96-cell contact sheet, 23 full-resolution stills, crop strips of the head turn, the step out, the draw, the gun arm, the stair, Mesa's face at 6×, the floor, and a 480×270 phone-size frame; an RMS onset scan of the sound; judged the picture before reading round 3).

The theorem is proved cleanly. No cell moves at any point: the knight leaves its window, fights, is painted and breaks purely by which cells are lit. Every beat of the ekphrasis is on screen within tolerance. What keeps this narrow is legibility: at phone size the duel is a knot, Uncle Mesa loses his identity in the walking poses, and the mirror floor still has to be searched for.

**2 · Native language.** The proof scene is met to the letter.
