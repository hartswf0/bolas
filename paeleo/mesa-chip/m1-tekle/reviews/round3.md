# SCION M1 · critic round 3 · REFERENCE STRONGER

Fresh independent critic (a 96-frame contact sheet, 25 full-resolution stills, 8 fps strips of the fall and the shatter, crops of the window, the floor and Mesa's head, a frame darkened to 30 %, an RMS envelope and the spectral peaks; judged the picture before reading round 2). A near miss. The theorem holds and every beat is on time, but three things are still not drawn in the glass: the knight does not step out whole, Uncle Mesa stops being Mesa during the duel, and the frame is lopsided.

**1 · The beats.**
- **Opening.** The dune, the canopy, the columns and the pale floor read. The descent reads weakly.
- **Head turn (3.0–3.6).** Visible at 960×540, but a flicker at phone size.
- **Steps out (4.5–4.8).** The window goes dark with only the knight's ghost lead in it, and a figure lights on the pier: the theorem at its best. But the figure outside is a coarse cream wedge of wall cells, not the silver, gold and red design, so "whole" does not read.
- **Sword (6.5) and gun (6.75).** They read.
- **The approach (7.25–9.5).** It reads over a near-silent drone: good tension.
- **Hit 1.** The shoulder turns cyan, and its pieces lie to the end.
- **The fall (12.2–12.5).** About six frames: brisk, but a fall.
- **The rise (13.8–14.1).** It reads.
- **Hits 2–5 (15–18).** The best passage: the knight keeps advancing while it comes apart.
- **Shatter (19.5–20.5).** It reads, and every colour stays.
- **The mirror floor.** Now present, but only findable in a crop.
- **The sit.** It reads.
- **The coda.** The empty window glows pale cyan, a real "after", and the light moves on across the pile.
- **Sound.** One bell partial per event: purposeful and synchronised, but schematic, and the gun raise has no sound.

**2 · Native language.** No cell moves, and the figures are drawn by which cells are lit. The ghost lead of every pose is a real "then, now and after" inside one cutting. Still failing: two drawing systems per figure. The hero poses have a fine cut; the in-between poses are coarse background cells, though the new dark halo helps. The calligraphic lead lives only in the canopy, and there is no icon manner.

**3 · The bar.**
- **Nothing moves that glass could not:** pass.
- **Colour is the event:** pass.
- **The lead holds in low light:** partly.
- **Dignity:** partly; he becomes a white lump in the duel.
- **Phone size:** partly.
- **Craft failures:**
  - the right 55 % of the frame is empty from 12 to 21.8 s;
  - the sword, the gun and the two bodies knot together at 15–19.8;
  - Mesa's cut changes scale between poses.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **In the standing poses:** a cream hat, dark-brown face cells, a white beard block, a white shirt, the yellow badge, khaki trousers, dark boots and the gun.
- **At 4× zoom:** gold flecks for the glasses.
- **Never:** two round lenses, eyes, the beard's length, pleats, or his age.
- **In the coarse poses (15–21.8):** the hat brim, face and beard dissolve.
