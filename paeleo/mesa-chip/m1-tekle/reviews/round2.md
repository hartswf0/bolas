# SCION M1 · critic round 2 · REFERENCE STRONGER

Fresh independent critic (a 96-frame contact sheet, 21 full-resolution event stills, about 70 crops, a per-frame change trace, a floor-brightness profile, a darkened grey-scale test, and the sound through a spectrogram, astats and an RMS envelope; judged the picture before reading round 1). A near miss: much better than round 1, but not yet clearing its own bar.

**1 · The ekphrasis.** Every scored beat happens at its time.
- **Opening.** The dune, the columns and the pale glass floor read. The descent reads weakly: he walks off a single block.
- **Head turn (3.0–3.3).** The visor wedge swings left past the window frame, toward Mesa. Visible, but tiny.
- **Steps out (4.5).** It happens, but the knight on the pier at 4.6–4.8 is a ragged pale column of wall cells, not the crisp knight from the window, so "whole" does not read.
- **Sword and gun.** They read; the gun is raised by lighting a different arm.
- **Hit 1.** It takes the upper chest and shoulder, and its cyan pieces lie to the end.
- **The fall (12.25–12.5).** About 6 intermediate frames: brisk, but now a fall.
- **The rise.** A crouch, then upright.
- **Hits 2–5.** Still the best passage.
- **Shatter.** Every shard keeps its colour, but the floor is never a mirror.
- **The sit.** An eight-frame lowering.
- **The coda light.** It crosses the pile (21.0–22.5) with a more than 50 % brightness change: met.
- **Sound.** A synchronised bell-partial strike for every event, purposeful but schematic.

**2 · Native language.** The theorem is proven honestly, and the ghost lead of every held pose, visible throughout, is a real Tekle-like idea: then, now and after in one cutting. But there are two drawing systems for the same figures. The hero poses have their own lead and read as designed glass. Every pose in between (Mesa at 0–1.5 and 15–21.9, the knight from 4.6 to 19.5) is a speckled blob of background cells with no outline of its own. The calligraphic lead lives only in the canopy, and there is no icon manner and no triptych.

**3 · The bar.**
- **Nothing moves that glass could not:** pass.
- **Colour is the event:** pass.
- **The lead holds the composition in low light:** fail; the darkened test at 15.0 leaves the knight as speckle.
- **Dignity:** partly.
- **Phone size:** partly.
- **Craft failures:**
  - the right 55 % of the frame is dead from 12 s;
  - the window is abandoned after 5 s;
  - the figures crowd the left third and overlap at 18–19.5.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.** In the hero poses at 960×540 he shows a cream hat, dark-brown face cells, a white chin and beard block, a white shirt, the yellow badge, khaki trousers, dark boots and the gun. Only at 6× zoom: a gold lens cell, and a beard separated from the shirt by lead. Not visible: a pair of lenses, eyes, the beard's length, the pleats, or his age.

Round 1 repairs:
1. **The lead draws the figures:** partly met (the hero poses only).
2. **The face in the icon manner:** partly met.
3. **No pops:** met.
4. **The coda light:** met.
5. **The mirror floor:** not met. It does not contradict the theorem, because fixed floor cells could be lit as a reflection.
6. **The head turn and the shoulder hit:** partly met.
7. **Use the window and the field:** not met.

Repair conditions (ranked):
1. **One drawing system per figure (4.6–19.5 knight; 0–2 and 14.5–22 Mesa).** Every lit pose has its own lead contour at least 2× the background weight, and the knight keeps its helm, plume, pauldrons and silver/gold/red division. Both are identifiable from lead alone when darkened to 30 %.
2. **The knight steps out whole (4.5–6.0):** the same design as the window knight.
3. **A mirror floor (12–24 s):** floor cells take the inverted colour of what stands above them.
4. **Use the window and the field after 12 s.**
5. **Mesa's face:** two gold lenses and a long beard, legible without zoom, in every pose.
6. **The head turn:** at least 3 visor cells, visible at 480×270.

Keep: the one fixed cutting with the ghost lead; the gun raised by lighting a different arm; the paints accumulating; the de-popped moves; the coda light; one bell strike per event.
