# SCION M4 · critic round 3 · PASS (narrow)

Fresh independent critic (a 96-frame contact sheet, 25 full-resolution stills, frame strips of the descent, the head turn, hits 2–5, the push-in and the coda, the pile's brightness measured; sound through an RMS envelope and 10 ms peak timing; judged the picture before reading round 2).

**1 · The beats.** Every beat of the ekphrasis is on screen at its time; three are thin.
- **The dune, the oculi and the slanted columns.** They read clearly.
- **The descent.** It reads, over two toy-like steps.
- **The head turn.** About 6 px on a 40 px head: the weakest beat.
- **The step out.** Strong: the window stays empty and blue.
- **The sword.** It comes from the hip with a pink arc.
- **The gun.** It reads.
- **Hit 1.** The shoulder sprouts cyan tiles, and they lie where the knight stood until 24 s.
- **The fall.** The sword arc lands and he goes over.
- **The rise.** Through a crouch.
- **Hits 2–5.** Each blooms and drops its colour.
- **Keeps walking.** It reads from 14 to 17 s. From about 17.6 s the legs are gone, and the knight is a helmet, a sword and three torso tiles hovering over the heap: Otomo-like loss of outline, but not walking.
- **The last shot.** A push-in, the red remnant blooming and flying apart.
- **The sit.** Clean, and fully in frame.
- **The light over the pieces.** It sweeps across the heap, and the shards brighten about 12 %, peaking at 21.8 s.
- **Sound.** Purposeful and well timed: footfalls, each shot cracking on time with a shard impact about 0.2 s later, near-silence while he is down, a held quiet beat, then the loudest event at the shatter (19.72 s).

**2 · Native language and the proof.** The ligne claire primitives are honestly present: uniform contour, flat fills, violet cast shadows angled away from the shafts, persistent tracers and sword arcs, and a bloom before every break. The proof is mostly carried: a clean-lined figure crosses the floor with its weapon leaving trails, is struck, and comes apart without the drawing ever going muddy. Not proved: "grows and splits like a living thing", since the bloom is rectangles inflating; and the ground is not vast.

**3 · The bar.** Craft failures:
- (a) the push-in is a scaled raster, so its lines thicken and soften at the climax; this is now the largest defect (the black bars are gone);
- (b) the reflections at almost full opacity make Mesa read as on stilts;
- (c) the draughtsmanship is still clip-art;
- (d) the tracers for shots 2–6 are pale;
- (e) the breaking is pretty, like confetti, but not frightening;
- (f) the blade overlaps his gun hand at 19.5.

Nothing is shaded that the light does not justify.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **Can see:** dark brown skin, a long pointed white beard distinct from the shirt, a round gold-brown lens (clear in the push-in), a cream wide-brimmed hat, a white shirt with pockets and a placket, a yellow badge (about 6 px), khaki trousers, dark boots and the cyan six-shooter.
- **Cannot see:** his age beyond the beard, vertical pleats, and facial features beyond the lens and beard.

He is a correct costume inventory on a stick body.

Round 2 repairs:
1. **The push-in:** met, but now it fails line weight.
2. **The coda light:** partly met (about 12 %).
3. **Glass under the paint:** partly met; under I3 a minority core is the ceiling.
4. **The tracers:** partly met.
5. **The figures as drawings:** mostly met.
6. **Keeps walking:** void, since the advance is fixed by the score, though the blade still crosses his hand at 19.5.
7. **The badge and pleats:** partly met.

Repair conditions (ranked):
1. **Redraw the push-in at its own scale,** with contour width within ±25 % of the wide.
2. **Keep a leg on the floor until the shatter** (17.0–19.4).
3. **The blade never overlaps his arm** at 19.3–19.7.
4. **Contact lines at the soles,** with reflections at 35 % opacity or less.
5. **Tracers at full paint saturation,** with a black contour on the yellow.
6. **The coda brightening at least 15 %,** with the hue held.
7. **The head turn:** at least 12 px of visor travel.
8. **The bloom grows along the outline** over at least 4 frames.

Keep: the empty window; the cyan shards where the knight stood; the bloom at every hit; the crisp heap; the shadows; the fall and rise; the near-silence while he is down; the quiet beat before the crash; the push-in as an idea (redrawn); the exact timing; the clean sit with the light passing over the pieces.
