# SCION M4 · critic round 1 · REFERENCE STRONGER

Independent critic (fresh agent; about 170 frames: 96 contact-sheet frames at 4 fps, 34 full-resolution stills at and just after every event, and about 40 frame-by-frame crops; verdict from the picture and the audio track only, no builder notes).

**1 · The beats on screen.** One locked wide shot, side-on, for the full 24 s. Most beats read at their times.
- **Head turn (3.0).** The visor slit moves to the left edge, toward Mesa, but only by about 6 px, so it is nearly invisible.
- **Steps out (4.5–5.2).** The knight slides left out of the blue lancet, whole, with a pale trail, and the window stays empty and blue: a strong touch.
- **Red sword (6.0–6.3).** It simply appears; there is no draw arc.
- **Gun (6.5–6.8).** It reads.
- **Hit 1 (9.4–10.2).** A cyan tracer, then a cyan organic bloom on the shoulder (9.6), then pieces fly (9.8) and lie on the floor to 24.0.
- **Mesa goes down (12.0–12.4).** A pink sword-arc trail sweeps down, but the blade passes through his torso and he goes down as a rigid plank in about 4 frames.
- **Gets up (13.9–14.4).** The same rigid board rotates up.
- **Hits 2–5.** Each is a bloom followed by a drop of paint-coloured pieces, and the knight visibly thins.
- **"Keeps walking".** Weak. The knight advances only about 70 px in 4 s and stands parked at sword reach without striking.
- **Shatter (19.5–20.3).** By 19.5 what remains of the knight is entirely red. It blooms (19.65), flies (19.9) and settles (20.3). Every freed piece keeps its paint, but none of the knight's own silver, gold and red glass survives on the floor.
- **Mirror floor.** Not drawn.
- **Lowers the gun and sits.** Both read clearly.
- **The light in the coda.** It fails: the moving floor light slides along the bottom edge and never touches the main pile.
- **Sound.** The events are marked: a drone, a burst at 4.5, a chord at 6.0, footsteps, a blip and a burst at each hit, a peak at 12.2, and the loudest event at 19.5–21.5. But the hit bursts are flat full-band white noise, not glass, and hit 1 is no louder than the footsteps.

**2 · Native language and the theorem.** The native language is partly there: uniform contour, flat fills, consistent cast shadow, trails that persist (the exit, the sword arc, the paint tracers), and the bloom before each break. The bloom is the film's best idea and it is carried out at every hit. But "calm drawing, uncalm events" needs drawing, and this is vector clip-art: stacks of rectangles and a two-pose scissor walk. The ground is a shallow stage, not vastness. There is no glass cathedral and no glass or mirror floor, and the knight reads as Mondrian tiles, not stained glass. Mœbius's actual pages, or Akira's bike trails and Tetsuo's body, beat it decisively on these terms.

**3 · The bar.** Line weight is constant, and the trails do show direction. Craft failures:
- (a) the figures do not read as drawings;
- (b) there is no acting in the fall or the rise, and the blade passes through Mesa without contact at 12.2;
- (c) the knight is parked at sword reach for 14.0–19.5 without striking;
- (d) the breaking is pretty but not frightening: the blooms are small, in the same static wide, with no scale change;
- (e) the sword after the shatter is a long red line running out of frame;
- (f) the floor light is drawn behind the pieces;
- (g) the hit sounds are flat noise blocks.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **Can see:** dark brown skin, a white beard (a block, separated from the shirt only by the contour), a gold lens dot, a cream wide-brimmed hat, a white shirt, khaki trousers, dark boots and a cyan six-shooter.
- **Cannot see:** his age beyond the beard, round glasses, anything guayabera about the shirt, and the yellow MESA CHIP badge.

Repair conditions (ranked):
1. **The coda light (22.0–24.0).** A floor light band passes over the main pile, and the shards under it brighten while keeping their hue, for at least 12 consecutive frames.
2. **A mirror or glass floor (0–24).** A visible inverted reflection of Mesa, the knight and the shards, on screen at 4.7, 9.8, 12.4, 19.9 and 23.5.
3. **The knight's own colours survive (19.5–24.0).** Silver, gold and red knight-glass shards are visible on the floor at 20.3 and 23.5, and the remnant is not uniformly repainted red.
4. **"Keeps walking" (14.0–19.5).** At least three visible alternating strides, closing at least 150 px, and no blade overlapping Mesa unless it is swinging.
5. **The fall and the rise as acting.** Visible contact, then a knee bend or hand plant, at least 6 frames each way, with no rigid plank rotation.
6. **Draughtsmanship.** A drawn profile with a round gold-rimmed lens, a beard silhouette distinct from the shirt, guayabera pleats or pockets and the yellow badge, lead lines between the knight's panes, and a closer framing on the bloom at 19.5–19.9 so that it fills at least 15 % of the frame height.
7. **Smaller fixes.** The draw travels from hip to guard over at least 4 frames with a trail. The fallen sword is contoured and kept in frame. The hit sounds are at least 6 dB over the footsteps, with a glass-like pitched decay.

Keep: the bloom before each break (the cyan shoulder at 9.6 and the red bloom at 19.65); the empty blue window; the pink sword-arc trail; the paint-coloured pieces that stay; the violet cast shadows and the constant line; the exact timing; the clear final sit.
