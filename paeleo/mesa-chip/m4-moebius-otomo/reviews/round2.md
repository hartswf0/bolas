# SCION M4 · critic round 2 · REFERENCE STRONGER

Fresh independent critic (96 contact-sheet frames, 36 full-resolution stills, frame strips of the draw, the gun, the fall, hits 2–3, the shatter and the coda; sound through a spectrogram, astats and silencedetect; judged the picture before reading round 1).

**1 · The beats.**
- **The dune and the columns.** The ceiling with its three oculi and the slanted shafts read. The floor now reflects Mesa, the knight and the fallen Mesa (3.3, 9.8, 12.5, 14.1).
- **The steps.** The descent reads, but the steps are tiny.
- **The head turn.** Still a few pixels.
- **The step out (4.5–5.0).** Strong.
- **The sword (6.0–6.2).** It now travels from the hip over about 4 frames, with a trail.
- **The gun.** Clean.
- **Hit 1.** A cyan tracer, a bloom on the shoulder, and the shoulder breaks off as cyan pieces that stay.
- **The fall (12.1–12.5).** It buckles at the knees and falls over about 8 frames.
- **The rise.** A crouch and a knee bend.
- **Hits 2–5.** Each blooms and drops, and the knight thins and lurches.
- **The last shot.** The remnant blooms, flies apart and settles.
- **The sit.** It reads, though he is half cut by the left edge.
- **The coda light.** Partly: it crosses the pile but is drawn under the shards.
- **Sound.** Purposeful. The near-silence while he lies down (12.2–13.8) is the best sound decision in the film, and the shatter is the loudest event.

**2 · Native language.** The contour, the flat fills, the violet cast shadows, the persistent trails and above all the bloom before each break are honestly there. "Calm drawing, uncalm events" half-proves itself: the events are uncalm, but the drawing is rectangles stacked on a pole, the knight's legs are one tile column, and Mesa's legs and their reflection fuse into a stilt at wide scale. The tracers for shots 2–5 are faint.

**3 · The bar.** Craft failures:
- (a) the punch-in at 19.54–20.08 is mis-registered, showing a black bar on the left and along the bottom: the largest single defect, at the climax;
- (b) the coda light passes under the pile;
- (c) the draughtsmanship is clip-art;
- (d) the blooms are small in the wide;
- (e) the knight advances only about 110 px, and the blade sits across Mesa's arm at 19.5;
- (f) the knight's own glass shows only as tiny pale cores.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **Can see:** dark brown skin, a pointed white beard distinct from the shirt, a round gold-rimmed lens, a cream hat, a white shirt with pockets and a placket, khaki trousers, dark boots and the gun.
- **Barely or not:** his age, guayabera pleats, the badge (a 2–3 px fleck) and facial features.
