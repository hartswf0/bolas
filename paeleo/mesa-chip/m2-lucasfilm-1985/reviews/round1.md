# SCION M2 · critic round 1 · REFERENCE STRONGER

Independent critic (fresh agent; about 250 frames: four 4 fps contact sheets, 26 full-resolution stills at and after every event, and 24 fps and 12 fps crop strips of the step-out, the walk, hit 1, the strike, the get-up and the coda; audio checked with astats, silencedetect, showspectrumpic and an RMS envelope; verdict from the picture, no builder notes).

**1 · The beats on screen.** Most land on time; four do not read.
- **Descent (0–2.5 s).** It reads.
- **Head turn (3.0 s).** It does not read: the rack focus to the window runs 2.25–3.3 s, so the knight is a soft smear during the turn.
- **Step out (4.55–5.0 s).** On time, in one continuous move, whole. But it is a superhero leap, not a step.
- **Draw (6.0–6.2 s) and gun (6.5–6.75 s).** Both read.
- **Hit 1 (9.5–10.3 s).** Clean, and the best single moment: a cyan burst off the right shoulder, with cyan shards tumbling, landing and lying to the end.
- **The strike (12.0–12.4 s).** It reads strongly: he topples back with directional blur.
- **The get-up (13.9–14.0 s).** A pop. There is also a hat continuity error: the hat lies on the floor, then is suddenly back on his head at 13.93 s.
- **Hits 2–6.** All on time, and they stay on the floor. Amber and yellow are hard to tell apart.
- **"Keeps walking."** Weak: the knight steps in place at point-blank range.
- **Shatter (19.5–20.5 s).** It reads.
- **Lowers the gun and sits.** Muddled: he sits at about 22.5 s, then the gun comes back up while he is seated.
- **The light moving on.** Present but faint, and it barely touches the main pile.

**2 · Native language and proof.** The knight is the real achievement:
- **Per-shard glints.** Slightly convex triangular shards, each with its own specular glint (6.2, 9.7, 15.0 s). It reads as glass and looks like it could break.
- **Motion blur where things move.** The leap, the fall and the get-up, though at 4.88 s it shows as two ghost copies rather than a streak.
- **The rack focus.** A genuine computed event, but placed so that it swallows the head turn.

The hand-keyed performance falls short. The walk barely advances and shows little weight shift, so the chain from attention to intention to gesture is thin. The plate is generic, with nothing that establishes "under the dune" or columns of light. The navy floor does give real mirror reflections.

The sound marks every event (footsteps, a chime at 3.0, an impact at the step-out, the draw's ring, the knight's footfalls, the glass shots, the strike block and its silent hold, the shatter's tinkle, and true silence from 23 s). The gun raise has no distinct sound.

**3 · The bar.**
- **Met:** the highlights are per shard, and the knight looks breakable.
- **Partly met:** the blur, which ghosts, and the focus, which comes at the wrong moment.
- **Not met:** weight shifting before the steps.
- **Craft failures:**
  - the hat teleport;
  - the pop get-up;
  - the gun re-raised while seated;
  - the two figures crowded on top of each other at 15–19.5 s;
  - amber and yellow indistinguishable;
  - the left step low in contrast.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **Can see:** brown skin, so he reads as a Black man; a cream wide-brimmed hat; a white shirt with a yellow badge square; khaki trousers; dark boots; and the gun.
- **Cannot see:** a long white beard (it merges into the shirt like a bib), two round gold lenses (there is one disc, which reads as a monocle), any guayabera detail, and his age. He is cruder than the knight.

Repair conditions (ranked):
1. **The head turn is legible (3.0–3.6 s).** The knight is sharp before 2.9 s, and its visor rotates visibly toward Mesa.
2. **The get-up and the hat (13.5–14.3 s).** No hat in two places. The get-up takes at least 0.6 s, with a push off a hand or knee.
3. **The walk with weight (7.0–9.5 s and 15.0–19.5 s).** The hips move over the planted foot, with a vertical bob and visible ground covered.
4. **Lowers the gun and sits (21–24 s).** The gun stays at or below the hip once lowered, and the step is distinguishable under him.
5. **The light moves on (20–24 s)** across the main multicoloured pile.
6. **Uncle Mesa.** A beard distinct from the shirt that reaches the chest, two round gold lenses or a framed round lens, and a guayabera cue.
7. **The step out (4.6–5.0 s).** A foot contacts the sill or floor, or the landing compresses, and the blur is a streak.
8. **The piece colours (15–18 s).** Amber and yellow are told apart from each other and from the gold.

Keep: the convex shard knight and its glints; the hit-1 burst and its cyan pile; the strike and the fall with directional blur; the mirror floor; the continuous move out of the window; the sound design, including the silent hold and the final silence; the true paint colours; the rack focus, re-timed.
