# SCION M3 · critic round 2 · PASS (narrow)

Fresh independent critic (144 contact-sheet frames at 6 fps; 60 frames at 24 fps across hit 1, the fall and the shatter; 24 full-resolution stills; crops of Mesa's face, the helmet and the coda; a scene-cut list, an RMS envelope, a spectrogram, astats and silencedetect; judged the picture before reading round 1).

**1 · The ekphrasis.** Most beats read at their times, and the story survives every cut cluster.

What reads:
- the step out, a jump cut that leaves the window empty (4.5–4.7);
- the sword and the gun (6.17, 6.5);
- the advance (7.3–9.5);
- hit 1 (cyan pieces lie from 9.92 to 24.0);
- the fall (12.2–12.67, strong);
- the rise (13.9–14.3);
- each colour of hits 2–5 lands at its second;
- the shatter (19.5–19.8, all six clumps distinct);
- the gun lowered, the cross to the step and the sit (20.8–23.0);
- the light moving across the pieces under the silence (23.3–23.95, about 29 % of the frame width).

Weak or missing:
- the descent down the steps is not told;
- the head turn barely moves (about a sixth of the helmet);
- "keeps walking" is not told (14.7–19.4, the knight is pinned at contact);
- amber is nearly lost at 15;
- there is no mirror floor in the action;
- the mosaic pools in the plate compete with the freed pieces.

**2 · Native language and proof.** The language is the film's own: card puppets, a torn collage plate, a hot worn grade and found inserts.

- **The fall is told three times as a genuine re-performance:** wide (12.21), medium where he is upright again and falls again (12.375), tilted reverse close where he falls a third time (12.5), then the wide with him lying (12.67). The story is never lost. Hit 1 and the shatter repeat the pattern.
- **The horned rider returns at decisions** (3.58, 7.0, 14.5, 21.25) and accumulates the paint. At 14.5 a single cyan reads as a tally of one, and at 21.25 the fully painted horns turn the walk to the step into a spent beast's rest. That is a real, if modest, insert that changes what the next shot means.

**3 · The bar.** Sound is purposeful:
- the shot reports lead the paint by 2–3 frames;
- a low horn sits under each horns insert and stays past the cut;
- a noise burst marks the fall;
- the bed drops out after shot 1 and after the last shot;
- true silence holds from 23.3 s.

Craft failures:
- the two figures interpenetrate at 15–19.4;
- the inherited mosaic pools muddle the pieces;
- there is no mirror in the action;
- amber is camouflaged;
- the sitter is jammed against the left edge;
- the head turn is illegible;
- dark silhouettes at the right edge go unexplained.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **On the puppet:** a cream hat, a dark-brown face sliver, a white shirt block, dark boots and a cyan six-shooter. The beard is indistinguishable from the shirt, the glasses read as a gold strip, the trousers read as yellow plaid, and the badge and his age are not visible.
- **In the found inserts:** he is plainly a dark-skinned man with a white beard, round glasses and a cream hat.

Round 1 repairs:
1. **The coda:** partly met. There is no reflective floor, and the pieces cover 5–10 % of the frame.
2. **The insert that changes meaning:** met, modestly.
3. **The sit:** partly met.
4. **The head turn:** not met.
5. **Keeps walking:** not met.
6. **The Mesa close-up:** not met on the puppet.

Repair conditions (ranked):
1. **Keeps walking (14.7–19.4).** At least two visible steps, no overlap of the silhouettes except at the strike, and one hit re-told by cutting.
2. **Clear the plate's mosaic pools,** or make them a reflective floor with reflections under the figures. The freed pieces cover at least 15 % of the coda frame.
3. **The head turn:** at least a third of the helmet's width, held, or a cut-in.
4. **The descent** over at least two drawn step edges.
5. **Amber** told apart from the gold at 15.5.
6. **The sit** at least 10 % of the frame width in from the left edge.
7. **The Mesa puppet:** two round gold discs, a long beard separate in tone, a yellow badge and khaki trousers.

Keep: the three-fold fall and the other clusters; the accumulating horns insert and its tone; the step-out jump cut; the six clumps; the shot reports and the dropouts; the silence over the travelling light; the puppets, the collage plate, the grade and the found inserts.
