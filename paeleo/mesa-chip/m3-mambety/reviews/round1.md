# SCION M3 · critic round 1 · REFERENCE STRONGER

Independent critic (fresh agent; 144 contact-sheet frames at 6 fps, about 45 full-resolution stills and crops at and after every score event, a scene-cut list, a spectrogram, silencedetect and transient timing; verdict from the picture, no builder notes).

**1 · The ekphrasis on screen.** Most beats read, three do not, and the coda is missing.
- **Head turn (3.0).** It barely reads: the visor bar shifts a few pixels.
- **Steps out whole (4.7).** It works: a jump cut leaves the leaded window visible and empty.
- **Sword (6.2) and gun (6.5–6.8).** Both read clearly.
- **Shoulder hit (9.5).** Strong: the shoulder goes cyan, and cyan shards lie on the floor from 10.2 to the end.
- **The fall (12.2–13.0).** Strong.
- **Gets up (14.0–14.3).** It reads.
- **Hits 2–5.** Each colour falls at its second, but amber (15) and yellow (17) are hard to tell from the knight's own gold. From 12 s on the knight stands at blade's length, so "keeps walking" reads as standing and flaking.
- **Shatter (19.5–20.4).** It reads, and all six colours lie in distinct clumps. But the floor in the action plate is orange sand, not a mirror.
- **Lowers the gun (20.9).** It reads.
- **Sits on the lowest step (22.1).** It does not read: no step is drawn, and his legs drop out of frame.
- **The light moves on across the pieces.** Absent. The last shot is the empty blue-tile floor, with no pieces.

The story survives the cutting through the fall, thins in the hits, and is lost in the coda.

**2 · Native language and the theorem.** The world is its own and built bottom-up: stiff card puppets, plates collaged from the game's stills, a torn seam along the floor line, hot and worn orange with grain and scratches, jump cuts inside gestures, and found inserts.

The first half of the hard proof is met twice:
- **The hit** is told as wide (9.5), medium (9.62) and mirrored close (9.75), then back to wide (9.92).
- **The knockdown** is told as wide (12.2), medium (12.375) and mirrored close (12.5), then back to the wide (12.67).

Neither loses the story.

The second half is not met: no insert changes what the next shot means.
- **The "10" insert** (the horned rider from behind) returns at 7.0, 14.5 and 21.25. It lands at decisions, but it is identical each time and leaves no meaning behind.
- **The colour smear (10.4)** is decoration.
- **The tile "sea"** bookends the film but is never tied to the pieces.

The re-tellings are crops and mirrorings rather than new angles: thin, but legal.

**3 · The bar.**
- **Sound agrees at the important moments.** Each shot report fires 3 frames before the paint lands, the fall's noise sits exactly on 12.2–12.9, a low tone sits under each "10" insert, and a plucked pulse ticks throughout. Disagreement on purpose is limited to those 3-frame leads.
- **The ending.** There is silence from 22.92, but it falls on an image with no pieces and no light moving.
- **Craft failures:**
  - the head turn is illegible;
  - the knight is pinned against Mesa with the sword resting on his chest (14.3–19.4);
  - amber and yellow are camouflaged in the gold;
  - his legs are cropped on the sit;
  - there is no mirror floor in the action.

**4 · Uncle Mesa.**
- **Can see:** a brown-skinned face block, a white beard (short to mid rather than plainly long), a cream wide-brimmed hat, a cream-white shirt (no guayabera detail), trousers that read brown with teal and green streaks more than khaki, dark boots and a cyan pistol.
- **Cannot see:** the gold-lensed round glasses (a single dot), the yellow badge, and his age.
- **The knight:** it reads correctly.

Repair conditions (ranked):
1. **The coda (23.1–24.0).** The final shot shows the six piece clumps on a reflective floor, with a light band travelling across them. The pieces cover at least 15 % of the frame, the light moves at least 20 % of the frame width, and it plays under the silence.
2. **An insert that changes meaning.** At least one returning insert visibly changes between its returns (for example the number counting the shots, or the image taking on the paint colours), and the shot after its last return reads differently because of it.
3. **The sit (21.9–23.1).** A step edge is drawn. His seat rests on it, knees bent, both boots on the floor inside the frame, held at least 0.4 s.
4. **The head turn (2.8–3.4).** The visor moves toward Mesa by at least a third of the helmet's width, held at least 6 frames. A cut-in to the helmet is allowed.
5. **Keeps walking (14.3–19.4).** At least two visible steps forward between the hits, with the sword not resting on his chest, and amber and yellow placed against silver pieces.
6. **Mesa, in a close shot.** Two gold lens discs and a yellow badge are distinguishable, and the trousers are khaki.

Keep: the three-way re-tellings of the hit and the fall; the step-out jump cut; the shards staying on the floor; the collage plate, the card puppets and the found inserts; the shot reports leading the paint; the fall's noise; the tone with each "10"; the ending silence starting before the cut.
