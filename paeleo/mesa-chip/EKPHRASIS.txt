Under the dune the floor is glass, and the light comes down in columns. Uncle Mesa comes down the steps. In a window of the
canyon wall a knight of stained glass turns its head. It steps out of the window, whole, and draws a red glass sword. Uncle
Mesa raises the six-shooter of paint. The knight comes on. He fires: the paint takes the knight's shoulder, and pieces fall
and lie on the floor in his colour. The sword comes down and he goes down. He gets up. He fires again, and again, and again;
the knight comes apart, piece by piece, and keeps walking. At the last shot it goes to pieces on the mirror floor, every shard
still holding its colour. Uncle Mesa lowers the gun and sits on the lowest step. The light moves on across the pieces.
