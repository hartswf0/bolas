# How ALLWAYS works

ALLWAYS makes editorial decisions through films and playable encounters. Its public statement supplies the question; a SCION prompt supplies a particular method; the produced proof supplies something another person can judge.

## A bounded production cycle

1. **Choose a question.** State what the viewer should be able to notice. For example: a repeated task becomes difficult, a visual match reveals different circumstances, or a navigable route makes missing evidence apparent.
2. **Define a source set.** Begin with a small identified collection. For an archival proof, select recordings whose public-domain status or permitted use has been checked for the intended use. Keep contributed, newly staged, and generated material identifiable. A large corpus is an expansion decision.
3. **Choose an operator.** Use one primary SCION and at most two supporting methods. Declare which rule governs timing, framing, source context, or interaction when they conflict.
4. **Make the proof.** Produce a short sequence with purposeful sound, or a complete interaction with entry, consequence, exit, and return. Keep original source material recoverable.
5. **Invite an independent reading.** The critic watches or operates the result before reading the builder's explanation. They identify a specific event, the largest gap, a repair that could be observed, and a strength to protect.
6. **Repair and expand.** Compare plausible causes on the same material. Expand after the defining relation becomes perceptible, then inspect the whole work again.

## The record carried by an edit

Each selected passage retains its source identifier, source interval, available credit and date/place information, known use conditions, transformations, and its position in the new work. Unknowns remain explicit. An editorial relation has its own record: why these passages were proposed as neighbors, what was changed, and what the comparison showed.

This is a working account that supports revisiting a decision. A complete form or valid source pointer does not authenticate a historical claim. Source interpretation and semantic judgments need their own attributed review.

## A match has several dimensions

We distinguish graphic position, shape, direction, motion phase, rhythm, sound, action, language, and historical context. The operator states which relation it is attempting. A motion score can help retrieve candidates; the edit still needs to make the relation readable at normal speed.

Physical measurements require an appropriate basis. Pixel radius does not establish centimeters. Screen motion does not establish the physical speed of a machine or a storm. If footage is retimed, cropped, stabilized, mirrored, or otherwise transformed, record the operation and preserve an unaltered comparison. Do not turn a selected similarity threshold into a claim that two events are identical.

A useful first match experiment compares at least two edits of the same sources: one that maximizes alignment, and one that allows contextual difference to interrupt it. The critic asks what each makes available and what it conceals.

## NOBODY HOME: removal is part of the work

The premise is computational erasure from home movies. A source recording and a derived empty-looking room are different artifacts. Preserve the original and identify the altered passage. A person mask locates removal; it does not by itself supply the room behind the person. Setting opacity to zero creates transparency. It does not recover occluded footage.

Separate background pixels actually visible elsewhere in the source from reconstructed or generated pixels. When the recording never reveals a surface, its appearance remains unknown; any filled version is an authored reconstruction. Retained shadows, displaced objects, moving chairs, and voices need a deliberate treatment because they may carry the absent person's consequences.

Begin with one short passage and a source/derived comparison before scaling to a collection. The required removal and compositing adapter remains to be built and reviewed. Naturally empty footage before an entry or after an exit can be a separate study, but does not satisfy the removal premise by itself.

## Other operations that require explicit state

- **ZERO MEMORY:** declare which working history resets and when. Source access, model training, session state, and the external review log are separate. A reset is a designed rule, not a claim that a model has forgotten its training.
- **ANYBODY, ANYWHERE:** parallel playback is an editorial relation. Only adequate time evidence can support a claim of simultaneous recording.
- **SPLIT SECOND:** align visible action thresholds. A recorded movement does not reveal an identical private decision.
- **LATENT:** spatial coordinates belong to the chosen model or arrangement. They are revisable ways of navigating records.
- **THE EYE-TRACE ARCHIVE:** screen-space anchors and estimated paths of attention must remain distinct from actual measured gaze.
- **OPERATIVE CINEMA / bridge study:** source footage, interpolation, and generated continuations retain separate identities. Continuity on screen does not establish continuity in the historical record.

## The SCION extension

The existing 54 animation prompts remain at [the original operator](../scion/operator.html). ALLWAYS adds [59 cinema, documentary, essay, and interactive operators](operator.html), IDs 055–113. Each new entry has its own theorem, native working objects, bounded proof, acceptance criteria, repair experiments, and source record. The new prompts adapt the shared eight-section structure; they have their own explicit doctrine and do not claim to repeat the original fifteen points verbatim.

Reference films belong to the method library. They are not ALLWAYS productions. Source research for the extension was textual. A source description, a reviewed prompt, and an accepted prototype are separate achievements. The proposed SCION proofs remain unexecuted until an actual run is recorded.

## Publication and status

A work record can move through concept, proof in progress, proof reviewed, work in development, and released. Store the actual critic finding separately from the status. Publishing a page or a prompt does not release a film. Each finished work should make its principal source and transformation account accessible without requiring technical overlays across the viewing experience.
