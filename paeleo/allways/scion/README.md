## Use

Choose one primary operator. Supply an original work, desired experience, format, available material and tools, permitted scope, and protected conditions. Make the short proof before expanding. A critic must inspect the produced film or interaction; reading its specification is not acceptance of the work.

Each prompt is complete on its own. Reference artists did not author or endorse these operational interpretations. The new entries adapt the original eight-part SCION structure, with explicit source and transformation records suited to their different materials.

Related works are identified separately rather than silently substituted. Each entry retains the original supplied label.
