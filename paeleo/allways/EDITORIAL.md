# Editorial decisions

The ALLWAYS statement was developed from several deliberately incompatible drafts: a demand for autonomous archival reconstruction, a polemic against official subjects, a refuge from the pressure of personal significance, a literal operating manual, and philosophical fragments questioning total preservation.

The final statement keeps their common question: **what else was happening?** It gives the disagreement a place in the work instead of resolving it through a claim that one gaze is innocent.

| Earlier proposition | Decision in the public statement |
| --- | --- |
| Personal authorship is over; surrender projects and funding to a single machine. | Authorship becomes a question of selection, classification, editing, transformation, and accountability. The practice makes a concrete artistic offer instead of demanding institutional surrender. |
| Official subjects are false; the background is truthful. | Change the allocation of attention while questioning the conditions and framing of both. Peripheral material can also be staged, misunderstood, or assigned a new story. |
| Ordinary life needs to be freed from the demand to be interesting. | Keep the permission to attend to a moment without making it justify a whole life. Do not promise a therapeutic effect for every viewer. |
| A person's name does not matter if their gesture moves us. | Attention can precede identification. Missing knowledge remains a reason to inquire. |
| Match cuts discover an invariant that human editors could not see. | Search proposes relations using chosen corpora, models, and measurements. Both people and systems can encounter unexpected resemblance. The actual edit must earn its relation. |
| Erasing people reveals the true room. | Removal creates a derived image. The source and reconstructed parts remain distinguishable; the absent people stay part of the question. |
| An unblinking eye can remember the whole record. | Keep the phrase as an early provocation. The practice works with bounded collections, situated attention, and documented gaps. |
| Technical commands replace poetic language. | A public statement, an editorial judgment, and an executable procedure do different work. The method translates a question into something inspectable without pretending to exhaust it. |

The unsupported billion-hours-per-day assertion and total-archive promises were removed. Invented scenes are not presented as recovered historical examples. The duplicated philosophical section in the supplied working draft was treated as one set of fragments.

The program title **B-ROLL OF CIVILIZATION** names an editorial ambition. It does not assert that this collection represents all people, all periods, or everything a recording omitted.

**NEARMATCH** carries the productive tension: resemblance gives us a reason to look; difference gives us a reason to keep looking.
