# ALLWAYS naming and proposed taxonomy

**ALLWAYS is a cinema practice within PAELEO.** It is not a replacement name for PAELEO or a claim to own the reference films used by SCION. This taxonomy organizes development concepts, proposed series, studies, methods, and retained names. It does not announce completed films or operating installations.

## The stable architecture

| Name | Role | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **ALLWAYS / Allways** | Cinema practice within PAELEO | The meeting of *always*, duration and recurrence, with *all ways*, multiple routes through material. It does not promise an exhaustive archive or uninterrupted operation. |
| **B-ROLL OF CIVILIZATION** | Central archive-based series | Background lives, routine work, incidental gestures, and the material around the intended scene. |
| **NEARMATCH / Nearmatch** | Editorial principle | Resemblance invites attention; difference prevents distinct lives from becoming interchangeable. |
| **SCION** | Method library | Production operators with bounded proofs and criticism. Reference titles remain lineages, not the ALLWAYS portfolio. |
| **OPERATIVE CINEMA** | Broad practice term | Selection, transformation, comparison, and presentation are operations with consequences. This broad use is distinct from the specific archival-bridging study below. |

One work has one canonical record. Series memberships, collections, and methods provide different routes to it; they do not require a hierarchy of competing brands. The catalogue is [WORKS.md](WORKS.md), with structured records in [works.json](works.json).

## Collections, formats, and editorial identities

| Supplied name | Proposed disposition |
|---|---|
| **EQUIVALENTS / Equivalents** | A collection of correspondences in form, action, and affect. The naming proposal invokes Alfred Stieglitz’s *Equivalents* as a historical homage; that homage does not make visual resemblance proof of equivalent feelings or lives. |
| **UNWITNESSED** | A collection about background attention, recorded absence, and unattended systems. It is not a verifiable assertion that no person has previously watched a source. |
| **MEANWHILE / Meanwhile** | A collection or editorial relation: another place or process continues beyond the current frame. Actual simultaneity requires evidence. |
| **COMMONTHREAD / Commonthread** | A proposed series preserving the original epic ambition: the ordinary tissue between plots becomes the sustained subject. Care, maintenance, waiting, and shared work can connect films without erasing their particular circumstances. It may also support a collection view. |
| **EVERDAY / Everday** | A proposed recurring or looping day format. Preserve the intentional spelling. It does not imply that a daily publishing schedule or automation has been established. |
| **DOUBLETAKE / Doubletake** | A comparative edition or study format: return, replay, or an alternate cut that changes the first encounter. |
| **BETWEEN TAKES** | The working notebook under ALLWAYS. Screening material, editions, and garments remain possible future extensions. |

## Research and method labels

| Supplied name | Proposed disposition |
|---|---|
| **LATENT** | A proposed navigable archive environment. Its coordinates express a selected representation, not memory’s natural or exact location. |
| **THE VECTOR POET** | An associative-editing study. Search proposes neighbors; an editor tests their relation, development, and limits. A score is not an interpretation. |
| **THE EYE-TRACE ARCHIVE** | A working title for composition and attention research. Screen anchors, motion, predicted salience, and measured gaze are different records. Frame centers cannot recover a camera operator’s actual gaze. |
| **OPERATIVE CINEMA — Archival Bridges** | The specific study preserving the original proposal: use synthetic passages to connect documentary fragments. Source endpoints and generated intervals remain distinct and inspectable. Seamlessness is a variable to test, not evidence that an event occurred. |
| **The Unwitnessed Match-Cut** | A named method: automated search nominates a relation, and an authored cut makes it perceptible. Preserve source context, alignment, and transformations. The poetic title cannot establish that nobody noticed the relation earlier. |
| **ANYRECORD** | An intake/catalogue study and archival ethic, retaining the original nod to Everynone. Different media deserve consideration; this does not make all records equally informative, representative, or interchangeable. |
| **WHOLEPART / Wholepart** | Theoretical vocabulary for fragments, composites, collections, and their relationships. One gesture is not a sufficient sample of a species. |

The early account of an agent discovering an invariant that had waited untouched for fifty years remains a metaphor in the development history. A correspondence depends on the sources, model, metric, crop, timing, and the editor’s judgment. Human and computational discovery can both be surprising and both remain situated.

## Retained alternatives and retired claims

| Supplied name or formulation | Canonical destination or disposition |
|---|---|
| **LATENTIC** | Retained alternative to LATENT. Do not activate a second environment without a distinct purpose. “Cinema without an author” is a provocation, not a factual description of the source makers, system design, and editing choices. |
| **UNWITNESS** | Retained alternative to UNWITNESSED. The verb could become a method title if a future work supplies a distinct operation. |
| **TOTAL RECALL** | Retained alternative to ZERO MEMORY; no complete global corpus or total recall capability is asserted. |
| **TOTAL RECALL / ZERO MEMORY** | Original compound proposal preserved in naming history. ZERO MEMORY is the canonical development title. |
| **THE PARTWHOLE / The Partwhole** | Retained alternative phrasing for WHOLEPART, not another project. |
| **NONEOTHER / Noneother** | Reserved philosophical name. Retire the claim that everyone is identical in physical and emotional anatomy. The useful aspiration is attention across difference. |

## Original lines and manifesto drafts

| Original wording | Disposition |
|---|---|
| **“B-ROLL OF CIVILIZATION.”** | Canonical series title; omit terminal punctuation in navigation. |
| **“Everything that happens to no one in particular.”** | Optional public line. Its poetic anonymity should coexist with particular source credits and circumstances. |
| **“EDITED BY AN EYE THAT NEVER BLINKS.”** | Campaign line or the voice of a particular unattended installation. It does not establish total vigilance or continuous operation. |
| **“History was recorded by millions of hands. It can only be remembered by an eye that never blinks.”** | Preserve as an early manifesto draft. Retire the exclusive second sentence, which discounts human remembering and gives the machine an unsupported monopoly. Proposed replacement: **“Recorded by many hands. Revisited through many ways of seeing.”** |

## Naming and evidence rules

- Case and punctuation variants are searchable aliases, not separate entities.
- “Uncataloged,” “unknown to this project,” “unintentionally recorded,” and “never viewed” are different claims.
- A synthetic bridge or erased figure is an authored transformation, not recovered historical evidence.
- A source title, a prompt specification, and an accepted prototype describe different achievements. Keep their records distinct.
- A retired title remains in naming history; it does not need an empty public project page.
