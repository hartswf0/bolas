The hen & the egg

PAE-04 · SCION 17 · South Park: repeatable graphic grammar · after the PAE-03 flour tower · style after a diner menu's screen-print spot art

Two inks, held drawings and a fixed score: the hen cannot stop the spatula, so she holds the heap, catches the egg, and becomes the menu picture.

Made of

Two inks (teal, tomato) and one grey on cream, drawn as flat closed shapes with a fixed, seeded scissor wobble and the cream line work printed a little off register. The hen is a kit of about fifteen cut pieces (body, scalloped tail, spiral wing, comb, beak, wattle, eye, leg) in eleven whole drawings; the cook is one figure with a hat, bandana, singing mouth, spatula and fork; the heap is 180 seeded straw strands.

Moved by

Substitution of whole drawings, held on twos: a flinch, a watch, a stand-tall, two walk drawings, a prop, a brace, a leap, a catch, a land, two gather drawings, a set-down and the menu pose. Only translation is interpolated, and every step is a hop whose foot is planted where it lands. Straw strands are particles: cut strands fly on ballistic arcs to the counter, and the gather carries the same strands, one by one, into the nest.

Measured from the render

choicescoremeasured

Onsets are read back from the per-frame trace of the drawn geometry (hen x, base lift, height, body area, egg position, strand counts), not printed from the score. No independent critic has watched this yet: the builder cannot approve its own work.
